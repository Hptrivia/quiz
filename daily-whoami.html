How to Play

Guess today's famous person in 5 tries. Type a name and pick anyone from the list.

Each guess shows how close you are:

  • 🟩 Match
  • 🟨 Close — same continent, same field but a different job (e.g. footballer vs tennis player), or born within 5 years
  • ⬛ No match
  • ⬆️⬇️ Born — the answer was born later ⬆️ or earlier ⬇️

Example: the answer is Rihanna and you guess Beyoncé → 🟩 Female · 🟨 USA (same continent) · 🟩 Singer / musician · ⬆️ born 1981.

A new person every day at midnight UTC.
