Custom Trivia Requests

Everything on Trivia Gauntlet is free to play. Want something made specifically for you — more questions for a theme that's already here, a brand-new show, Episode Mode for something specific, or trivia for something else entirely? That's available as a paid custom request.

Some things people ask for:

  • A new TV show, movie, game, or topic that isn't on the site yet
  • More questions added to a theme that's already here
  • More rounds for Versus mode so you and a friend don't run out of questions
  • Episode Mode built out for a specific show
  • A full pub quiz night — rounds, categories, whatever format you run
  • Trivia for something unrelated to what we cover — a work event, a class, exam review, a wedding, a trivia night, whatever you've got in mind

Pricing depends entirely on the size and scope of the request, so there's no fixed price list — tell us what you want and we'll quote it.
