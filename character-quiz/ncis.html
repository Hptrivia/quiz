How the NCIS test works

Every question puts you in a situation from the world of NCIS. Pick the answer that's most like you, not the one you think sounds best. Each answer adds hidden points to the characters who would make the same choice. We ran all 4,194,304 possible answer combinations through the scoring: every character can be reached, and no result takes more than 19% of them.

Your result shows your main character, the character you're secondarily like, the one you'd clash with, and a full percentage breakdown across the cast, so two people who both get the same character can still have very different mixes.

FAQ

What is the NCIS test?

It's a free personality quiz that matches you to a NCIS character. You answer 11 questions set inside the world of the show, and every answer quietly scores points for different characters. You get your main character, your runner-up, who you'd clash with, and a percentage breakdown of all 7.

Which NCIS characters can I get?

There are 7 possible results: Leroy Jethro Gibbs, Tony DiNozzo, Ziva David, Timothy McGee, Abby Sciuto, Ducky Mallard or Jimmy Palmer.

How long does the NCIS test take?

About two minutes — 11 multiple-choice questions, one tap each.

How is the result calculated?

Each answer adds hidden points to the characters it fits. The character with the most points is your result, and your breakdown shows how your points split across the whole cast. The same answers always give the same result. Before publishing, every possible combination of answers was run through the scoring to make sure every character can be reached and no single character dominates.

Which NCIS character is the rarest result?

Ducky Mallard. Only about 13% of all possible answer combinations lead to Ducky Mallard, compared with about 18% for the most common result, Tony DiNozzo.

Is the NCIS test free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email. When you're done you can test your actual NCIS knowledge with the trivia quiz.
