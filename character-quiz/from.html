How the From test works

Every question puts you in a situation from the world of From. Pick the answer that's most like you, not the one you think sounds best. Each answer adds hidden points to the characters who would make the same choice. We ran all 16,777,216 possible answer combinations through the scoring: every character can be reached, and no result takes more than 15% of them.

Your result shows your main character, the character you're secondarily like, the one you'd clash with, and a full percentage breakdown across the cast, so two people who both get the same character can still have very different mixes.

FAQ

What is the From test?

It's a free personality quiz that matches you to a From character. You answer 12 questions set inside the world of the show, and every answer quietly scores points for different characters. You get your main character, your runner-up, who you'd clash with, and a percentage breakdown of all 8.

Which From characters can I get?

There are 8 possible results: Boyd Stevens, Jim Matthews, Tabitha Matthews, Donna Raines, Jade Herrera, Kenny Liu, Sara Myers or Victor.

How long does the From test take?

About two minutes — 12 multiple-choice questions, one tap each.

How is the result calculated?

Each answer adds hidden points to the characters it fits. The character with the most points is your result, and your breakdown shows how your points split across the whole cast. The same answers always give the same result. Before publishing, every possible combination of answers was run through the scoring to make sure every character can be reached and no single character dominates.

Which From character is the rarest result?

Jim Matthews. Only about 9% of all possible answer combinations lead to Jim Matthews, compared with about 15% for the most common result, Kenny Liu.

Is the From test free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email. When you're done you can test your actual From knowledge with the trivia quiz.
