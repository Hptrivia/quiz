How the New Girl test works

Every question puts you in a situation from the world of New Girl. Pick the answer that's most like you, not the one you think sounds best. Each answer adds hidden points to the characters who would make the same choice. We ran all 1,048,576 possible answer combinations through the scoring: every character can be reached, and no result takes more than 27% of them.

Your result shows your main character, the character you're secondarily like, the one you'd clash with, and a full percentage breakdown across the cast, so two people who both get the same character can still have very different mixes.

FAQ

What is the New Girl test?

It's a free personality quiz that matches you to a New Girl character. You answer 10 questions set inside the world of the show, and every answer quietly scores points for different characters. You get your main character, your runner-up, who you'd clash with, and a percentage breakdown of all 5.

Which New Girl characters can I get?

There are 5 possible results: Jess Day, Nick Miller, Schmidt, Winston Bishop or Cece.

How long does the New Girl test take?

About two minutes — 10 multiple-choice questions, one tap each.

How is the result calculated?

Each answer adds hidden points to the characters it fits. The character with the most points is your result, and your breakdown shows how your points split across the whole cast. The same answers always give the same result. Before publishing, every possible combination of answers was run through the scoring to make sure every character can be reached and no single character dominates.

Which New Girl character is the rarest result?

Winston Bishop. Only about 16% of all possible answer combinations lead to Winston Bishop, compared with about 27% for the most common result, Nick Miller.

Is the New Girl test free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email. When you're done you can test your actual New Girl knowledge with the trivia quiz.
