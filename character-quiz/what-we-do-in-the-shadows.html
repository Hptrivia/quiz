How the What We Do in the Shadows test works

Every question puts you in a situation from the world of What We Do in the Shadows. Pick the answer that's most like you, not the one you think sounds best. Each answer adds hidden points to the characters who would make the same choice. We ran all 1,048,576 possible answer combinations through the scoring: every character can be reached, and no result takes more than 19% of them.

Your result shows your main character, the character you're secondarily like, the one you'd clash with, and a full percentage breakdown across the cast, so two people who both get the same character can still have very different mixes.

FAQ

What is the What We Do in the Shadows test?

It's a free personality quiz that matches you to a What We Do in the Shadows character. You answer 10 questions set inside the world of the show, and every answer quietly scores points for different characters. You get your main character, your runner-up, who you'd clash with, and a percentage breakdown of all 6.

Which What We Do in the Shadows characters can I get?

There are 6 possible results: Nandor the Relentless, Laszlo Cravensworth, Nadja, Colin Robinson, Guillermo or The Guide.

How long does the What We Do in the Shadows test take?

About two minutes — 10 multiple-choice questions, one tap each.

How is the result calculated?

Each answer adds hidden points to the characters it fits. The character with the most points is your result, and your breakdown shows how your points split across the whole cast. The same answers always give the same result. Before publishing, every possible combination of answers was run through the scoring to make sure every character can be reached and no single character dominates.

Which What We Do in the Shadows character is the rarest result?

Guillermo. Only about 13% of all possible answer combinations lead to Guillermo, compared with about 19% for the most common result, Laszlo Cravensworth.

Is the What We Do in the Shadows test free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email. When you're done you can test your actual What We Do in the Shadows knowledge with the trivia quiz.
