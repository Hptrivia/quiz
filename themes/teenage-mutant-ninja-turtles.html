Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles trivia works because the 2012 series built a huge world of mutants, aliens and villains over five seasons, giving fans plenty of details to remember. Questions can cover the brothers, their allies and enemies, and the trips to space and other dimensions.

A strong TMNT quiz can include Leonardo, Raphael, Donatello and Michelangelo, Master Splinter, April O'Neil and Casey Jones, Shredder and the Foot Clan, Karai, Tiger Claw, Dogpound and Rahzar, Chris Bradford, the Kraang and TCRI, the Shellraiser, Mutation Day, the Fugitoid, Ivan Steranko and Kavaxas. It works because fans remember every mutation and every villain.
