Love Island UK trivia works because more than a decade of summer and winter series has turned islanders, catchphrases and villa rituals into pop-culture shorthand. Questions can cover the winners, the jobs islanders left behind and the challenges that went viral.

A strong Love Island UK quiz can include the Mallorca villa and the winter series in South Africa, Casa Amor and stick or twist, the Hideaway, the firepit and the Beach Hut, the personalised water bottles, Movie Night and the heart rate challenge, Iain Stirling's narration, Caroline Flack, Laura Whitmore and Maya Jama as hosts, Aftersun and Unseen Bits, winners like Dani and Jack, Amber and Greg and Ekin-Su and Davide, Molly-Mae and Tommy Fury, and Love Island: All Stars. It works because fans can quote every series and remember every recoupling.
