  • What is the name of the radical feminist house on campus in season 3? Lilith House
  • Which college does Veronica dream that Lilly would have attended? Vassar

Veronica Mars trivia works because every season is built around a central mystery, with many smaller cases along the way. Questions can cover the Lilly Kane murder, the bus crash, Hearst College and the Hulu revival.

A strong Veronica Mars quiz can include Veronica and her dog Backup, Keith Mars and Mars Investigations, Lilly Kane and Duncan Kane, Logan and Aaron Echolls, Wallace Fennel, Weevil and the PCHers, Mac, Dick and Beaver Casablancas, Neptune High and the Pirates, the 09ers, Abel Koontz, the Neptune Grand, Hearst College and the Castle, Lilith House and season four's spring break bombings. It works because fans who solved the mysteries alongside Veronica remember the clues.
