The Last Kingdom trivia works because it blends real Anglo-Saxon history with a sprawling cast of Saxon and Dane characters, giving fans plenty of battles, alliances and betrayals to be quizzed on.

A strong Last Kingdom quiz can include Uhtred of Bebbanburg, King Alfred the Great, Brida, Ragnar, Aethelflaed and Father Beocca, Uhtred's sword Serpent-Breath, his lost fortress of Bebbanburg, and the ongoing conflict between the Saxons and the Danes. The theme stays engaging because it spans the show's source novels through to the concluding film, Seven Kings Must Die.
