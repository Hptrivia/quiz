Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What is the name of the classic Doom map hidden as a secret in The Necropolis? House of Pain
  • Which planet's moons are the setting of the original Doom? Mars
  • What is the name of the towering Hell city where souls are refined into Argent energy? Nekravol
  • What are the small collectible statues hidden throughout Doom (2016)'s levels called? Doomguy figurines
  • Which boss is fought at the very end of the original Doom? Spider Mastermind
  • What is the name of Doom 3's 2005 expansion pack? Resurrection of Evil
  • What are the two weapon mod options available for the Combat Shotgun in Hell on Earth? Sticky Bombs and Full Auto
  • In Doom 3, what item could not be held at the same time as a weapon? Flashlight
  • In which facility does Olivia Pierce carry out her research in Doom (2016)? Lazarus Labs
  • What is the name of the object in Doom (2016) that grants access to the Rune Trials? Helix Stone

Doom trivia works because the series turned demon-slaying into pure speed and spectacle across three decades of shooters. Fans can be tested on the weapons, the demons, the lore of the Slayer and the levels that made each game famous.

A strong Doom quiz can include the classic UAC games on Mars, Doom 3's Delta Labs disaster, the 2016 reboot and Olivia Pierce's cult, Doom Eternal's Sentinel lore and Maykr hierarchy, Doom: The Dark Ages and its Sentinel war, the arsenal from the shotgun and chainsaw to the BFG and Crucible, glory kills and rune upgrades, the demon bestiary from imps to the Icon of Sin, and the id Software history behind it all. It works because it rewards fans who know both the lore and the level design.
