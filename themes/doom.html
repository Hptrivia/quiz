Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What are Doom Eternal's hidden, extremely difficult optional arenas called? Slayer Gates
  • Which competitive multiplayer mode did Doom help popularize? Deathmatch
  • How many Hell Priests are invading Earth at the start of Doom Eternal? 3
  • Which angelic ruler serves as a main antagonist in Doom Eternal? Khan Maykr
  • What is the name of the combat training arena inside Doom Eternal's Fortress of Doom? The Ripatorium
  • What is the name of the heaviest, slowest-recharging melee weapon in Doom: The Dark Ages? The Dreadmace
  • What is the name of the vast Martian portal to Hell that the Slayer must seal with the Crucible? The Well
  • What is the armoured Arachnotron variant in Doom: The Dark Ages called? The Ballistatron
  • What is the name of the elite armoured hunter demon introduced in Doom: The Dark Ages? The Agaddon Hunter
  • How many eyes must be destroyed to drop Kalibas's shield in Nekravol? 4

Doom trivia works because the series turned demon-slaying into pure speed and spectacle across three decades of shooters. Fans can be tested on the weapons, the demons, the lore of the Slayer and the levels that made each game famous.

A strong Doom quiz can include the classic UAC games on Mars, Doom 3's Delta Labs disaster, the 2016 reboot and Olivia Pierce's cult, Doom Eternal's Sentinel lore and Maykr hierarchy, Doom: The Dark Ages and its Sentinel war, the arsenal from the shotgun and chainsaw to the BFG and Crucible, glory kills and rune upgrades, the demon bestiary from imps to the Icon of Sin, and the id Software history behind it all. It works because it rewards fans who know both the lore and the level design.
