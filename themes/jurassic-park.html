Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which actor plays Dr. Ian Malcolm across several Jurassic films? Jeff Goldblum
  • According to Hammond, what city is Dennis Nedry from? Cambridge, Massachusetts
  • Which actor plays InGen security chief Vic Hoskins in Jurassic World? Vincent D'Onofrio
  • The lawyer Donald Gennaro represents which law firm during the park inspection in Jurassic Park? Cowan, Swain & Ross
  • In Jurassic World, corporate sponsors want naming rights to new dinosaurs; which mobile carrier is jokingly attached to the Indominus rex? Verizon Wireless
  • What do the words on the banner that falls in the visitor center at the end of Jurassic Park read? "When Dinosaurs Ruled the Earth"
  • What is the name of the Jurassic World paddock attraction that houses the park's veteran T. rex? T. Rex Kingdom
  • Which biotech corporation is the villain of Jurassic World Dominion? Biosyn
  • What is the name of the Jurassic World attraction where children ride baby dinosaurs? The Gentle Giants Petting Zoo
  • In which film is the Indominus rex first introduced? Jurassic World

Jurassic Park trivia works because the film's premise, cast, and dinosaur set pieces are instantly recognizable, giving fans a mix of science, character moments, and blockbuster spectacle to be tested on.

A strong Jurassic Park quiz can include Alan Grant, Ellie Sattler, Ian Malcolm, John Hammond, InGen, Isla Nublar, the Tyrannosaurus rex, Velociraptors, the electrified fences, and the Michael Crichton novel the film is based on. The theme works because it can move between behind-the-scenes filmmaking trivia and details from the story itself.
