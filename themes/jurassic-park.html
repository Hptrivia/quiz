Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In Dominion, Biosyn secretly engineers giant versions of which crop-destroying insect? Locusts
  • Which tiny predator in Dominion is named after the Greek personification of doom? Moros
  • In Jurassic World, corporate sponsors want naming rights to new dinosaurs; which mobile carrier is jokingly attached to the Indominus rex? Verizon Wireless
  • Which clawed, plant-eating dinosaur helps Rexy defeat the Giganotosaurus in the finale? A Therizinosaurus
  • What is Franklin Webb's job on the Isla Nublar rescue team? Systems analyst
  • What is the name of the genetic safeguard that leaves the park's dinosaurs dependent on supplements? The lysine contingency
  • Owen disrupts the auction using which dome-headed, ramming dinosaur? A Stygimoloch
  • What is Dr. Alan Grant's profession? Paleontologist
  • Which technician monitors the park control room alongside Lowery Cruthers? Vivian
  • In which US state is Grant and Sattler's dig site at the start of the film? Montana

Jurassic Park trivia works because the film's premise, cast, and dinosaur set pieces are instantly recognizable, giving fans a mix of science, character moments, and blockbuster spectacle to be tested on.

A strong Jurassic Park quiz can include Alan Grant, Ellie Sattler, Ian Malcolm, John Hammond, InGen, Isla Nublar, the Tyrannosaurus rex, Velociraptors, the electrified fences, and the Michael Crichton novel the film is based on. The theme works because it can move between behind-the-scenes filmmaking trivia and details from the story itself.
