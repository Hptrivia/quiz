American Gods trivia works because the series mixes real mythology with a road-trip story, giving every episode a new god, a new town, and a new detail to remember. Fans can be tested on the characters, the deities, and the roadside stops along the way.

A strong American Gods quiz can include Shadow Moon, Mr. Wednesday, Laura Moon, Mad Sweeney, Bilquis, Czernobog, Mr. Nancy, Mama-ji, Sam Black Crow, Easter, Technical Boy, Media, Mr. World, Jack's Crocodile Bar, and Shadow's nine-day vigil. It works because it turns myth, religion, and modern America into a story with a huge cast of memorable figures.
