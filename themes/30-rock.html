30 Rock trivia works because the series packs an enormous number of jokes, running bits, and guest appearances into every episode, giving fans a dense catalog of specific details to be tested on.

A strong 30 Rock quiz can include Liz Lemon, Jack Donaghy, Tracy Jordan, Jenna Maroney, Kenneth Parcell, the fictional show TGS with Tracy Jordan, NBC and GE references, and the show's rapid-fire callback humor. The theme works because it rewards fans who caught the background jokes as much as those who remember the main storylines.
