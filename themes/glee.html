Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In the finale's flash-forward, Sue Sylvester rises to which political office? Vice President of the United States
  • Which Journey song became the signature anthem of New Directions? Don't Stop Believin'
  • Which musical is McKinley staging in 'The First Time,'? West Side Story
  • What award did Mary Halloran win when her Anne Frank adaptation transferred to New York? Obie
  • Rachel blanks and flubs which signature song at her NYADA audition? Don't Rain on My Parade
  • Which star plays the imposing NYADA dean, Carmen Tibideaux? Whoopi Goldberg
  • What grade does Puck receive on his geography test in the Season 3 finale, allowing him to graduate? C-
  • Which city is Mike Chang's Joffrey Ballet scholarship based in? Chicago
  • What is Sue Sylvester's recurring nickname for Kurt Hummel? Porcelain
  • What bar do Blaine and Karofsky meet at in the Season 6 premiere? Scandals

Glee trivia works because the musical dramedy mixes big covers, high-school rivalries and the New Directions' road to Nationals. Fans can be tested on the characters, the songs and the storylines across the seasons.

A strong Glee quiz can include Rachel Berry and Finn Hudson, Kurt, Blaine and the Warblers, Santana, Brittany, Quinn and Puck, Mr. Schuester and the ever-scheming Sue Sylvester, the New Directions and rival clubs, the show choir competitions from Sectionals to Nationals, the guest stars and tribute episodes, and the standout musical numbers that defined the show. It works because it rewards fans who knew every solo and slushie.
