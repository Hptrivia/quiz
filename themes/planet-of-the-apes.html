  • In the 2024 film, what animals are the feral humans seen with when Sylva's raiders attack? Zebras

Planet of the Apes trivia works because the films tell one continuous story of Caesar and the apes across generations, so details from one movie matter again in the next. Fans can be tested on characters, events, and how the world changes.

A strong Planet of the Apes quiz can include Caesar, Will Rodman, Koba, Maurice, Rocket, Cornelius, Nova, Noa, Proximus, the Simian Flu, the San Bruno Primate Sanctuary, and the Colonel. It works because a long, connected story gives fans specific characters, places, and turning points to remember.
