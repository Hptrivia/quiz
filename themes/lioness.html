Lioness trivia works because the series is full of high-stakes missions, undercover identities, and tense turns that make small details worth remembering. Fans can be tested on the operatives, the targets, and the places the story takes them.

A strong Lioness quiz can include Joe, Cruz, Kaitlyn, Josephina Carrillo, Pablo, National Security Advisor Monty Hollar, the former Navy SEAL Tex, SERE training, Walter Reed, Jordan, and Costa Rica. It works because the story combines training, undercover work, and geopolitical stakes into a tense, detailed thriller.
