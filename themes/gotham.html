Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Who poses as the ghost of Oswald's father to unsettle him? Basil
  • What is the nickname of Tom, the kidnapper who abducts Bruce and Silver? The Knife
  • What role does Lee first take at the Narrows fight ring? Medic
  • What nationality is Nikolai, the mob boss Fish Mooney plots with against Falcone? Russian
  • Which organization does Barbara take over in the fourth season? The League of Shadows
  • What does Jerome call the band of villains he assembles? Legion of Horribles
  • Which secret society pulls the strings behind Hugo Strange's experiments? The Court of Owls
  • How does Oswald imprison Nygma at the end of the third season? Frozen in ice
  • What is Professor Pyg's real name? Lazlo Valentin
  • What job does Kristen Kringle hold at the GCPD? Archivist

Gotham trivia works because the series gives every famous Batman villain an origin story with its own twists. Questions can cover Gordon's cases at the GCPD, Oswald's climb to King of Gotham and the strange experiments at Indian Hill.

A strong Gotham quiz can include Jim Gordon and Harvey Bullock, Bruce Wayne and Alfred, Oswald Cobblepot the Penguin, Edward Nygma and Kristen Kringle, Fish Mooney, Selina Kyle, Barbara Kean and the Sirens nightclub, Carmine Falcone and Sal Maroni, Theo Galavan and the Maniax, the Order of St. Dumas, Hugo Strange and Indian Hill, the Court of Owls, Bruce's double 514A and the ten-years-later finale. It works because fans can trace each villain from their first appearance.
