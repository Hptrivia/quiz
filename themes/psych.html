Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In which season's finale does the Yin and Yang saga finally conclude? Season 5
  • For what festival do Shawn and Gus travel to a remote mountain town in season 5? Cinnamon Festival
  • How old did Shawn's father say he was when he started to have psychic visions? 18
  • On which network did Psych originally air? USA Network
  • From which relative did Shawn inherit his eidetic memory? His mother
  • What is the profession of Declan Rand, the wealthy rival who briefly dates Juliet? Criminal profiler
  • What year school reunion did Shawn and Gus attend? 13th
  • What's the name of the town Shawn and Gus travel to in season five after an email invite? Dual Spires
  • In the alias "Gus T.T. Showbiz," what does the extra T stand for? Talent
  • What is the title of the first Psych movie, released in 2017? Psych: The Movie

Psych trivia works because the show pairs case-of-the-week mysteries with constant pop-culture riffing and running gags, giving fans both plot details and a deep well of in-jokes to be tested on.

A strong Psych quiz can include Shawn Spencer, Burton 'Gus' Guster, Carlton Lassiter, Juliet O'Hara, Henry Spencer, the Santa Barbara Police Department, the Blueberry car, and the pineapple hidden in nearly every episode. The theme stays engaging because it rewards fans who track both the mysteries and the show's constant running jokes.
