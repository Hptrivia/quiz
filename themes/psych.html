Psych trivia works because the show pairs case-of-the-week mysteries with constant pop-culture riffing and running gags, giving fans both plot details and a deep well of in-jokes to be tested on.

A strong Psych quiz can include Shawn Spencer, Burton 'Gus' Guster, Carlton Lassiter, Juliet O'Hara, Henry Spencer, the Santa Barbara Police Department, the Blueberry car, and the pineapple hidden in nearly every episode. The theme stays engaging because it rewards fans who track both the mysteries and the show's constant running jokes.
