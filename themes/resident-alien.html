Resident Alien trivia works because the show blends an alien's deadpan view of humanity with a small-town murder mystery and a cast of lovable locals. Questions can cover Harry and his friends in Patience, the government agents on his trail and the aliens who follow him to Earth.

A strong Resident Alien quiz can include Harry Vanderspeigle and his lost device, Asta Twelvetrees, D'arcy Bloom, Sheriff Mike Thompson and Deputy Liv Baker, Max Hawthorne who can see Harry's true form, Mayor Ben and Kate Hawthorne, the octopus named 42, the Greys, the Mantid and Heather the Blue Avian, General McCallister, Peter Bach and his podcast, the murder of Sam Hodges, the 59 bar and Joe's Diner, and the time-travel trip to 1970. It works because four seasons of alien mishaps give fans plenty of oddly specific moments to remember.

If you enjoy Resident Alien trivia, also try Killing Eve and 24 on Trivia Gauntlet.
