  • What powerful company is determined to wipe out piracy in the films? East India Trading Company
  • What does Sao Feng possess that the crew needs to sail to World's End? Navigational charts
  • In which film does Captain Salazar hunt Jack Sparrow? Dead Men Tell No Tales
  • What Royal Navy ship do Jack and Will commandeer to chase the Black Pearl in the first film? The Interceptor
  • During the three-way duel in Dead Man's Chest, the men fight atop what runaway object? A water wheel
  • Which actor plays Will Turner? Orlando Bloom
  • Where does Angelica claim to have spent years before Jack found her again? A convent
  • What kind of undead pet does Barbossa keep? A monkey
  • Which actor plays Captain Hector Barbossa? Geoffrey Rush
  • What is the assembly of pirate leaders called in At World's End? The Brethren Court

Pirates of the Caribbean trivia works because the franchise mixes real pirate lore with supernatural curses and sprawling crews, giving fans plenty of ships, artifacts and legends to be tested on across five films.

A strong Pirates of the Caribbean quiz can include Jack Sparrow, Will Turner, Elizabeth Swann, Davy Jones, Captain Barbossa and Blackbeard, ships like the Black Pearl and the Flying Dutchman, the Pirate Code, and curses tied to the Caribbean's supernatural legends. The theme stays engaging because it spans multiple generations of pirates and shifting alliances across the series.
