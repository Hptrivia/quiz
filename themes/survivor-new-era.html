Survivor New Era trivia works because the shorter, twist-heavy seasons from Survivor 41 onward have created a fresh set of winners, blindsides and advantages for fans to track. Questions can cover the castaways, the tribes and the twists of each season.

A strong Survivor New Era quiz can include host Jeff Probst, the 18-player, 26-day format filmed in Fiji's Mamanuca Islands, the Shot in the Dark and its one-in-six odds, the Beware Advantage and the hourglass twist, tribes losing their flint, the final four fire-making challenge, winners such as Erika, Maryanne, Mike Gabler, Yam Yam, Dee, Kenzie, Rachel and Kyle, the Tika Three and the Reba Four, and the Survivor 50 celebration season. It works because fans who watch every Tribal Council can remember who played what and when.
