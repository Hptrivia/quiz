Real Housewives trivia works because nearly two decades of feuds, reunions and taglines across many cities have given fans a huge store of memorable moments. Questions can cover the women, their businesses and the scandals that made headlines.

A strong Real Housewives quiz can include Vicki Gunvalson and Coto Insurance, Brooks Ayers, Teresa Giudice and Joe's deportation to Italy, LuAnn de Lesseps as The Countess, Dorinda Medley's Blue Stone Manor, Gizelle Bryant and EveryHue Beauty, Ashley Darby's restaurant Oz, Ubah Hassan's hot sauce, Sara Al Madani in Dubai, the opening credits group shots, Andy Cohen and the multi-part reunions. It works because Bravo fans remember every table flip and every tagline.
