Westworld trivia works because the series mixes a twisting puzzle-box story with hosts, timelines and questions about consciousness and control. Fans can be tested on the characters, the parks, the mysteries and the show's many reveals.

A strong Westworld quiz can include Dolores, Maeve, Bernard, the Man in Black, Ford and Arnold, the host uprising, the maze and the multiple timelines, Delos and the parks, the Forge and the simulations, consciousness and the bicameral mind, and the major twists and reveals across the seasons. It works because it combines a complex puzzle-box narrative with deep characters and big philosophical ideas.
