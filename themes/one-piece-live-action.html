One Piece live-action trivia works because the series turns Eiichiro Oda's manga into a real-world adventure while adding its own details, scenes and dialogue. Questions can cover the Straw Hats, the villains they face and the islands they visit along the way.

A strong One Piece live-action quiz can include Luffy and the Gum Gum Fruit, Zoro and his swords, Nami and the tangerine grove, Usopp and Syrup Village, Sanji and the Baratie, Zeff and the All Blue, Shanks, Garp, Coby and Captain Morgan, Buggy the Clown, Kuro, Arlong and Mihawk, Smoker and Tashigi in Loguetown, the Going Merry, Laboon and Crocus, Baroque Works and Princess Vivi, and Chopper and Dr. Kureha on Drum Island. It works because fans can compare every scene with its anime and manga counterpart.
