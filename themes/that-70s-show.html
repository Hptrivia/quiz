That '70s Show trivia works because eight seasons of basement hangouts, running gags and coming-of-age storylines gave the gang a huge shared history fans still quote. Questions can cover Eric and Donna, Kelso and Jackie, and the circle of friends who never really left Point Place.

A strong That '70s Show quiz can include Eric Forman and Topher Grace, Donna Pinciotti and Laura Prepon, Michael Kelso and Ashton Kutcher, Jackie Burkhart and Mila Kunis, Steven Hyde and Danny Masterson, Fez and his foreign exchange student status, Kitty and Red Forman, Bob and Midge Pinciotti, the basement Circle scenes, the Vista Cruiser station wagon, Leo the aging hippie, and the Green Bay Packers helmet the gang wears for stupid decisions. It works because fans who watched every season remember exactly which basement moment, road trip or breakup belonged to which year.
