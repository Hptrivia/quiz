The Good Place trivia works because the show is full of running jokes, twists and tiny background gags that reward a close rewatch. Questions can cover how the humans died, the neighborhood's rules, Michael's reboots and the big reveals in each season.

A strong Good Place quiz can include Eleanor and her 'ya basic' insult, Chidi the ethics professor and his stomachaches, Tahani and her sister Kamilah, Jason and the Jacksonville Jaguars, Michael the architect and his love of paper clips, Janet and her cacti, the frozen yogurt shops, 'fork' and 'shirt', Mindy St. Claire's Medium Place, Shawn and the Bad Place, Judge Gen, Doug Forcett, the Accountants and the 802 reboots. It works because fans who followed every twist remember the details.
