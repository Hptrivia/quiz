Six Feet Under trivia works because every episode opens with a death that echoes through the Fisher family's own lives, giving fans a mix of case-of-the-week mortality and a deep, ongoing family drama to be tested on.

A strong Six Feet Under quiz can include Nathaniel Fisher Sr.'s death that opens the series, David and Keith's relationship, Nate's brain condition, Brenda's family history, Claire's art school journey, and the show's famous flash-forward finale. The theme stays engaging because it can move between the Fisher & Sons funeral home's daily cases and the family's long-running personal struggles.
