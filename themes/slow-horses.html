Slow Horses trivia works because it blends genuine espionage plotting with dry, cutting humor and a cast of exiled agents, giving fans both mission details and the show's sharp character dynamics to be quizzed on.

A strong Slow Horses quiz can include Jackson Lamb, River Cartwright, Diana Taverner, Catherine Standish and the rest of the Slough House exiles, MI5's Regent's Park, the source Mick Herron novels, and the disasters that landed each agent in Slough House. The theme stays engaging because every season adapts a distinct spy plot while deepening the same core cast.
