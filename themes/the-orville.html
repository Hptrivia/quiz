The Orville trivia works because the show pairs genuine workplace-comedy energy with earnest, classic-style space adventure. Fans can be tested on the ship's crew dynamics, its alien cultures like the Moclans, and the Union's conflicts with rival species.

A strong The Orville quiz can include Captain Ed Mercer and his ex-wife Kelly Grayson serving together, Bortus and his Moclan culture, the artificial lifeform Isaac, Dr. Claire Finn, the Union and the Krill, and the show's blend of workplace comedy and old-school sci-fi adventure. It works because it plays like an affectionate homage to classic space exploration shows while carving out its own characters and conflicts.
