Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In Season 5, What is the name of Detective Lowe's daughter? Scarlett
  • What is the surname of the new family that moves into the Murder House after the Harmons? Ramos
  • What is the name of the fraternity whose members assault Madison at the party in Season 3? Kappa Lambda Gamma
  • What does the secret ingredient in Madame LaLaurie's beauty poultice consist of? Human pancreas
  • What is the name of the servant at Miss Robichaux's Academy who has no tongue? Spalding
  • What instrument did Vivien play professionally in Murder House? Cello
  • What is the name of Kit Walker's wife in Asylum? Alma
  • What was Briarcliff Manor before the Catholic Church converted it into a sanitarium? Tuberculosis ward
  • What school does Liz arrange for both Scarlett Lowe and Will Drake's son to attend in Season 5? Thatcher School
  • What room number at the Hotel Cortez is described as a room that is never rented out? 64

American Horror Story trivia works because each season is a self-contained nightmare, from Murder House and Asylum to Coven and Hotel, tied together by a recurring cast. Fans can be tested on the characters, the settings and the scares across the anthology.

A strong American Horror Story quiz can include the Harmon family and the Murder House, Briarcliff and the horrors of Asylum, the witches of Coven, the Hotel Cortez and Freak Show, the recurring actors who play new roles each season, the ghosts, monsters and villains, and the connections that link the seasons together. It works because it rewards fans who follow both the standalone stories and the shared universe.
