Love Island USA trivia works because every season brings new couples, recouplings and viral moments, and the show's rules and challenges give fans plenty to be tested on. Questions can cover the winners, the villa twists and the islanders who defined each summer.

A strong Love Island USA quiz can include the villas in Las Vegas, Hawaii, California and Fiji, bombshells and Casa Amor, stick or twist, the Hideaway and the firepit, Movie Night and the Hearts on Fire challenge, Aftersun and the Love Island app, hosts Arielle Vandenberg and Ariana Madix, narrator Iain Stirling, winners like Serena and Kordell and Amaya and Bryan, and fan favourites such as JaNa and Kenny and Rob Rausch. It works because fans who watched every episode remember who coupled with whom and how each season ended.
