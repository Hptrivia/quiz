Everybody Loves Raymond trivia works because its comedy comes almost entirely from one recognizable family dynamic, giving fans plenty of specific running jokes and character quirks to be tested on.

A strong Everybody Loves Raymond quiz can include Ray Barone's job as a sportswriter, his wife Debra's frustrations with his in-laws, Marie and Frank Barone living across the street, Ray's brother Robert's rivalry with him, and the family's Long Island setting. The theme stays engaging because it draws on the constant friction between Ray's own household and his overbearing parents next door.
