Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What type of scandal does the cruise division face that threatens the company's public image? Sexual misconduct and cover-ups
  • Who is the COO of Waystar Royco fired in the first season? Frank Vernon
  • Which major American newspaper is part of Waystar Royco’s portfolio? The New York Globe
  • In season three, which country do the Roy siblings visit for their mother’s wedding? Italy
  • Where did the wedding between the political consultant and the theme park executive take place? Eastnor Castle
  • Who delivers the internal documents about the cruise scandal to a Senate investigation? Greg Hirsch
  • What is the name of the chain of theme parks and adjoining resorts owned and operated by Waystar Royco? Brightstar Adventure
  • What is the name of the presidential candidate that ATN calls the election for in Season 4? Jeryd Mencken
  • Which Roy sibling is often referred to by the nickname "Shiv"? Siobhan Roy
  • Who is Siobhan Roy married to? Tom Wambsgans

Succession trivia works because the show is built on sharp character conflict, shifting alliances, humiliations, negotiations, and repeated battles for control inside one deeply dysfunctional family. Viewers tend to remember both the big corporate moves and the vicious little moments between characters.

A strong Succession quiz can include Logan, Kendall, Shiv, Roman, Connor, Tom, Greg, Waystar Royco, board votes, acquisitions, elections, scandals, family weddings, betrayals, and the many failed or half-successful attempts to take control. The theme works because it can balance business strategy, character ego, family resentment, and the specific lines, habits, and incidents that made the series so quotable and tense.
