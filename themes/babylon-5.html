Babylon 5 trivia works because the show built one of TV's earliest long-form serialized arcs, with real political stakes, alien cultures, and a war that reshapes the galaxy. Fans can be tested on its commanders, its factions, and the station's central mission of keeping the peace.

A strong Babylon 5 quiz can include Commander Sinclair and Captain Sheridan, Ambassador Delenn and the Minbari, Londo Mollari and G'Kar's rivalry between the Centauri and Narn, Susan Ivanova, Michael Garibaldi, the Shadow War, the Grey Council, the Earth-Minbari War, and the founding of the Interstellar Alliance. It works because it spans political intrigue, alien cultures, and a five-season arc with real consequences.
