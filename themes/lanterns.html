  • Which publisher's characters is Lanterns based on? DC Comics
  • What does the Guardian give Bernadette as compensation? A diamond

  • What name did Abin Sur give for the sector he protected? Sector 2814

  • What nickname does Guy Gardner use for the prison Sinestro escaped from? Spazkaban
  • What phenomenon does the Halogram use to describe the portal connecting two points in the universe? Einstein-Rosen bridge

Lanterns trivia works because the series brings the Green Lantern Corps down to earth with a small-town crime story and two timelines. Questions can cover Hal and John, the murder they are sent to solve and the cosmic secrets behind it.

A strong Lanterns quiz can include Hal Jordan and his frozen body, John Stewart and his Marine sniper past, John Stewart Sr., Sinestro, Kilowog, Sector 2814, Coast City, the Nebraska town of Chadron, Kerry Kane from Gotham, Earl the greenhouse plant, Antaan, the Manhunter, Mogo, the power rings and oaths, and the people of the small town caught up in the case. It works because the show hides clues for viewers who pay attention to both timelines.
