NCIS trivia works because more than twenty seasons have built one of TV's most recognisable teams, packed with rules, nicknames and running gags. Questions can cover Gibbs and his rules, Tony, Ziva and McGee, Abby's lab and Ducky's autopsy room.

A strong NCIS quiz can include Gibbs and his boat in the basement, Gibbs's rules, Tony DiNozzo and his movie references, Ziva David and Mossad, Timothy McGee and Thom E. Gemcity, Abby Sciuto and Caf-Pow, Ducky Mallard and Jimmy Palmer, Director Jenny Shepard and Leon Vance, Tobias Fornell, Mike Franks, Kasie Hines, Ari Haswari, the Port-to-Port Killer and the Washington Navy Yard. It works because long-time fans know the team's catchphrases and history by heart.
