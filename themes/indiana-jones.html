Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In The Last Crusade, how long has the immortal knight guarded the Grail? Seven centuries
  • In which decade is Kingdom of the Crystal Skull set? 1950s
  • In The Last Crusade, the third Grail challenge, the 'Path of God,' requires Indy to take what? A leap of faith
  • Which actor has played Indiana Jones in all five films? Harrison Ford
  • Who directed the first four Indiana Jones films? Steven Spielberg
  • Who created the character of Indiana Jones? George Lucas
  • In Kingdom of the Crystal Skull, which ancient tribe guards the city of Akator? The Ugha
  • In Temple of Doom, the climactic rope bridge is suspended over a river full of what? Crocodiles
  • In Dial of Destiny's 1944 opening, what holy relic are Indy and Basil after, which turns out to be a fake? The Lance of Longinus
  • In Raiders of the Lost Ark, Indy and Sallah discover the monkey was killed by eating what? Poisoned dates

Indiana Jones trivia works because each film sends Indy chasing a different legendary artifact across a different corner of the world, giving fans a huge spread of relics, sidekicks and set pieces to be tested on.

A strong Indiana Jones quiz can include Indiana Jones, Marion Ravenwood, Short Round, Henry Jones Sr. and Helena Shaw, artifacts like the Ark of the Covenant, the Holy Grail and the Crystal Skull, and locations from Cairo to Venice to Shanghai. The theme stays engaging because it spans four decades of films while following the same archaeologist-adventurer formula.
