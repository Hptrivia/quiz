Cowboy Bebop trivia works because every session is a self-contained story with a distinct villain, setting, and mood, all tied together by a crew haunted by its past. Fans can be tested on bounties, ships, and the syndicate that hangs over the series.

A strong Cowboy Bebop quiz can include Spike Spiegel, Jet Black, Faye Valentine, Ed and Ein the data dog, Vicious, the Red Dragon Syndicate and its ruling elders, the Swordfish II, bounties, Mars, and the show's episodes, which are called sessions. It works because it blends noir, western, and sci-fi into episodes that are easy to remember one by one.
