Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • According to "Terminator 3," what caused Sarah Connor's death? Leukemia
  • In "Terminator 3," what does the T-X use to seize control of other machines? Nanotechnology
  • What are Skynet's large airborne hunting machines in the future war called? Hunter-Killers
  • In the opening of "Terminator: Dark Fate," in what year is John Connor killed? 1998
  • Which film first introduced the shape-shifting liquid-metal Terminator to the series? Terminator 2: Judgment Day
  • What vehicle does Enrique Salceda give Sarah Connor in "Terminator 2"? A Ford Bronco
  • Which catchphrase does Kyle Reese first say to Sarah Connor? Come with me if you want to live
  • What is the model designation of the villain Terminator in "Terminator: Dark Fate"? Rev-9
  • What are the fast motorcycle machines Skynet deploys in "Terminator Salvation" called? Moto-Terminators
  • Which desert survivalist supplies Sarah Connor with weapons in "Terminator 2"? Enrique Salceda

Terminator trivia works because the franchise's time-travel structure keeps rewriting its own future, giving fans multiple timelines, Terminator models and Judgment Day dates to keep track of and be tested on.

A strong Terminator quiz can include Sarah Connor, John Connor, the T-800, T-1000 and other Terminator models, Skynet and Cyberdyne Systems, Judgment Day, and the shifting timelines introduced across the sequels. The theme stays engaging because each film reopens the question of whether the future can actually be changed.
