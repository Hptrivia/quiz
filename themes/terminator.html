Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In the motel repair scene of the first film, what does the Terminator cut out of itself? Its eye
  • What material forms the Rev-9's shape-shifting outer layer in "Terminator: Dark Fate"? Mimetic polyalloy
  • In what year is the main present-day action of "Terminator: Dark Fate" set? 2020
  • What type of machine is the Terminator described as? Cyborg
  • At the end of "Terminator Salvation," what does Marcus give to save John Connor? His heart
  • In "Terminator Genisys," which model is Skynet's agent who converts John Connor? T-5000
  • How does the T-1000 copy a person or object? By touch
  • In the first film, who is sent from the future to protect Sarah Connor? Kyle Reese
  • In "Terminator Genisys," Skynet is disguised as what kind of product about to launch? An operating system
  • In "Terminator: Dark Fate," what is the name of the young woman the Rev-9 is sent to kill? Dani Ramos

Terminator trivia works because the franchise's time-travel structure keeps rewriting its own future, giving fans multiple timelines, Terminator models and Judgment Day dates to keep track of and be tested on.

A strong Terminator quiz can include Sarah Connor, John Connor, the T-800, T-1000 and other Terminator models, Skynet and Cyberdyne Systems, Judgment Day, and the shifting timelines introduced across the sequels. The theme stays engaging because each film reopens the question of whether the future can actually be changed.
