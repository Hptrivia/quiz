  • What is Kyle Reese's serial number in the franchise? DN38416
  • What is the name of the artificial intelligence that turns against humanity? Skynet

Terminator trivia works because the franchise's time-travel structure keeps rewriting its own future, giving fans multiple timelines, Terminator models and Judgment Day dates to keep track of and be tested on.

A strong Terminator quiz can include Sarah Connor, John Connor, the T-800, T-1000 and other Terminator models, Skynet and Cyberdyne Systems, Judgment Day, and the shifting timelines introduced across the sequels. The theme stays engaging because each film reopens the question of whether the future can actually be changed.
