  • What is Oraetta Mayflower's job? Nurse
  • What is Karl Weathers' profession in Luverne? Lawyer
  • What new identity is Hanzee Dent given after the Sioux Falls massacre? Moses Tripoli
  • Which network airs Fargo? FX
  • At what restaurant near Luverne does Rye commit a triple murder? Waffle Hut
  • What profession does Malvo pose as to befriend Burt Canton? A dentist
  • What nickname does the ghost haunting the Smutny home go by? Mr. Snowman

  • What is the name of the experimental cancer drug trial Betsy Solverson joins? Xanadu
  • What is the name of the shady company Emmit Stussy borrowed money from? Narwhal

Fargo trivia works because every season brings a new cast, a new decade and a new spiral of bad decisions. Questions can cover Lester Nygaard and Lorne Malvo, the Solversons, the Blumquists and the crime families across the anthology.

A strong Fargo quiz can include Lester Nygaard the insurance salesman from Bemidji, Lorne Malvo and his trail of chaos, Molly and Lou Solverson, Gus Grimly the mailman, Sam Hess and his trucking business, Stavros Milos the Supermarket King, Peggy and Ed Blumquist the beautician and butcher, Rye Gerhardt and the Waffle Hut murders, the Fargo mob and Lester's fall through the ice. It works because the details are as memorable as the twists.
