American Dad trivia works because it mixes CIA spy-fi absurdity with sharp family sitcom comedy, giving fans a wide mix of Stan's missions and the Smith household's running gags to be tested on.

A strong American Dad quiz can include Stan Smith's job at the CIA, the alien Roger's many disguises and personas, Klaus the goldfish's brain-transplant backstory, Steve and his friend group, and the Smith family's Langley Falls neighborhood. The theme stays engaging because it can move between Stan's espionage plots and the household's own chaotic family comedy.
