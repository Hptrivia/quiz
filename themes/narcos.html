Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which policy does Escobar wage his bombing campaign to have abolished? Extradition
  • Which guerrilla group storms the Palace of Justice in Narcos? M-19
  • Which birthday is Félix celebrating as his associates' loyalty starts to fray? His 40th
  • In which year did Narcos premiere? 2015
  • What tactic does Walt's team use to work its way up the cartel's ranks? Kidnappings
  • What is the name of the vigilante outfit that starts destroying Escobar's associates and property? Los Pepes
  • Which family member is Escobar speaking to on the radio when he is finally traced? His son
  • Which of Escobar's prized possessions do the vigilantes burn? His car collection
  • How many episodes are in each season across both Narcos series? Ten
  • Which politically connected businessman do the DEA capture by diverting his private jet? Rubén Zuno Arce

Narcos trivia works because the show dramatizes the real rise and fall of some of the biggest drug traffickers in history, mixing manhunts with cartel politics. Fans can be tested on Escobar's empire, the DEA's pursuit, and the Mexican cartel saga that follows it.

A strong Narcos quiz can include Pablo Escobar's rise and manhunt, the DEA agents Steve Murphy and Javier Pena, the Medellin and Cali cartels, the founding of Narcos: Mexico with Felix Gallardo and the Guadalajara cartel, Amado Carrillo Fuentes, and the real-world events the show dramatizes. It works because it spans two eras of the drug trade with a huge cast of traffickers and the agents hunting them.
