Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which border town does Pablo Acosta run his smuggling operation from? Ojinaga
  • Which DEA agent works undercover against the cartel in Narcos: Mexico? Kiki Camarena
  • Which trade agreement forms a backdrop to the later seasons of Narcos: Mexico? NAFTA
  • In which year did Narcos premiere? 2015
  • What is the name of the DEA operation launched to hunt down the men behind a murdered agent? Operation Leyenda
  • Which actor plays Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo in Narcos: Mexico? Diego Luna
  • What is the name of the police unit assembled to hunt Escobar? The Search Bloc
  • Which political party holds power throughout Narcos: Mexico? The PRI
  • Which country does Rafa flee to after the DEA burns his marijuana fields? Costa Rica
  • What nickname does Amado Carrillo Fuentes become known by? Lord of the Skies

Narcos trivia works because the show dramatizes the real rise and fall of some of the biggest drug traffickers in history, mixing manhunts with cartel politics. Fans can be tested on Escobar's empire, the DEA's pursuit, and the Mexican cartel saga that follows it.

A strong Narcos quiz can include Pablo Escobar's rise and manhunt, the DEA agents Steve Murphy and Javier Pena, the Medellin and Cali cartels, the founding of Narcos: Mexico with Felix Gallardo and the Guadalajara cartel, Amado Carrillo Fuentes, and the real-world events the show dramatizes. It works because it spans two eras of the drug trade with a huge cast of traffickers and the agents hunting them.
