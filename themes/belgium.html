Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which large African territory was ruled by Belgium as a colony until 1960? The Congo
  • Which Belgian king ran the Congo Free State as his own private possession? Leopold II
  • Which colour forms the middle band of the Belgian flag? Yellow
  • Which fictional Belgian detective was created by Agatha Christie? Hercule Poirot
  • Which currency is used in Belgium today? The euro
  • Which Belgian singer-songwriter recorded the classic chanson Ne me quitte pas? Jacques Brel
  • Which town founded by the Romans claims to be the oldest in Belgium? Tongeren
  • Which Brussels-born anatomist published On the Fabric of the Human Body in 1543? Andreas Vesalius
  • Which nickname describes the dense urban region bounded by Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent and Leuven? The Flemish Diamond
  • In which sport did the Belgian Stefan Everts win ten world championships? Motocross

Belgium trivia works well because it can move easily between the country's language divide, EU and NATO institutions, food culture, and history without feeling repetitive. It gives you more range than a narrow capitals-only or flag-only country quiz.

A strong Belgium quiz can draw from Brussels as home to the EU and NATO, the Flanders-Wallonia language split, Brussels sprouts, waffles, chocolate and beer, comic-strip icons like Tintin, artists such as Magritte, and figures like King Baudouin and jazz musician Toots Thielemans. That mix helps the theme feel more complete, because players are not only recalling geography but also wider cultural and historical knowledge tied to the country.
