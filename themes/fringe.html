  • At which airport did the passenger flight in the first episode come down? Logan International
  • What relation is Ella to Olivia? Her niece
  • What is the name of the autopilot system fitted at the airport in the opening episode? PEARL
  • Which flower appears in the letter Walter receives as a sign of forgiveness? Tulip
  • What was the job of the man who quietly helped Olivia escape the other side? A taxi driver
  • In which branch of the military did Olivia serve as a special investigator? The Marine Corps
  • Which government body oversees Fringe Division in the future? Ministry of Science

  • In which century was the book about the vanished civilisation published? The 1800s
  • How many Observers were originally sent back to study early humanity? Twelve

Fringe trivia works because the show mixes case-of-the-week sci-fi horror with a sprawling mythology about a parallel universe. Fans can be tested on Walter's experiments, the two worlds' counterpart characters, and the Observers who watch over it all.

A strong Fringe quiz can include Olivia Dunham, the eccentric scientist Walter Bishop, his son Peter, Astrid Farnsworth, the parallel universe and its counterpart characters, the Observers, and the show's recurring fringe science cases. It works because it spans standalone weird-science cases and a deep mythology about two worlds colliding.
