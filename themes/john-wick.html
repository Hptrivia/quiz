Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In Chapter 4, what breed is Mr. Nobody's dog? Belgian Malinois
  • In the first film, what car does Winston give John to make up for Ms. Perkins' attack? A Dodge Charger
  • How many main John Wick films star Keanu Reeves as the lead? Four
  • In John Wick: Chapter 3, to which country does John flee? Morocco
  • In Chapter 3, how many trained dogs does Sofia fight with? Two
  • In Chapter 2, how does Santino destroy John's house? With a grenade launcher
  • In Chapter 2, what is the name of the mirrored museum exhibit where the finale unfolds? Reflections of the Soul
  • What is the one unbreakable rule on Continental grounds? No killing allowed
  • What was John Wick's former profession before he retired? Assassin
  • In Chapter 3, how long are Winston and the Bowery King given to step down? Seven days

John Wick trivia works because the franchise builds out an entire criminal underworld with its own rules and institutions, giving fans plenty of lore, allies and set pieces to be tested on across four films.

A strong John Wick quiz can include John Wick, Winston, the Continental Hotel, the High Table, the Ruska Roma, John's marker and blood oath, and his reputation as Baba Yaga throughout the underworld. The theme stays engaging because it spans a tightly connected mythology of assassins, hotels and criminal governance across the whole series.
