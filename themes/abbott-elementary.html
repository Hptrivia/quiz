Abbott Elementary trivia works because the mockumentary format gives every character sharp, quotable moments, and the show's Philadelphia public-school setting grounds its jokes in specific, memorable running storylines.

A strong Abbott Elementary quiz can include Janine Teagues, Gregory Eddie, Ava Coleman, Mr. Johnson, Melissa Schemmenti, Barbara Howard and Jacob Hill, the fictional Willard R. The theme stays engaging because it spans the show's tight ensemble dynamics and its season-by-season storylines.
