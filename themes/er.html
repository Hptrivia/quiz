  • What was Abby Lockhart's profession before she became a doctor? Nurse
  • What does Sam Taggart's younger sister Kelly do for a living? Fashion designer
  • In a celebrated third-season episode, a robbery and hostage standoff plays out inside what kind of business? A convenience store
  • Which region of Sudan does Carter later do aid work in? Darfur
  • In the pilot, which basic skill is Carter shown learning for the first time? Starting an IV
  • Bob Newhart's three-episode season ten character worked in which profession? Architect
  • Doug Ross is honoured in season two with an award in which category? Community service
  • In which season do Doug Ross and the show's original head nurse both depart the series? Season 5
  • Which war-torn province does John Carter work in Africa? Kisangani
  • Which weaponized substance is accidentally released in the emergency room in the season fifteen premiere? Ricin

ER trivia works because the show set the template for the modern hospital drama, with fast-paced trauma cases and a huge cast of doctors whose personal lives played out alongside the medicine. Fans can be tested on its cases, its characters, and its most talked-about episodes.

A strong ER quiz can include Dr. Mark Greene, Dr. Doug Ross, Dr. Susan Lewis, John Carter's journey from student to attending, Kerry Weaver, Jing-Mei Chen, County General Hospital, the show's famous cold-open trauma cases, and the series finale. It works because it spans fifteen seasons of medical crises, staff turnover, and long-running personal storylines.
