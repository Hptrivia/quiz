  • What do the two mouth noise performers bill their act as? Kings of Mouth Noise
  • What record does the trash-talking fighter bring into the pay-per-view promo? 14-0
  • In which county do the bar mitzvah party motivators ply their trade? Nassau County
  • What is the title of the album that gives the detective his evidence against Gun Rack? I Killed Darnell Simmons

  • A TV news crew fabricates a sighting of which creature, sparking a neighborhood riot? A Pegasus

  • What is the stage name of the socially conscious rapper in the duet music video? Da Struggle

Key & Peele trivia works because nearly every sketch has a memorable character, catchphrase, or twist that fans quote for years. Fans can be tested on who played whom, where the joke lands, and what the punchline actually was.

A strong Key & Peele quiz can include Keegan-Michael Key, Jordan Peele, Luther the anger translator for Obama, substitute teacher Mr. Garvey, Meegan and Andre, the East/West College Bowl, Wendell, and celebrity guest appearances by actors like Ty Burrell and Rashida Jones. It works because the sketches are short, distinctive, and full of specific details fans remember.
