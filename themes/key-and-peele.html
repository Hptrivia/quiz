Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which strikingly plain name closes the West team introductions in the first East/West Bowl sketch? Dan Smith
  • In which year is the wartime Germany sketch set? 1942
  • In the sports-coverage parody, which profession gets the fame and pay of pro athletes? Teachers
  • A TV news crew fabricates a sighting of which creature, sparking a neighborhood riot? A Pegasus
  • What is the name of the pizza place Wendell phones with his enormous order? Mario's Pizza
  • How many points did the star player score before urging kids to jump off roofs? 33
  • In the superhero team sketch, what is the real color of the Falcon his teammates call Black Falcon? Green
  • Which instrument do the two duelling musicians play in the silent black-and-white club sketch? Trumpets
  • What word do the two strange airplane passengers use for terrorists? Terries
  • What is the name of the struggling public school for wizards? Clortho

Key & Peele trivia works because nearly every sketch has a memorable character, catchphrase, or twist that fans quote for years. Fans can be tested on who played whom, where the joke lands, and what the punchline actually was.

A strong Key & Peele quiz can include Keegan-Michael Key, Jordan Peele, Luther the anger translator for Obama, substitute teacher Mr. Garvey, Meegan and Andre, the East/West College Bowl, Wendell, and celebrity guest appearances by actors like Ty Burrell and Rashida Jones. It works because the sketches are short, distinctive, and full of specific details fans remember.
