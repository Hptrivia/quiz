Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In what year did the first X-Men film premiere in theaters? 2000
  • What corporation creates X-23 and other mutant children as weapons in Logan? Transigen
  • In which film does the character Cable first appear? Deadpool 2
  • What is Domino's mutant power? Luck manipulation
  • What are the anti-mutant robots built to hunt and kill mutants called? Sentinels
  • At which nuclear facility does Stryker imprison and experiment on mutants in X-Men Origins: Wolverine? Three Mile Island
  • In which area is the X-Mansion located? Westchester, New York
  • In which decade is X-Men: Apocalypse set? 1980s
  • Which comic book publisher created the X-Men? Marvel Comics
  • What is the codename of the secret program that gives Wolverine his metal skeleton? Weapon X

X-Men trivia works because the franchise spans decades of films, timelines and cast changes, giving fans a huge range of characters, powers and storylines to be tested on beyond just the core team.

A strong X-Men quiz can include Wolverine, Professor X, Magneto, Mystique, Storm, Deadpool, the Weapon X program, adamantium, the Days of Future Past timeline reset, and villains like Apocalypse and William Stryker. The theme stays engaging because it can move between the original trilogy, the prequel era, and the standalone spinoffs without losing thread.
