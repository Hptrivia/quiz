Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What is Wade Wilson's occupation before he becomes Deadpool? Mercenary
  • What device does Professor X use to locate mutants worldwide? Cerebro
  • Which coastal prison island does the government use to hold mutants in X-Men: The Last Stand? Alcatraz Island
  • Which Horseman title does Magneto receive from Apocalypse? War
  • In which Nazi concentration camp does young Erik Lehnsherr first manifest his powers in X-Men: First Class? Auschwitz
  • What is Colossus's mutant ability? Turns to steel
  • What is the name of the shapeshifting alien race Vuk leads in Dark Phoenix? D'Bari
  • Which actor plays Wade Wilson / Deadpool? Ryan Reynolds
  • What is the name of the secret facility where Ajax transforms Wade Wilson into Deadpool? The Workshop
  • What is the name of the mutant refuge island Magneto runs in Dark Phoenix? Genosha

X-Men trivia works because the franchise spans decades of films, timelines and cast changes, giving fans a huge range of characters, powers and storylines to be tested on beyond just the core team.

A strong X-Men quiz can include Wolverine, Professor X, Magneto, Mystique, Storm, Deadpool, the Weapon X program, adamantium, the Days of Future Past timeline reset, and villains like Apocalypse and William Stryker. The theme stays engaging because it can move between the original trilogy, the prequel era, and the standalone spinoffs without losing thread.
