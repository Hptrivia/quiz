Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What was James Holden's position aboard the Canterbury before it was destroyed? Executive officer
  • What kind of ship is the Canterbury, destroyed early in the series? An ice hauler
  • Which private security company employs Miller as a detective on Ceres? Star Helix Security
  • What is Inspector Sematimba's employer on Eros? CPM
  • What does the abbreviation OPA stand for? Outer Planets Alliance
  • Which streaming service revived the show after its original service stopped making it? Amazon Prime Video
  • What is Alex Kamal's role aboard the Rocinante? Pilot
  • Which two Martian warships are sent to intercept the Rocinante in the Season 5 finale? Serrio Mal and Koto
  • What is the name of the first world settled beyond the Ring, where Season 4 largely takes place? Ilus
  • Who is the de facto captain and leader of the Rocinante's crew? James Holden

The Expanse trivia works because its solar system politics are as detailed as its characters, and fans track every ship, faction and Belter phrase. Questions can cover the Rocinante crew, the protomolecule and the war between Earth, Mars and the Belt.

A strong Expanse quiz can include the Canterbury and the Scopuli in the pilot, Holden, Naomi, Amos and Alex aboard the Rocinante, Detective Miller on Ceres and Belter creole terms like welwala, Chrisjen Avasarala and UN politics, Fred Johnson and the OPA, Marco and Filip Inaros and the Free Navy, the protomolecule and Eros, the ring gate and Medina Station, Mars and the MCRN, the Laconia arc, and the cast behind the show. It works because it rewards fans who followed the whole six-season run.
