The Expanse trivia works because its solar system politics are as detailed as its characters, and fans track every ship, faction and Belter phrase. Questions can cover the Rocinante crew, the protomolecule and the war between Earth, Mars and the Belt.

A strong Expanse quiz can include the Canterbury and the Scopuli in the pilot, Holden, Naomi, Amos and Alex aboard the Rocinante, Detective Miller on Ceres and Belter creole terms like welwala, Chrisjen Avasarala and UN politics, Fred Johnson and the OPA, Marco and Filip Inaros and the Free Navy, the protomolecule and Eros, the ring gate and Medina Station, Mars and the MCRN, the Laconia arc, and the cast behind the show. It works because it rewards fans who followed the whole six-season run.
