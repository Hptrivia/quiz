Silo trivia works because the show is built around a slow-burn mystery, with a large cast of engineers, judges, IT officials and rebels whose secrets and lies define each season. Questions can cover Juliette and the mechanical department, the Pact and the rules that hold the silo together, and the discoveries that unravel everything the residents were told.

A strong Silo quiz can include Juliette Nichols and Rebecca Ferguson, Holston and Allison Becker, Bernard Holland and Robert Sims, Lukas Kyle, Walker and Shirley from Mechanical, Mayor Ruth Jahns and Deputy Marnes, the Pact and the Cleaning, relics and the Judicial department that confiscates them, Silo 17 and Solo, the fifty silos built by humanity, the Syndrome and Deputy Billings, the Flamekeepers and Gloria, and the Algorithm and Safeguard protocols that threaten every silo from within. It works because fans who watched every season know exactly which lie, relic or uprising shaped each silo's fate.
