Toy Story trivia works because almost everyone knows Woody and Buzz, yet the films are packed with small details fans remember for life. Questions can cover Andy's toys, Sid next door, Al's Toy Barn, Sunnyside Daycare, Forky and the road trip that follows.

A strong Toy Story quiz can include Woody, Buzz Lightyear, Jessie, Mr Potato Head, Rex, Hamm, Slinky Dog and Bullseye, Sid Phillips and his mutant toys, Stinky Pete and the Roundup gang, Al's Toy Barn, Emperor Zurg, Lotso and Sunnyside Daycare, Bonnie, Forky, Gabby Gabby, Duke Caboom and Bo Peep, plus the voice cast, directors and the running gags and catchphrases that carry across the whole series. It works because the films reward both the casual viewer who grew up with them and the fan who tracks every background toy.
