Vanderpump Rules trivia works because more than a decade of hookups, breakups and restaurant openings has given fans an enormous amount of drama to remember. Questions can cover the bars, the couples and the scandals.

A strong Vanderpump Rules quiz can include Lisa Vanderpump, SUR as Sexy Unique Restaurant, PUMP and TomTom, Tom Sandoval and Schwartz's own bar, Stassi, Kristen, Katie, Jax, Ariana, Scheana and her song, James Kennedy, Lala, Brittany, Raquel, Scandoval, the Witches of WeHo and the reunions. It works because fans remember every lie and every bar opening.
