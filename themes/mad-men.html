Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which position does Peggy Olson first have when she starts at Sterling Cooper? Secretary
  • By the end of the series, Peggy is working at which agency? McCann Erickson
  • Who is credited for the opening theme song of the show? RJD2
  • Which character is a media department head who pushes for television advertising? Harry Crane
  • What is Don Draper’s secret birth name? Dick Whitman
  • Which character is a young African American secretary hired as Don’s assistant at the new firm? Dawn
  • Which client does Don avoid pursuing because it would require security clearance from the government? North American Aviation
  • Which character is the eccentric copywriter who suffers a severe mental breakdown and believes a computer is affecting his thoughts? Michael Ginsberg
  • Which company campaign leads to Don’s iconic pitch using family slides and the word “Carousel”? Eastman Kodak Company
  • Which character works as a head of accounts at a rival firm and later joins Don’s merged company? Ted Chaough

Mad Men trivia works because the show is full of sharply drawn characters, memorable pitches, status games, marriages, affairs, and workplace shifts tied to a very specific era. Its details are quieter than action-driven shows, but they are often just as memorable.

A strong Mad Men quiz can include Don, Peggy, Roger, Joan, Betty, Pete, Sterling Cooper and its later forms, advertising campaigns, office hierarchy, family strain, identity questions, and the wider backdrop of 1960s American culture. The theme works because it can move between character psychology, business ambition, period detail, social change, and the smaller personal moments that give the series its depth.
