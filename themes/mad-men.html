Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In Season 6, which company beats Don Draper’s team to win the Heinz account? J. Walter Thompson
  • Which character fatally hangs himself in his office at the firm? Lane Pryce
  • What is the opening theme of Mad Men? A Beautiful Mine
  • Which auto company’s account plays a key role in the formation of Sterling Cooper Draper Pryce? Jaguar
  • What is Don Draper’s wife’s name at the start of the series? Betty
  • What is the name of Don and Betty Draper’s youngest son? Gene
  • Which character is the senior copywriter who was forced to take a leave of absence after urinating his pants? Freddy Rumsen
  • What is the name of Peggy Olson’s on-and-off journalist boyfriend? Abe
  • What is the product type of the “Carousel” pitch? Slide projector
  • Which character is a doctor who becomes Betty Draper’s second husband? Henry

Mad Men trivia works because the show is full of sharply drawn characters, memorable pitches, status games, marriages, affairs, and workplace shifts tied to a very specific era. Its details are quieter than action-driven shows, but they are often just as memorable.

A strong Mad Men quiz can include Don, Peggy, Roger, Joan, Betty, Pete, Sterling Cooper and its later forms, advertising campaigns, office hierarchy, family strain, identity questions, and the wider backdrop of 1960s American culture. The theme works because it can move between character psychology, business ambition, period detail, social change, and the smaller personal moments that give the series its depth.
