Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In which season does Vanya come out and take the name Viktor? Season 3
  • What is Elliott, the man who shelters Five in 1963? Conspiracy theorist
  • How many years before season 1 does Reginald turn Luther part ape? Seven years
  • What item of Reginald's goes missing after his death, making Luther suspect foul play? His monocle
  • How many children do Diego and Lila have in season 4? Three
  • Which time-policing organization employs Hazel and Cha-Cha? The Commission
  • After Five kills the time agency's board, how long is he given to gather his siblings? 90 minutes
  • In which year does the alternate-timeline Reginald dismiss Pogo? 2014
  • What kind of animal is Pogo, Reginald's assistant? A chimpanzee
  • In which year did the series premiere? 2019

The Umbrella Academy trivia works because each sibling's power and personality creates endless chaos. Questions can cover the Hargreeves siblings, Sir Reginald, Pogo and Grace, the Commission and the apocalypse they keep trying to prevent.

A strong Umbrella Academy quiz can include Luther on the Moon, Diego, Allison and her rumors, Klaus with Hello and Goodbye tattooed on his palms, Five and his mannequin companion Dolores, Ben, Vanya and her book Extra Ordinary, Sir Reginald Hargreeves and his monocle, Pogo the chimpanzee, Grace the robot mother, Hazel and Cha-Cha of the Commission, Griddy's Doughnuts and the 43 children born on October 1, 1989. It works because every season rewrites the timeline.
