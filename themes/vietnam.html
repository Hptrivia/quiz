Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which UNESCO World Heritage Cham temple complex in central Vietnam was built between the 4th and 14th centuries? My Son Sanctuary
  • What 1954 international agreement ended the First Indochina War and temporarily partitioned Vietnam? Geneva Accords
  • What turmeric-marinated grilled fish dish is a Hanoi specialty, often served with dill and rice noodles? Cha ca
  • Which river delta in southern Vietnam is famous for its floating markets? Mekong Delta
  • What noodle soup, usually made with beef or chicken broth, is Vietnam's most famous dish? Pho
  • What is the Vietnamese rice paper used to wrap fresh spring rolls called? Banh trang
  • Which Vietnamese beauty queen became the first Miss Universe Vietnam titleholder to place in the international Top 5? H'Hen Nie
  • Which Vietnamese swimmer became a national hero after winning multiple gold medals at the SEA Games in the 2010s? Nguyen Thi Anh Vien
  • What is Vietnam's highest mountain, nicknamed the 'Roof of Indochina'? Fansipan
  • Which UNESCO Global Geopark in Ha Giang province, near the Chinese border, is known for its dramatic karst plateau? Dong Van Karst Plateau Geopark

Vietnam trivia works well because it can move easily between the Vietnam War era, geography, food, and modern culture without feeling repetitive. It gives you more range than a narrow capitals-only or flag-only country quiz.

A strong Vietnam quiz can draw from the Vietnam War and the fall of Saigon, the Mekong River, Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, the dong, iconic dishes like pho and spring rolls, coffee and pepper exports, and figures like Vo Nguyen Giap and Thich Nhat Hanh. That mix helps the theme feel more complete, because players are not only recalling geography but also wider cultural and historical knowledge tied to the country.
