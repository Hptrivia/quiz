Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which national park in the Annamite mountains is home to the rare Edwards's pheasant and other endemic bird species? Bach Ma National Park
  • Which Vietnamese-French director won acclaim for the film 'The Scent of Green Papaya'? Tran Anh Hung
  • What letter is Vietnam's shape on a map often compared to? S
  • What yellow-noodle dish from Quang Nam province is topped with turmeric broth, herbs, and rice crackers? Mi quang
  • What flower is Vietnam's national flower? Lotus
  • Which 1954 battle ended French colonial rule in Vietnam? Battle of Dien Bien Phu
  • What economic reform program, launched in 1986, transitioned Vietnam toward a market economy? Doi Moi
  • What was Hanoi's historical name during centuries as Vietnam's imperial capital? Thang Long
  • Which UNESCO-listed limestone landscape near Ninh Binh is often nicknamed 'Ha Long Bay on land'? Trang An
  • Which Vietnamese shooter won the country's first-ever Olympic gold medal, at Rio 2016? Hoang Xuan Vinh

Vietnam trivia works well because it can move easily between the Vietnam War era, geography, food, and modern culture without feeling repetitive. It gives you more range than a narrow capitals-only or flag-only country quiz.

A strong Vietnam quiz can draw from the Vietnam War and the fall of Saigon, the Mekong River, Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, the dong, iconic dishes like pho and spring rolls, coffee and pepper exports, and figures like Vo Nguyen Giap and Thich Nhat Hanh. That mix helps the theme feel more complete, because players are not only recalling geography but also wider cultural and historical knowledge tied to the country.
