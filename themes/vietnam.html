Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which Vietnamese singer scored a global viral hit with the song 'See Tinh'? Hoang Thuy Linh
  • What rare mammal, nicknamed the 'Asian unicorn,' was discovered in Vietnam's forests in 1992? Saola
  • Which river flows through Hanoi and gives its name to a major northern delta region? Red River
  • What spicy noodle soup, named after its home city, features beef, pork, and a rich lemongrass-chili broth? Bun bo Hue
  • Which body of water separates northern Vietnam from Hainan Island, China? Gulf of Tonkin
  • Which Vietnamese author wrote 'The Tale of Kieu,' the country's most celebrated work of literature? Nguyen Du
  • Which underground tunnel network near Ho Chi Minh City was used by guerrilla fighters during the Vietnam War? Cu Chi Tunnels
  • What is Vietnam's most popular spectator sport? Football
  • What traditional Vietnamese performing art features puppets that dance on the surface of water? Water puppetry
  • In which year did the Vietnam War end with the fall of Saigon? 1975

Vietnam trivia works well because it can move easily between the Vietnam War era, geography, food, and modern culture without feeling repetitive. It gives you more range than a narrow capitals-only or flag-only country quiz.

A strong Vietnam quiz can draw from the Vietnam War and the fall of Saigon, the Mekong River, Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, the dong, iconic dishes like pho and spring rolls, coffee and pepper exports, and figures like Vo Nguyen Giap and Thich Nhat Hanh. That mix helps the theme feel more complete, because players are not only recalling geography but also wider cultural and historical knowledge tied to the country.
