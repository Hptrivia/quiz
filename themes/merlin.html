Merlin trivia works because five series in Camelot mix Arthurian legend with a secret the whole show turns on, giving fans knights, sorcerers, dragons and prophecies to remember. Questions can cover Merlin and Arthur, Morgana s turn and the creatures behind each quest.

A strong Merlin quiz can include Merlin and Colin Morgan, Arthur Pendragon and Bradley James, Uther and Gaius, Guinevere and Lancelot, Morgana and Morgause and the Old Religion, the dragons Kilgharrah and Aithusa and Merlin s power as a Dragonlord, Excalibur and the Lady of the Lake, villains and allies from Agravaine and Alator to Mordred, quests involving the Dorocha, the Eye of the Phoenix and the fortress of Ismere, and the final Battle of Camlann. It works because fans who watched every series know which secret, spell and prophecy shaped Albion.
