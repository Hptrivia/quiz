The Summer I Turned Pretty trivia works because the show builds its whole world around one beach house and one long-running love triangle that fans have picked a side in. Questions can be tested on Belly's summers, the Fisher family, and the key moments that shift the triangle.

A strong The Summer I Turned Pretty quiz can include Belly Conklin, the Fisher brothers Conrad and Jeremiah, their mother Susannah, the Cousins Beach setting, the debutante ball, and the milestones of Belly's summers as the love triangle develops across seasons. It works because it spans a slow-burn romance with plenty of specific, memorable summer moments to test.
