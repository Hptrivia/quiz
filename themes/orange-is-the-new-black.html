Orange Is the New Black trivia works because seven seasons at Litchfield gave nearly every inmate a name, a nickname and a flashback of her own. Questions can cover Piper and Alex, the guards and administrators, and the riot and privatization storylines that reshaped the prison.

A strong Orange Is the New Black quiz can include Piper Chapman and Taylor Schilling, Alex Vause and Laura Prepon, Red, Crazy Eyes, Taystee, Poussey, Nicky, Sophia, Gloria, Daya and Aleida, Pennsatucky and Big Boo, the guards from Bennett and Pornstache to Caputo and Piscatella, MCC and the shift to private management, the season four death that triggers the riot, max and the Season 5 standoff, ICE detention in the final season, Piper's real-life inspiration in Piper Kerman's memoir, and the flashbacks that explain how each woman landed inside. It works because fans who watched every season know whose backstory belongs to whom.
