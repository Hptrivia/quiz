Taylor Swift trivia works because her career spans multiple musical eras and a huge, well-documented catalog, giving fans plenty of albums, lyrics and career milestones to be tested on.

A strong Taylor Swift quiz can include albums like Fearless, 1989, Reputation and Evermore, her re-recorded 'Taylor's Version' releases, tours like the Eras Tour, and career milestones from her Nashville debut to her Billboard and Grammy history. The theme stays engaging because it can move between song-specific detail and the wider arc of her career.
