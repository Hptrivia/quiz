Carrie trivia works because the series reworks a story everyone knows with new characters, social media bullying and a darker look at Margaret White's household. Questions can cover the students, the adults around Carrie and the way each episode builds toward prom night.

A strong Carrie quiz can include Carrie and Margaret White, Sue Snell, Chris Hargensen and her Carrie Red sockpuppet account, Billy, Tommy, guidance counselor Peter Morton, Stella the neighbor girl, Chris's father John Hargensen, the hidden camera in Margaret's sewing room, the party at the empty McMansion, the pig's blood plan, Damon Nostra the fake psychic and the finale with Adelaide. It works because fans of the book and the films can test how well they know this new version on Prime Video.
