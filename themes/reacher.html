Reacher trivia works because each season drops the towering ex-military drifter into a new town and a new conspiracy, adapted from Lee Child's novels. Fans can be tested on the cases Reacher stumbles into, the allies he picks up, and the details specific to each season's story.

A strong Reacher quiz can include Jack Reacher's background in Army Criminal Investigation, Detective Oscar Finlay and officer Roscoe Conklin, the conspiracies uncovered in Margrave in season one, the New York case in season two, and the season three case adapted from the novels. It works because each season adapts a different Lee Child novel with its own self-contained conspiracy.
