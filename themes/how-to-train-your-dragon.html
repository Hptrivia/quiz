How to Train Your Dragon trivia works because it pairs genuine world-building around Viking and dragon lore with a strong central friendship, giving fans plenty of specific dragon species and village detail to be tested on.

A strong How to Train Your Dragon quiz can include Toothless as a Night Fury, Hiccup's village of Berk, his father Stoick the Vast, his friend-turned-rival Astrid, the dragon-training academy, and the wider dragon species introduced across the trilogy. The theme stays engaging because it spans Hiccup's growth from outcast to leader and Berk's changing relationship with dragons.
