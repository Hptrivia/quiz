Sinners trivia works because Ryan Coogler's 1932 Mississippi vampire story packs a huge amount of detail into a single night. Questions can cover the twins Smoke and Stack, the opening of Club Juke, Sammie's guitar and the vampires who come for them all.

A strong Sinners quiz can include Elias and Elijah, the twins known as Smoke and Stack, their years with the Chicago Outfit and the sawmill they buy in Clarksdale, Sammie the preacherboy and his father Jedidiah, Delta Slim, Annie, Cornbread and Pearline, Grace and Bo Chow's store, the vampire Remmick and Wild Mountain Thyme, garlic and sunlight and the Klan led by Hogwood, the 1992 epilogue with Buddy Guy, plus the filmmaking side with Michael B. Jordan in a dual role, Ryan Coogler directing, Ludwig Goransson's score and the Oscar nominations for Delroy Lindo and Wunmi Mosaku. It works because it rewards fans who caught the history woven through the horror.
