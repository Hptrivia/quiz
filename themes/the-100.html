The 100 trivia works because seven seasons moved from a single crashed pod of delinquents to warring clans, hidden bunkers and entirely new worlds, giving fans a huge cast of leaders, warriors and impossible choices to track. Questions can cover Clarke and the Sky People, the Grounder Commanders, and the shifting alliances that defined each season.

A strong The 100 quiz can include Clarke Griffin and Eliza Taylor, Bellamy Blake, Octavia Blake and her bond with the Grounder Lincoln, Raven Reyes, Commander Lexa and the Grounder Coalition capital of Polis, the Ark and Mount Weather, the Mountain Men and Reapers, Jaha and Kane, the City of Light and A.L.I.E., the jump to Sanctum and the new-world storylines of the final seasons, and the source novels by Kass Morgan. It works because fans who watched every season know exactly which alliance, betrayal or apocalypse shaped the story.
