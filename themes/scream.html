Scream trivia works because the franchise is built on a recognisable killer identity, self-aware horror-movie rules, and a returning cast that gives long-time fans plenty to track across films.

A strong Scream quiz can include the Woodsboro setting, the Ghostface identity, Dewey Riley, Sidney Prescott, Windsor College in Scream 2, and the newer films like Scream VI and the 2022 legacy sequel. The theme works because it can move between individual film plots, the killer's changing identities, and the franchise's long-running cast of survivors.
