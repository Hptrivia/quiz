Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which city is the capital of France? Paris
  • At which 1815 battle did Napoleon suffer his final defeat? Waterloo
  • Which walled medieval citadel in southern France is a UNESCO World Heritage Site? Carcassonne
  • Which French novelist wrote In Search of Lost Time? Marcel Proust
  • How many departments does France have in total today? 101
  • Which French king was known as the Sun King? Louis XIV
  • Which player scored twice with his head in the 1998 World Cup final? Zinedine Zidane
  • Which queen of France was executed by guillotine in 1793? Marie Antoinette
  • Which French general crowned himself Emperor in 1804? Napoleon Bonaparte
  • In which year was the constitution of the Fifth Republic adopted? 1958

France trivia works well because it can move easily between Paris landmarks, national history, food and wine culture, and politics without feeling repetitive. It gives you more range than a narrow capitals-only or flag-only country quiz.

A strong France quiz can draw from Paris and the National Assembly, the motto Liberté, Égalité, Fraternité, regional foods like Roquefort and Cognac, the origins of Champagne, and history from the Treaty of Verdun through to the French Revolution. That mix helps the theme feel more complete, because players are not only recalling geography but also wider cultural and historical knowledge tied to the country.
