Back to the Future trivia works because the trilogy is packed with callbacks, running gags, and tightly linked timelines that reward anyone who has watched the films closely. Fans can be tested on rules, dates, and small details from all three films.

A strong Back to the Future quiz can include Marty McFly, Doc Brown, Biff Tannen, George and Lorraine McFly, Clara, Einstein, Marlene, Hill Valley, the DeLorean hitting 88 mph, the 1955 town, the alternate 1985, the Old West of 1885, and Marty's future employer CusCo. It works because a handful of dates and objects connect all three films into one story.
