Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In which year did the anime series premiere? 2004
  • Schuwald sends Karl Neumann to carry a message to someone in which city? Dresden
  • In which country's war had Hugo Bernhardt killed the mother of the girl he now looks after? Myanmar
  • On which birthday does Johan appear to Nina? Her twentieth
  • Besides writing children's books, what was Bonaparta trained as? A brain surgeon
  • Julius Reichwein, who becomes an ally of Nina, works in which profession? A psychiatrist
  • In which city does Johan target the wealthy recluse Hans Georg Schuwald? Munich
  • What does Lunge leave at Grimmer's grave? A beer
  • Under which pen name did Bonaparta publish The Nameless Monster? Emil Scherbe
  • Which line of work does Eva Heinemann take up by the end of the series? Interior design

Monster trivia works because the series is a slow, layered mystery in which small details from one arc matter again much later. Fans can be tested on the characters, the places, and the clues that tie the story together.

A strong Monster quiz can include Dr. Kenzo Tenma, Johan Liebert, Nina Fortner, Inspector Lunge, the 511 Kinderheim orphanage, the Baby, Dieter, Grimmer, the picture books by Bonaparta, and the poisoned candy that kills the hospital's leaders. It works because a tight, twisting plot gives every character and location a role to remember.
