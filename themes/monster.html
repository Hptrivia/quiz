Monster trivia works because the series is a slow, layered mystery in which small details from one arc matter again much later. Fans can be tested on the characters, the places, and the clues that tie the story together.

A strong Monster quiz can include Dr. Kenzo Tenma, Johan Liebert, Nina Fortner, Inspector Lunge, the 511 Kinderheim orphanage, the Baby, Dieter, Grimmer, the picture books by Bonaparta, and the poisoned candy that kills the hospital's leaders. It works because a tight, twisting plot gives every character and location a role to remember.
