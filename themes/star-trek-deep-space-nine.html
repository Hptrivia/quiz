Deep Space Nine trivia works because it was the most serialized Star Trek series of its era, with long story arcs, complex villains and a station full of memorable regulars. Questions can cover the crew, the Bajoran and Cardassian politics and the war with the Dominion.

A strong Deep Space Nine quiz can include Benjamin Sisko as the Emissary, Kira Nerys, Odo and his need to regenerate, the Great Link, Quark's bar, Rom and Nog, Jadzia and Ezri Dax, Worf, Julian Bashir, Miles O'Brien, Garak the tailor, Gul Dukat, Weyoun, the Female Changeling, the Prophets and the wormhole, Terok Nor and the Defiant. It works because fans remember every arc and every Ferengi Rule of Acquisition.
