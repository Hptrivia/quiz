  • What is the name of the secret facility where River was experimented on? The Academy
  • What is the name of Tracey's hometown, where the crew intends to deliver his body in 'The Message'? St. Albans
  • At what battle does a flashback show Tracey serving alongside Mal and Zoe? Du-Khang
  • What gift does Jayne receive from grateful townsfolk in 'Our Mrs. Reynolds'? A rainstick
  • What was Jayne Cobb's job before he joined Serenity's crew? Mercenary
  • What is the title of Firefly's opening theme song? The Ballad of Serenity
  • Which actress plays Inara Serra? Morena Baccarin
  • What do Simon and River pretend to be in order to be smuggled into the Ariel hospital? Corpses
  • What alias does Saffron give herself when Mal first encounters her again? Bridget
  • What military rank did Zoe hold at the Battle of Serenity Valley? Corporal

Firefly trivia works because the show built a vivid crew and a lived-in space-western universe in just one season, and fans have kept its details alive ever since. Questions can be tested on Serenity's crew, their jobs, and the world of the Alliance and the Reavers.

A strong Firefly quiz can include Captain Malcolm Reynolds, Zoe and Wash, Inara, Jayne Cobb, Kaylee Frye, Simon and River Tam, the ship Serenity, the Alliance and the Unification War, the Reavers, and the crew's various jobs and close calls. It works because the short-lived series still packed in a distinct crew, a lived-in universe, and a devoted fanbase.
