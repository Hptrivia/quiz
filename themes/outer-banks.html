Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What symbol, inscribed on Elizabeth's amulet, points the Pogues toward the crypt? A half moon
  • According to legend in Season 4, what power is the blue crown said to grant? The power to grant wishes
  • In what year is Denmark Tanny killed? 1844
  • Per Wes's posthumous letter, JJ presses his adoptive father Luke to explain which code word? Albatross
  • Which actor plays John B? Chase Stokes
  • What is the name of the golden treasure sought in Season 4? The blue crown
  • In which South American country does the show place the lost city of El Dorado? Venezuela
  • As explained in Season 3, what exactly is a gnomon? A sundial's shadow-caster
  • What is the poorer, southern area of the island where the Pogues live called? The Cut
  • What legendary treasure does John B's father disappear while searching for? The gold of the Royal Merchant

Outer Banks trivia works because each season centers on its own treasure hunt with new legends, locations and betrayals, giving fans a fresh set of specific clues and set pieces to be quizzed on every year.

A strong Outer Banks quiz can include John B Routledge, Sarah Cameron, JJ, Pope Heyward, Kiara, Rafe Cameron and Ward Cameron, the Pogues-versus-Kooks class divide, the Twinkie van, and the hunt for the Royal Merchant's gold. The theme stays engaging because it spans multiple treasure hunts across different countries and legends.
