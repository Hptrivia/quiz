  • Which musician has a recurring Season 7 role as political figure Robert Huggins? André Benjamin

The Shield trivia works because seven seasons in Farmington traced one crooked unit from the pilot's last scene to the finale's, with a barn full of detectives, captains and gangs in between. Questions can cover Vic and the Strike Team, the Barn's leadership and the cases that closed in on them.

A strong The Shield quiz can include Vic Mackey and Michael Chiklis, Shane Vendrell, Curtis Lemansky and Ronnie Gardocki, Claudette Wyms and CCH Pounder, Dutch Wagenbach, David Aceveda and Captain Monica Rawling, the Farmington Barn and the Strike Team clubhouse, Terry Crowley and the pilot's final scene, the Armenian money train, Antwon Mitchell, Kavanaugh's internal affairs investigation, the One-Niners and Byz Lats, Corinne and Mara, Cassidy Mackey, and the immunity deal and desk job that end the series. It works because fans who watched every season know exactly which lie caught up with which character.
