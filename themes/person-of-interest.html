Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • The woman who runs the government's Machine programme is known by which one-word codename? Control
  • Which network did Finch hack as a teenager, drawing federal attention? ARPANET
  • Which computer virus does Finch steal in the final season? Ice-9
  • In which season does the team begin living under permanent cover identities? Season 4
  • Finch draws the surnames for his aliases from which category of names? Birds
  • What are the corrupt police officers and city officials who run organised crime known as? HR
  • Which actor plays John Reese? Jim Caviezel
  • What work does the Machine's front company hire people to do? Data entry
  • Which profession did Shaw train for before she became a government operative? Medicine
  • The team's dog responds to commands in which language? Dutch

Person of Interest trivia works because it pairs a case-of-the-week crime format with a slow-building AI mythology, giving fans both a new person to save each episode and a long-running conspiracy to be tested on.

A strong Person of Interest quiz can include Finch and Reese's New York City base of operations, the Machine's Social Security number outputs, Detective Carter and Fusco's roles, the rival AI Samaritan, and Root and Shaw's recruitment into the team. The theme stays engaging because it moves between individual rescue missions and the wider fight to control the Machine.
