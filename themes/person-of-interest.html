Person of Interest trivia works because it pairs a case-of-the-week crime format with a slow-building AI mythology, giving fans both a new person to save each episode and a long-running conspiracy to be tested on.

A strong Person of Interest quiz can include Finch and Reese's New York City base of operations, the Machine's Social Security number outputs, Detective Carter and Fusco's roles, the rival AI Samaritan, and Root and Shaw's recruitment into the team. The theme stays engaging because it moves between individual rescue missions and the wider fight to control the Machine.
