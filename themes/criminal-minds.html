Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In which state does the team track down the cannibalistic Unsub Floyd Feylinn Ferell? Florida
  • What was Penelope Garcia's hacker alias before joining the FBI? The Black Queen
  • Who was the elusive stalker-killer known as 'The Replicator' in Season 8? John Curtis
  • Reid is shown to be able to read extremely fast — roughly how many words per minute? 20,000
  • By what nickname was the killer George Foyet known? The Reaper
  • What does BAU stand for? Behavioral Analysis Unit
  • After leaving the BAU, which organization did Emily Prentiss work for? Interpol
  • What is Reid's favorite kind of donut? Chocolate frosted with sprinkles
  • Who recruited Penelope Garcia into the FBI? Aaron Hotchner
  • How many times has David Rossi been married? Four times

Criminal Minds trivia works because the show pairs a rotating gallery of memorable unsubs with a tight-knit BAU team whose dynamics evolve over many seasons, giving questions both case-of-the-week variety and long-running character depth. The focus on profiling and psychology gives the trivia a distinctive angle compared with other crime dramas.

A strong Criminal Minds quiz can include the Behavioral Analysis Unit and its FBI role, Aaron Hotchner, Derek Morgan, Spencer Reid, Penelope Garcia, David Rossi, Jennifer Jareau and Emily Prentiss, Gideon's early leadership, recurring unsubs like the Reaper and the Replicator, the team's profiling methods and jet, opening and closing quotes, and major arcs across the long run of the series.
