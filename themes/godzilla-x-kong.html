Godzilla x Kong trivia works because the MonsterVerse links its films through the same Titans, the same organization, and a growing cast of humans caught between them. Fans can be tested on creatures, characters, and set pieces across the whole series.

A strong Godzilla x Kong quiz can include Monarch, Dr. Serizawa, Ford Brody, Dr. Ilene Andrews, Walter Simmons and Apex, Mothra, Rodan, King Ghidorah, Mechagodzilla, Shimo, Yucca Mountain, Skull Island, and the Colosseum. It works because the creature battles are easy to picture and the film-to-film connections give plenty of specific details to ask about.
