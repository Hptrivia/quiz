Barry trivia works because the show mixes hitman thriller plotting with cringe-comedy about the acting world, and lets both halves get darker as it goes. Fans can be tested on Barry's crimes, his acting class, and the widening web of people he drags down with him.

A strong Barry quiz can include Barry Berkman's double life as a contract killer, his acting teacher Gene Cousineau, his relationship with Sally Reed, NoHo Hank and the Chechen and Bolivian crime factions, Fuches's manipulation of Barry, the jobs that keep pulling him back in, and the show's final-season time jump.
