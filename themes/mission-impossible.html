  • Which actor plays Owen Davian? Philip Seymour Hoffman

Mission: Impossible trivia works because the franchise has spent decades building elaborate heists, disguises and stunts, giving fans plenty of specific missions, gadgets and betrayals to be tested on across the whole series.

A strong Mission: Impossible quiz can include the stolen NOC list from the first film, Ethan Hunt's IMF team, the self-destructing mission briefings, Luther Stickell's constant presence across the series, and the franchise's practical stunts like the Burj Khalifa climb and helicopter chases. The theme stays engaging because each film centers on a different impossible heist with its own set of rules to break.
