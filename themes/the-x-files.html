Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In 'Little Green Men,' Mulder visits an abandoned radio telescope in which U.S. territory? Puerto Rico
  • In which season is Scully's son William born, in the episode 'Existence'? Season 8
  • What nickname did Scully's Navy-captain father give her? Starbuck
  • What is written on the Cigarette Smoking Man's lighter? Trust No One
  • In 'Never Again,' Scully impulsively gets a tattoo of which symbol? An ouroboros
  • Which of the two lead agents is the believer in the paranormal? Mulder
  • What fictional brand of cigarettes does the show's chain-smoking villain favor? Morley
  • Which agent, played by Robert Patrick, becomes Scully's partner in the later seasons? John Doggett
  • What is the name of Mulder's sister, abducted when they were children? Samantha
  • In 'Bad Blood,' the agents investigate vampires in which Texas town? Chaney

The X-Files trivia works because the series mixes a sprawling conspiracy mythology with standalone case episodes, giving fans both a deep continuity to track and a wide range of individual stories to remember.

A strong X-Files quiz can include Fox Mulder, Dana Scully, the FBI's X-Files division, the Smoking Man, Walter Skinner, the alien colonization mythology, and standout monster-of-the-week episodes. The theme works because it rewards fans who followed the overarching conspiracy as closely as those who remember the show's stand-alone scares.
