  • In 'Small Potatoes,' what is the name of the shape-shifter who impersonates Mulder? Eddie Van Blundht
  • What nickname does Langly go by among the Lone Gunmen? Ringo
  • What is the nickname of the show's shadowy, chain-smoking antagonist? The Cigarette Smoking Man
  • Which mythical piece of pottery do the agents investigate in 'Hollywood A.D.'? The Lazarus Bowl
  • What is written on the Cigarette Smoking Man's lighter? Trust No One

The X-Files trivia works because the series mixes a sprawling conspiracy mythology with standalone case episodes, giving fans both a deep continuity to track and a wide range of individual stories to remember.

A strong X-Files quiz can include Fox Mulder, Dana Scully, the FBI's X-Files division, the Smoking Man, Walter Skinner, the alien colonization mythology, and standout monster-of-the-week episodes. The theme works because it rewards fans who followed the overarching conspiracy as closely as those who remember the show's stand-alone scares.
