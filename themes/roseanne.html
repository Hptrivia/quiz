Roseanne trivia works because the show grounded its comedy in real family life over many seasons. Questions can cover the Conner family, Jackie's many jobs, the family businesses and the big moments from its long run.

A strong Roseanne quiz can include Roseanne and Dan Conner, Becky, Darlene and DJ, Aunt Jackie and her time as a police officer, Wellman Plastics, Rodbell's luncheonette, the Lunch Box and its loose meat sandwiches, Dan's motorcycle shop, Lanford, Illinois, Darlene's goth phase, David Healy, Becky eloping with Mark and the lottery win. It works because fans grew up with the Conners.
