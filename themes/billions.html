Billions trivia works because it pits a Wall Street empire against the prosecutors trying to bring it down, giving fans a long-running power struggle full of legal maneuvering, high finance and personal vendettas to be tested on.

A strong Billions quiz can include Axe Capital's Westport headquarters, Chuck Rhoades' role as U.S. Attorney, Wendy Rhoades' work as the firm's in-house performance coach, Taylor Mason's rise within the firm, and the recurring showdowns between Axelrod and Chuck across the show's insider-trading investigations. The theme stays engaging because it spans courtroom strategy, trading floor drama and the personal cost of the rivalry.
