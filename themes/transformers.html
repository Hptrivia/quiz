Transformers trivia works because the franchise packs in a huge roster of named robots alongside human characters and escalating alien-tech plots, giving fans plenty of factions, artifacts and set pieces to be tested on.

A strong Transformers quiz can include Optimus Prime, Bumblebee, Megatron and Starscream, the Autobots and Decepticons, the AllSpark, human characters like Sam Witwicky and Cade Yeager, and key artifacts and locations across the film series. The theme stays engaging because it spans the original live-action trilogy, its sequels, and the Beast-era prequels.
