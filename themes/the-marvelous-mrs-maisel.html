The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel trivia works because the show follows one woman's fast-talking rise through the stand-up world against a richly detailed late-1950s New York backdrop. Fans can be tested on Midge's routines, her circle of family and comedians, and the milestones of her career.

A strong Mrs. Maisel quiz can include Midge Maisel's rise through the stand-up comedy circuit, her manager Susie Myerson, her parents Abe and Rose Weissman, her ex-husband Joel, comedian Lenny Bruce, the Gordon Ford Show, and the show's period New York setting. It works because it spans Midge's career milestones alongside her tangled family and romantic life.
