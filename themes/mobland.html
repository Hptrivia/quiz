  • In which city is MobLand set? London

MobLand trivia works because the series packs family betrayal, gang warfare and a heavyweight cast into a tight first season. Questions can cover Harry and the Harrigans, the feud with the Stevensons and the deals that go wrong from Antwerp to the Cotswolds.

A strong MobLand quiz can include Harry Da Souza and his wife Jan, Conrad and Maeve Harrigan, Kevin, Brendan and Eddie Harrigan, Bella and Seraphina, Richie and Tommy Stevenson and the Sinful Monkey, the fentanyl known as The Fire, the boxing gym sit-downs, the Cotswolds estate, the Antwerp diamond deal and Jaime Lopez's cartel, retired officer Colin Tattersall and undercover Alice, and Conrad's old nicknames like 100 Guns. It works because the show rewards viewers who kept track of every deal, double-cross and body.
