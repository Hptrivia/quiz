Battlestar Galactica trivia works because the series packs military drama, political intrigue and deep Cylon mythology into four seasons that fans have picked apart for years. Questions can cover the fleet, the pilots and their call signs, the numbered Cylon models and the Final Five.

A strong Battlestar Galactica quiz can include Admiral William Adama, President Laura Roslin, Kara 'Starbuck' Thrace, Lee 'Apollo' Adama, Gaius Baltar, Number Six, Sharon 'Boomer' Valerii and Athena, Helo, Saul Tigh, Tom Zarek and the Astral Queen, Admiral Cain and the Pegasus, tylium fuel, Joe's Bar, the survivor count, New Caprica, Searider Falcon, Doloxan, resurrection technology and the Final Five. It works because the show rewards fans who tracked every Cylon reveal and every call sign.
