Smallville trivia works because it reframes a very familiar hero through a long-running high-school-to-adulthood arc, giving it more character and relationship detail than a typical superhero story.

A strong Smallville quiz can include Clark Kent's origins on Krypton, the Kent family dog Shelby, The WB as the original network, Lana Lang and her nanotech suit storyline, Whitney Fordman, and Checkmate figures like Maxwell Lord. The theme works because it can move between Superman mythology, small-town relationships, and the show's own long-running storylines.
