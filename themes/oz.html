Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which actor plays Chris Keller? Christopher Meloni
  • How is Italian boss Nino Schibetta murdered? Ground glass mixed into his food
  • Which inmate does Cyril defeat in the boxing final, triggering a lockdown over racial unrest? Hamid Khan
  • Vern Schillinger is the leader of which group? The Aryans
  • Which inmate fatally shoots Kareem Said? Lemuel Idzik
  • Which infamous prison riot does McManus cite from his hometown as his inspiration? Attica
  • In the boxing tournament, O'Reily drugs Cyril's opponents with which sedative? Chloral hydrate
  • What crime is Kareem Said convicted of? Arson
  • What state is Oswald State Penitentiary located in? New York
  • When inmates are moved to general population, which lettered unit does McManus transfer them to? Unit B

Oz trivia works because six seasons inside Oswald State packed in gangs, guards, schemes and deaths at a pace no other prison drama matched. Questions can cover Emerald City, the rival factions and the narrator who framed every episode.

A strong Oz quiz can include Oswald State Correctional Facility and the Emerald City experiment, Augustus Hill and his narration, Tobias Beecher and Vern Schillinger, Chris Keller, Ryan O'Reily and Simon Adebisi, Kareem Said and the Muslims, Miguel Alvarez and El Cid, Bob Rebadow, Burr Redding and Enrique Morales, Warden Leo Glynn, Tim McManus, Sister Peter Marie and Father Ray Mukada, the Aryans, Italians, Latinos, Homeboys and Bikers, the riot that closes the first season, and the executions, overdoses and hits that thin the cast. It works because the show rewards viewers who tracked every alliance and every body.
