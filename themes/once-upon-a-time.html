  • In what fictional seaside town is most of Once Upon a Time set? Storybrooke
  • Which television network originally aired Once Upon a Time? ABC
  • Belle becomes Rumplestiltskin's servant as part of a deal to protect her father's kingdom from what threat? Ogres
  • Which fairy-tale figure is Sheriff Graham in the Enchanted Forest? The Huntsman
  • In which U.S. state is Storybrooke located? Maine

  • What special ingredient does Henry Mills like in his hot cocoa? Cinnamon
  • What public office does Regina Mills hold in Storybrooke? Mayor
  • How much money went 'missing' from the budget for the new Storybrooke playground? $50,000

Once Upon a Time trivia works because it reimagines familiar fairy-tale characters with new identities and alliances, giving fans plenty of ground to cover between the Enchanted Forest backstories and the Storybrooke present.

A strong Once Upon a Time quiz can include Emma Swan, Henry Mills, Regina Mills as the Evil Queen, Snow White and Prince Charming as Mary Margaret and David, Rumplestiltskin as Mr. Gold, Captain Hook, Storybrooke and the Enchanted Forest, and artifacts like Excalibur and Pandora's Box. The theme stays engaging because every character carries two identities and a web of crossed fairy-tale storylines to track.
