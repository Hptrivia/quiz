Landman trivia works because the show packs every episode with oil-field detail, sharp dialogue and family drama. Questions can cover M-Tex Oil, Tommy's crew, his complicated family and the deals and disasters that drive the story.

A strong Landman quiz can include Tommy Norris, Angela and Ainsley, Cooper and his six wells, Monty Miller, Cami Miller, Ariana, Rebecca and her $900 hourly rate, Nate, Dale, Gallino and the cartel, the gas leak, the hurricane settlement, TCU cheerleading, Hot Pockets in the pilot, the Will Rogers Memorial Center and the new company at the end of Season 2. It works because fans remember every big number and every one-liner.
