Grimm trivia works because the series built a detailed world of German-named Wesen, royal families and secret orders around a police procedural. Questions can cover Nick's cases, his Wesen friends and the lore found in his aunt's trailer.

A strong Grimm quiz can include Nick Burkhardt and his partner Hank, Sergeant Wu, Monroe the Blutbad clockmaker, Rosalee the Fuchsbau and her spice shop, Juliette and Eve, Adalind the Hexenbiest, Captain Renard the half Zauberbiest, the woge, the Verrat and the Royals, Black Claw, the Wesenrein, Diana, the Keys and the staff, and Wesen like the Eisbiber and Siegbarste. It works because fans who learned every creature name can be tested on lore as well as plot.

If you enjoy Grimm trivia, also try Shameless and The Handmaid's Tale on Trivia Gauntlet.
