White Collar trivia works because it combines a slick con-artist lead, an unlikely FBI partnership, and a rotating cast of heists and schemes that give the show a lot of memorable detail to draw questions from.

A strong White Collar quiz can include Neal Caffrey, his partner Mozzie, FBI agents Peter Burke, Jones and Diana, series creator Jeff Eastin, and storylines like Neal's run against the Pink Panthers crime syndicate. The theme works because it can move between con schemes, FBI procedure, character relationships, and the show's ongoing tension between crime and redemption.
