Hunter x Hunter trivia works because the series has clear rules, Nen powers, and distinct arcs, so every fight and exam has a detail worth remembering. Fans can be tested on characters, abilities, and the arcs that define the story.

A strong Hunter x Hunter quiz can include Gon Freecss, Killua, Kurapika, Leorio, Hisoka, the Phantom Troupe, Nen and its Hatsu principle, the Hunter Exam, Whale Island, Yorknew City, Greed Island, the Chimera Ants, Meruem, Neferpitou, and NGL. It works because its systems and arcs are detailed enough to build many distinct questions.
