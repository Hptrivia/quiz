Bob's Burgers trivia works because the show surrounds one small family restaurant with a whole town of oddball regulars, rivals and running jokes. Questions can cover the Belchers, the neighbours on Ocean Avenue and the punny Burger of the Day board.

A strong Bob's Burgers quiz can include Bob and Linda Belcher, Tina and her friend fiction, horses and Jimmy Jr., Gene and his keyboard, Louise and her bunny ears and Kuchi Kopi, Teddy the handyman, Jimmy Pesto's Pizzeria across the street, Mr. Fischoeder and Wonder Wharf, Mr. Frond at Wagstaff School, Aunt Gayle and Mr. Business, Boyz 4 Now and The Equestranauts, the Thundergirls, the Burger of the Day chalkboard and The Bob's Burgers Movie. It works because long-time viewers know every neighbour, every song and every pun on the board.

If you enjoy Bob's Burgers trivia, also try New Girl and Parks and Recreation on Trivia Gauntlet.
