The Hawk trivia works because its first season packs a full comeback season of golf into ten episodes, from the Korn Ferry Tour to the PGA Tour, with a father-son rivalry driving every round. Questions can cover Lonnie and Lance, the tournaments and the running jokes fans picked up immediately.

A strong The Hawk quiz can include Lonnie Hawkins and Will Ferrell, Lance Hawkins and Jimmy Tatro, co-creators Harper Steele and Chris Henchy, Old Henry and his memorial, Stacy and the Teed Off drink, the Korn Ferry Tour run and the PGA Tour card, the W.H. Rawlings Tire Invitational, the OccuNet Classic and the Visit Knoxville Open, sponsors, caddies and rivals like Hans Korsguaard and Gustavo Meyer, the 2010 near-Slam and the 2009 Player of the Year season, and the road-trip detours that fill the middle of the season. It works because fans who binged the first season remember every bet, brand and bad decision.
