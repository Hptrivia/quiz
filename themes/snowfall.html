Snowfall trivia works because six seasons trace a tangled web of dealers, cartels and CIA operatives across Los Angeles, Mexico and Central America. Questions can cover Franklin and his family, Teddy McDonald's operation and the enemies they made along the way.

A strong Snowfall quiz can include Franklin Saint and his mother Cissy, Uncle Jerome and Aunt Louie, Leon Simmons and Wanda, Kevin Hamilton, Gustavo 'El Oso' Zapata and Lucia Villanueva, Teddy McDonald and the CIA's Contra operation, Avi Drexler, Claudia Crane, Andre Wright, Skully and the Inglewood Bloods, the KGB's Ruben, Kane Hamilton, the South Central streets of 1983 and Teddy's final betrayal. It works because viewers who followed the full six seasons can trace every deal and every death.
