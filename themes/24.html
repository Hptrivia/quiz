Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which character, played by Shohreh Aghdashloo, is a terrorist matriarch in Season 4? Dina Araz
  • In the early seasons, CTU headquarters is located in which city? Los Angeles
  • Who plays President Wayne Palmer? D.B. Woodside
  • Season 8 relocates the main action to which city? New York
  • In Season 6, a nuclear device detonates in which Los Angeles suburb? Valencia
  • What is Chloe O'Brian's profession at CTU? Computer analyst
  • 24 won the Emmy for Outstanding Drama Series for which of its seasons? Season 5
  • What is the title of the TV movie set in Africa that bridges Seasons 6 and 7? 24: Redemption
  • Which CTU official does Jack execute on Stephen Saunders' orders in Season 3? Ryan Chappelle
  • Who is the main terrorist antagonist of 24: Legacy? Jadalla Bin-Khalid

24 trivia works because every season is a single day told in real time, so the plots, betrayals and body counts stack up fast and stick in the memory. Questions can cover Jack Bauer, CTU, the Palmer administration and the mastermind behind each crisis.

A strong 24 quiz can include Jack Bauer and Kiefer Sutherland, CTU Los Angeles and its directors from Tony Almeida and Bill Buchanan to Chloe O Brian at the keyboard, the Palmer brothers David and Wayne and the presidents who follow them, season antagonists like Victor Drazen, Stephen Saunders, Habib Marwan and the Araz family, set pieces such as the Chandler Plaza virus, the Override device and Operation Nightfall, plus the spin-offs 24: Live Another Day and 24: Legacy and the creators Joel Surnow and Robert Cochran. It works because fans who tracked the clock remember exactly which hour everything went wrong.
