Riverdale trivia works because seven seasons layered comic-book roots onto murder mysteries, gangs and increasingly wild twists, giving fans a huge cast of characters and storylines to track. Questions can cover Archie and his friends, the town's darkest secrets, and the mysteries that kicked off each season.

A strong Riverdale quiz can include Archie Andrews and his father Fred, Betty Cooper and her sister Polly, Veronica Lodge and her father Hiram, Jughead Jones (real name Forsythe) and his father FP, Pop Tate and Pop's Chock'lit Shoppe, the neighboring town of Greendale, Cheryl Blossom and the Blossom family, the Southside Serpents, and the source material in Archie Comics. It works because fans who watched every season know exactly which murder, gang war or twist reshaped Riverdale.

If you enjoy Riverdale trivia, also try Stranger Things and Pretty Little Liars on Trivia Gauntlet.
