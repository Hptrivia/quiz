Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In what year is Christine mainly set, when Arnie buys the car? 1978
  • What profession does the adult Ben Hanscom grow up to have in It? Architect
  • What official body investigates the events of Carrie afterward? The White Commission
  • John Coffey secretly heals the warden's wife of what ailment in The Green Mile? A brain tumor
  • What single word does King repeatedly use for how the past resists change in 11/22/63? Obdurate
  • What cosmic ritual do the Losers use to battle It on the astral plane? Ritual of Chud
  • What malevolent creature is said to haunt the burial ground in Pet Sematary? The Wendigo
  • How many years pass before the Losers reunite in Derry in It? 27
  • The Shawshank story first appeared in which Stephen King collection? Different Seasons
  • What hobby is the Losers' Club member Stan Uris known for in It? Birdwatching

Stephen King trivia works because his novels share a recurring universe of Maine towns and recurring lore while each book offers its own distinct cast, villain and setting, giving fans a huge range of specific details to be tested on.

A strong Stephen King quiz can include Jack Torrance and the Overlook Hotel, Pennywise and the Losers' Club of Derry, Annie Wilkes and Paul Sheldon, Cujo and the Camber family, Christine and Arnie Cunningham, Carrie White, John Coffey and the Green Mile, Mother Abagail and the superflu of The Stand, and Roland Deschain's quest in The Dark Tower. The theme works because it rewards deep familiarity with King's recurring towns, characters and cross-referenced lore.
