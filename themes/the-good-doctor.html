The Good Doctor trivia works because it combines detailed medical-case storylines with a close-knit hospital cast, giving fans plenty of specific procedures, relationships and season arcs to be quizzed on.

A strong Good Doctor quiz can include Shaun Murphy, Aaron Glassman, Lea Dilallo, Neil Melendez, Claire Browne and Alex Park, St. Bonaventure Hospital, Shaun's savant syndrome, and the show's roots as a remake of a South Korean drama. The theme stays engaging because it spans both Shaun's personal growth and the hospital's changing cast and leadership.
