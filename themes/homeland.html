Homeland trivia works because eight seasons of CIA operations moved from suburban Virginia to Beirut, Berlin, Islamabad and Kabul, giving fans a long chain of assets, handlers and double-crosses to keep straight. Questions can cover Carrie and Brody, Saul and Quinn, and the operations that defined each season.

A strong Homeland quiz can include Carrie Mathison and Claire Danes, Nicholas Brody and Damian Lewis, Saul Berenson and Mandy Patinkin, Peter Quinn and Rupert Friend, Abu Nazir and the plot that opens the series, Carrie's bipolar disorder and the jazz and clozapine that go with it, David Estes, Dar Adal and the politics inside Langley, the Beirut, Berlin, Islamabad and Kabul postings, Aayan, Haqqani, Yevgeny Gromov and Russian tradecraft, Dana and Jessica Brody, and the marine-turned-congressman arc that runs through the first three seasons. It works because the show rewards viewers who tracked every asset, alias and betrayal.
