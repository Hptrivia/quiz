Frasier trivia works because the series built one of TV's most quotable ensembles around a simple premise, giving fans a deep well of running jokes, family history, and radio-show moments to draw from.

A strong Frasier quiz can include Frasier Crane, Niles Crane, Martin Crane, Daphne Moon, Roz Doyle, the dog Eddie, KACL radio, the Crane apartment, Niles and Daphne's slow-burn romance, Frasier's ex-wife Lilith, and the show's roots as a Cheers spinoff. The theme works well because it rewards fans who remember both the sharp one-liners and the season-long arcs.
