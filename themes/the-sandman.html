Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What is Bernie Capax's profession? Lawyer
  • In which city is John Dee held in a psychiatric institution? Buffalo
  • What does Bette, the diner waitress, aspire to be? A writer
  • Who plays the Corinthian? Boyd Holbrook
  • To which town does Rosemary drive John Dee to collect his belongings? Mayhew
  • What title does the woman married to the possessed Kevin Brody hold? Princess
  • Who plays the new Dream after Morpheus dies? Jacob Anderson
  • In which year does Dream have Shakespeare's company perform for the King and Queen of Faerie? 1593
  • How often does Death spend a day living as a mortal? Every hundred years
  • What is Mervyn Pumpkinhead's job in the Dreaming? Janitor

The Sandman trivia works because its world is full of myths, strange creatures and unforgettable stories. Questions can cover Dream's escape, his lost tools, the residents of the Dreaming and the Endless family.

A strong Sandman quiz can include Dream imprisoned in a glass sphere by Roderick Burgess in 1916, the Order of Ancient Mysteries and Fawney Rig, Alex Burgess and his punishment, Dream's helm in Hell, his pouch and ruby, Lucienne the librarian, Matthew the raven, Goldie the gargoyle, Johanna Constantine the exorcist, John Dee, Death, Desire and the Corinthian. It works because every episode feels like its own tale.
