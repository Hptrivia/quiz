Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • How many autonomous communities make up Spain? 17
  • What is the highest mountain in Spain? Teide
  • Which strait separates southern Spain from Morocco? Strait of Gibraltar
  • In which town is the tomato-throwing festival La Tomatina held? Buñol
  • Mount Teide stands on which Spanish island? Tenerife
  • Which tiny country sits between Spain and France in the Pyrenees? Andorra
  • Which mountain range forms the border between Spain and France? Pyrenees
  • On which date do Spanish children traditionally receive gifts from the Three Kings? 6 January
  • What is the Spanish tradition of an afternoon rest called? Siesta
  • What is the brand name of Spain's high-speed rail service, launched between Madrid and Seville in 1992? AVE

Spain trivia works well because it can move easily between Madrid and its regions, art and history, food culture, and modern sport without feeling repetitive. It gives you more range than a narrow capitals-only or flag-only country quiz.

A strong Spain quiz can draw from Madrid, Pablo Picasso's Guernica, the Euro 2024 win over England, the Alborán Sea, Cava from Catalonia, the 1212 Battle of Las Navas de Tolosa, and regional dishes like cocido madrileño. That mix helps the theme feel more complete, because players are not only recalling geography but also wider cultural and historical knowledge tied to the country.
