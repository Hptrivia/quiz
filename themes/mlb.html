MLB trivia works because baseball fans love records, nicknames and history, and the game has more than a century of iconic moments. Questions can range from well-known legends to statistics and deeper franchise history.

A strong MLB quiz can include Babe Ruth, Lou Gehrig the Iron Horse, Joe DiMaggio, Jackie Robinson and number 42, Nolan Ryan's no-hitters, Barry Bonds, David Ortiz, Mike Trout, Shohei Ohtani and the 50-50 club, the Yankees' World Series titles, the Cy Young Award, the Home Run Derby, the pitch clock, Kirk Gibson's walk-off and Don Larsen's perfect game. It works because baseball's records and history are endlessly quizzable.
