Tell Me Lies trivia works because its twisty timelines, secrets and betrayals keep fans hooked. Questions can cover Lucy and Stephen, their friend group, Macy's death and the fallout that follows.

A strong Tell Me Lies quiz can include Lucy Albright and Stephen DeMarco, Pippa, Bree and Evan, Wrigley the football player, Diana and her choice of Stanford, Macy's car crash, Evan's birthday at the lake house and the Canoe Olympics, the Hawaiian end-of-year party, Stephen's LSAT panic attack and Yale acceptance, Lucy's LiveJournal story, CJ and Lydia in the 2015 timeline. It works because every lie catches up with someone.
