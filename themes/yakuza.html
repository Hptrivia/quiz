Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What fictional Tokyo entertainment district is the series primarily set in? Kamurocho
  • Completing the many optional objectives fills what in-game checklist? The Completion List
  • What is Kiryu's undercover job in Fukuoka in Yakuza 5? Taxi driver
  • What kind of nightlife business can players manage in Yakuza 0? A cabaret club
  • Which traditional hanafuda card game can be played in the series? Koi-Koi and Oicho-Kabu
  • What are the powerful special attacks in Yakuza combat called? Heat Actions
  • Which eye did Majima lose, hidden by his eyepatch? His left eye
  • Which historical spinoff, set during the Bakumatsu period, got a Western remake in 2023? Like a Dragon: Ishin!
  • What underground area beneath Kamurocho recurs as a hub for illegal activity? Purgatory
  • Which information-broker character aids Kiryu across several games? The Florist

Yakuza trivia works because the series blends a serious crime saga with over-the-top brawls, substories and the neon streets of Kamurocho. Fans can be tested on the characters, the story and the world across Kiryu's saga and Like a Dragon.

A strong Yakuza quiz can include Kazuma Kiryu the Dragon of Dojima, Goro Majima, Ichiban Kasuga and the shift to turn-based combat, the Tojo Clan and rival families, Kamurocho and Sotenbori, the substories and minigames, Haruka and Kiryu's found family, the heat actions and fighting styles, and the twists across the mainline games and Kiwami remakes. It works because it rewards fans who walked every alley of Kamurocho.
