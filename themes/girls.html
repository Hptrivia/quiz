Girls trivia works because the show is full of awkward moments, messy relationships and quotable disasters. Questions can cover Hannah's writing career, the four friends, the men in their lives and life in Brooklyn.

A strong Girls quiz can include Hannah Horvath from East Lansing and her parents cutting her off, Marnie, Jessa and Shoshanna, Adam Sackler, Ray and Grumpy's coffee shop, Elijah, Charlie and his app, Jessa's surprise wedding to Thomas-John, Hannah's Greenpoint apartment, the opium tea in the pilot, Oberlin College and the season 1 finale wedding cake on the beach. It works because fans remember every cringe-worthy moment.
