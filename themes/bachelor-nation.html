Bachelor Nation trivia works because more than two decades of roses, proposals and dramatic exits have produced a huge cast of leads and contestants fans still argue about. Questions can cover who won each season, what the leads did for a living and where the journeys ended.

A strong Bachelor Nation quiz can include Alex Michel and Trista Rehn as the very first leads, Chris Harrison and Jesse Palmer as hosts, the Bachelor Mansion in Agoura Hills, Neil Lane rings and After the Final Rose, hometown dates and Fantasy Suite week, The Men Tell All, Colton Underwood jumping the fence, Juan Pablo's signature line, Peter Weber the pilot, Corinne and her nanny Raquel, Chad and his deli meat, and the recent seasons of Zach Shallcross, Joey Graziadei, Charity Lawson and Jenn Tran. It works because fans remember every lead, every villain and every exotic final destination.

If you enjoy Bachelor Nation trivia, also try Survivor: New Era and Twin Peaks on Trivia Gauntlet.
