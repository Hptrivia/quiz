Dune trivia works because its desert world is packed with houses, prophecies and factions that fans love to argue about. Questions can cover Paul Atreides and Lady Jessica, the Fremen of Arrakis, the spice trade and the films that brought it all to screen.

A strong Dune quiz can include House Atreides and House Harkonnen, Paul's journey from Caladan to the sietches of Arrakis, Lady Jessica and the Bene Gesserit with the Litany Against Fear, Chani and the Fremen and their terms like Lisan al-Gaib and Eyes of Ibad, sandworms and spice harvesting, the Spacing Guild and the Emperor's Sardaukar, ornithopters and crysknives, and the cast and filming of the Denis Villeneuve films. It works because it rewards fans of both the novels and the movies.
