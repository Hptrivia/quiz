Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What is the maximum length a sandworm can reach, according to the Dune documentary narration? 400 meters
  • What reason does Dr. Yueh give for betraying House Atreides? His captive wife
  • What item does Paul display to prove he is the Duke of Arrakis before the assembled Fremen? Ducal signet ring
  • What imperial year is recorded in Princess Irulan's diary at the start of Dune: Part Two? 10,191
  • What is the private Fremen name given to Paul within Sietch Tabr? Usul
  • What Fremen ritual combat rule forbids yielding and ends only in death? Amtal rule
  • How many crew members are aboard the stricken spice crawler Delta Ajax 9? 21
  • Who plays Baron Vladimir Harkonnen? Stellan Skarsgård
  • How many years did the Harkonnens control Arrakis before the Emperor reassigned it to House Atreides? 80 years
  • What does the Fremen name 'Sihaya' mean? Desert Spring

Dune trivia works because its desert world is packed with houses, prophecies and factions that fans love to argue about. Questions can cover Paul Atreides and Lady Jessica, the Fremen of Arrakis, the spice trade and the films that brought it all to screen.

A strong Dune quiz can include House Atreides and House Harkonnen, Paul's journey from Caladan to the sietches of Arrakis, Lady Jessica and the Bene Gesserit with the Litany Against Fear, Chani and the Fremen and their terms like Lisan al-Gaib and Eyes of Ibad, sandworms and spice harvesting, the Spacing Guild and the Emperor's Sardaukar, ornithopters and crysknives, and the cast and filming of the Denis Villeneuve films. It works because it rewards fans of both the novels and the movies.
