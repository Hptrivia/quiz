College football trivia works because the sport is built on tradition, with rivalries, mascots and stadiums fans care about as much as the results. The recent playoff era and realignment add plenty of new questions alongside the history.

A strong college football quiz can include Nick Saban's seven titles, Kirby Smart's Georgia, Joe Paterno at Penn State, the Horseshoe, Kyle Field and Reveille, Howard's Rock at Clemson, the Egg Bowl and the Old Oaken Bucket, Bo Jackson and Charles Woodson winning the Heisman, Rutgers beating Princeton in 1869, Texas and Oklahoma joining the SEC, the Pac-12 collapse, and Indiana's unbeaten 2025 season with Fernando Mendoza. It works because fans follow both the history and the chaos.
