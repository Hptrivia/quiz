King of the Hill trivia works because the show's humor comes from small, specific details about its characters and their small Texas town. Questions can cover Strickland Propane, the alley crew, the Hill family's history and the Hulu revival.

A strong King of the Hill quiz can include Hank and 'propane and propane accessories', Strickland Propane, Ladybird the bloodhound, Peggy the Boggle champion, Bobby the prop comic, Dale Gribble and Rusty Shackleford, Bill Dauterive the Billdozer, Boomhauer, Luanne and the Manger Babies, Lucky, Cotton Hill and his lost shins, John Redcorn, Kahn and the Souphanousinphones, Mega Lo Mart, Alamo beer and the revival's adult Bobby. It works because fans love the show's deep-cut details.
