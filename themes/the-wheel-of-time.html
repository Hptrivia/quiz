The Wheel of Time trivia works because the series builds an intricate fantasy world with deep lore, giving fans plenty of characters, factions, and world-building details to be tested on.

A strong Wheel of Time quiz can include Moiraine Damodred, Rand al'Thor, Mat Cauthon, Perrin Aybara, Egwene al'Vere, Nynaeve al'Meara, the Aes Sedai, the Two Rivers, the Eelfinn, and channeling and Warders. The theme stays rich because it can test both the show's central mystery and the deep fantasy lore built out across seasons.
