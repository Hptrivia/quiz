South Park trivia works because more than twenty-five seasons of satire have produced a massive cast of townspeople, alter egos and unforgettable episodes. Questions can cover the four boys, their families and the running gags fans still quote.

A strong South Park quiz can include Stan Marsh, Kyle Broflovski, Eric Cartman and Kenny McCormick, Butters and Professor Chaos, Randy Marsh and Tegridy Farms, Mr. Garrison and Mr. Hat, Mr. Mackey, Chef, Towelie and Mr. Hankey, Terrance and Phillip, Cheesy Poofs and Casa Bonita, the Coon and Friends, ManBearPig, the Underpants Gnomes, Scott Tenorman, Imaginationland, the World of Warcraft episode and South Park: Bigger, Longer & Uncut. It works because fans can quote decades of episodes line for line.
