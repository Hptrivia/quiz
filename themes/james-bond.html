Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What hand does Le Chiffre hold when he loses the final round to Bond's in Casino Royale? A full house
  • What government body absorbs MI6 and MI5 under C's plan in Spectre? Joint Intelligence Service
  • In which country does the high-stakes poker tournament in Casino Royale take place? Montenegro
  • How much is Bond paid for killing Patrice, according to the Macau casino payout? €4 million
  • What item does Bond drop in the snow at the end of the film to symbolize letting go of Vesper? Her necklace
  • What is the name of the shadowy organization Bond investigates in Quantum of Solace? Quantum
  • What winning poker hand does Bond hold in the decisive final round of Casino Royale? A straight flush
  • In which Italian city does the opening car chase end in Quantum of Solace? Siena
  • What government body holds a public inquiry into M's handling of the stolen hard drive i Skyfall? Intelligence and Security Committee
  • In which London prison is Blofeld held during the events of No Time to Die? Belmarsh

James Bond trivia works because the franchise spans over sixty years of films, giving fans a huge spread of villains, gadgets, theme songs and plot twists to be tested on across every era of 007.

A strong James Bond quiz can include Bond, M, Q, Moneypenny, villains like Blofeld and Safin, allies like Vesper Lynd and Felix Leiter, MI6, iconic gadgets, and plot beats from Casino Royale through No Time to Die. The theme stays engaging because it can move between classic Cold War-era Bond and the modern Daniel Craig films.
