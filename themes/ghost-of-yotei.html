Ghost of Yotei trivia works because Atsu's revenge story is told through a clear list of targets, each with their own region, weapon and story. Questions can cover the Yotei Six, the weapons Atsu learns and the game's side quests and world.

A strong Ghost of Yotei quiz can include Atsu and the Night of the Burning Tree, the sash bearing the names of the Yotei Six, Saito, the Kitsune, the Spider and the Dragon, the Oni and Ishikari Castle, her father Kengo the swordsmith and the Wolf Blade, Jubei and the Matsumae clan, the dual katana, odachi and kusarigama, the shamisen, Ainu traditions, onsens and the Mythic Tales. It works because the game rewards fans who explored Ezo fully.
