Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which Guilty Remnant member is murdered by masked assailants early in Season 1? Gladys
  • What color of clothing do members of the Guilty Remnant always wear? White
  • On what date does the Sudden Departure take place? October 14, 2011
  • What is the name of Kevin Garvey's teenage daughter? Jill
  • How long after the Departure does the main story begin? Three years
  • What was Laurie Garvey's profession before she joined the cult? Therapist
  • What is Matt Jamison's profession? Minister
  • How old was Nora Durst when her parents were killed? 7
  • The cast of which 1980s sitcom is said to have almost entirely departed? Perfect Strangers
  • In the Season 2 prehistoric prologue, what fatally bites the cavewoman? A rattlesnake

The Leftovers trivia works because three seasons built a whole world out of one unexplained event, moving from Mapleton to Jarden to the Australian outback. Questions can cover the Sudden Departure, the Guilty Remnant and the strange rules each new place plays by.

A strong The Leftovers quiz can include the Sudden Departure on October 14 and the two percent who vanished, Kevin Garvey and Justin Theroux, Nora Durst and Carrie Coon, Laurie, Jill and Tom Garvey, Matt Jamison and his gospel, Patti Levin and the white-clad Guilty Remnant, Holy Wayne and his hugs, the move to Jarden Texas and its Miracle nickname, the Murphys and Regina King, the Department of Sudden Departure, the purgatory hotel of International Assassin, the Australian season and the ferry cult, plus the machine, the flood and the ending Nora describes. It works because fans who watched every episode know which mystery the show answered and which it deliberately left alone.
