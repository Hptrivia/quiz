Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In the trilogy's epilogue, how many children do Katniss and Peeta have? Two
  • What substance does Haymitch frequently abuse? Alcohol
  • Which best friend of young Snow in the prequel came from District 2 before moving to the Capitol? Sejanus Plinth
  • How is Head Gamemaker Seneca Crane executed after the first Games? Locked in a room with nightlock berries
  • Which numbered Hunger Games serves as the setting for the prequel? The 10th
  • How did Katniss's father die? A mine explosion
  • What prestigious prize does Coriolanus Snow hope to win at the Academy in the prequel? The Plinth Prize
  • Who directed Catching Fire and both Mockingjay films? Francis Lawrence
  • Which actress plays President Alma Coin of District 13? Julianne Moore
  • Who commands Katniss's squad in Mockingjay, played by Mahershala Ali? Boggs

The Hunger Games trivia works because Panem is full of tributes, districts and arena twists that fans know by heart. Questions can cover Katniss and Peeta, the Gamemakers and the Capitol, and the rebellion that runs through the whole series.

A strong Hunger Games quiz can include Katniss Everdeen and the reaping of District 12, Peeta Mellark and their training scores, Haymitch and Effie, Cinna and the flaming costumes, the arena traps and the muttations, careers and victors like Finnick, Johanna, Beetee and Wiress, the Quarter Quell in Catching Fire, District 13 and Squad 451 in Mockingjay, Presidents Snow and Coin, the prequel era of The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, and the cast and directors of the films. It works because it rewards fans of both the books and the movies.
