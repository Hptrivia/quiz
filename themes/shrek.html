Shrek trivia works because the films are stuffed with fairytale parodies, running jokes and quotable moments that fans still repeat decades later. Questions can cover the swamp, Lord Farquaad and Duloc, Princess Fiona's curse, Far Far Away and the Fairy Godmother.

A strong Shrek quiz can include Shrek, Donkey, Princess Fiona, Lord Farquaad and Duloc, the Magic Mirror, the Gingerbread Man, the Big Bad Wolf and the fairytale refugees, Dragon, Puss in Boots, Far Far Away, the King and Queen, the Fairy Godmother and Prince Charming, Artie and Merlin, Rumpelstiltskin, plus the voice cast, the soundtrack choices and the sequels that expanded the world. It works because the surface jokes are famous while the specific names, places and plot beats separate real fans from casual viewers.
