Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which sea lies off South Korea's west coast? Yellow Sea
  • Which Gyeongju temple houses the Dabotap and Seokgatap stone pagodas? Bulguksa
  • Roughly how many people live in South Korea? About 52 million
  • Which Seoul palace was the main royal residence of the Joseon dynasty? Gyeongbokgung
  • What is South Korea's legislature called? National Assembly
  • Which South Korean film won the Academy Award for Best Picture? Parasite
  • Which K-pop girl group counts Jennie, Lisa, Jisoo and Rosé among its members? Blackpink
  • Which country ruled Korea as a colony from 1910 to 1945? Japan
  • Which Korean Netflix series follows debt-ridden contestants playing deadly children's games? Squid Game
  • Which island off the southern coast is South Korea's largest and its most popular holiday destination? Jeju

South Korea trivia works well because it can move easily between the Korean War and the DMZ, K-pop and modern pop culture, and older history and landmarks without feeling repetitive. It gives you more range than a narrow capitals-only or flag-only country quiz.

A strong South Korea quiz can draw from the border with North Korea and the Demilitarized Zone, the 1953 armistice, K-pop acts like BTS, ondol underfloor heating, temples such as Bulguksa, the April Revolution, and the 2002 World Cup run. That mix helps the theme feel more complete, because players are not only recalling geography but also wider cultural and historical knowledge tied to the country.
