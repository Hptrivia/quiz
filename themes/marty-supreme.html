Marty Supreme trivia works because Josh Safdie's table tennis drama packs a huge amount of detail into Marty Mauser's hustle, from the shoe shop and the Harlem halls to the British Open and the world stage. Questions can cover his rivals, his backers and every match that defines the run.

A strong Marty Supreme quiz can include Marty Mauser and his relentless pursuit of greatness, Kay Stone, Rachel, Wally, Murray, Milton and Mishkin, the rival Kletzki and the Rockwell name, the shoe shop job and the Harlem scene, the British Open in London and the world championship in Tokyo, grips and playing styles from penholder to chopper, plus Timothee Chalamet's performance, Josh Safdie's direction and the 1950s New York setting that frames the whole film. It works because the film rewards viewers who tracked the names, scores and deals behind the matches.
