Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • In 'Smithereens,' Chris demands to speak with the founder of which social-media company? Smithereen
  • In the Season 7 episode 'Common People,' what is the subscription service that keeps Amanda alive called? Rivermind
  • In 'Demon 79,' what is the name of the demon who tasks Nida with the killings? Gaap
  • What is the first name of the rating-obsessed woman at the center of 'Nosedive'? Lacie
  • Which technology company is behind the simulation in 'San Junipero'? TCKR Systems
  • In 'Hotel Reverie,' what is the name of the technology that inserts a modern star into a classic film? Redream
  • In 'San Junipero,' what is the name of the woman Yorkie falls in love with? Kelly
  • In 'Men Against Fire,' what is the name of the neural implant that alters soldiers' perception? MASS
  • In 'Striking Vipers,' on which occasion do Danny and Karl agree to reunite in the game each year? Danny's birthday
  • In which year did the festive special 'White Christmas' first air? 2014

Black Mirror trivia works because each episode is its own self-contained story, giving fans a wide range of distinct plots, technologies, and endings to be quizzed on rather than one continuous narrative.

A strong Black Mirror quiz can include episodes like San Junipero, White Christmas, Bandersnatch, USS Callister, Nosedive, and The National Anthem, creator Charlie Brooker's recurring themes of technology and society, and the twist endings that define the show's reputation. The theme stays engaging because every episode offers its own distinct set of details to draw questions from.
