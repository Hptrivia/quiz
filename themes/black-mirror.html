Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which actor plays Nanette Cole in 'USS Callister'? Cristin Milioti
  • In 'Hated in the Nation,' the deadly autonomous drones are disguised as which insect? Bees
  • What does the twist ending of 'Mazey Day' reveal the troubled actress to be? A werewolf
  • In 'The National Anthem,' how many people are said to watch the Prime Minister's broadcast? 1.3 billion
  • Which actor plays Robert Daly in 'USS Callister'? Jesse Plemons
  • In 'Demon 79,' how many people must Nida kill to prevent the apocalypse? Three
  • In 'Arkangel,' Sara ultimately attacks her mother with which object? A tablet
  • In 'Nosedive,' Lacie tries to raise her score in order to attend whose wedding? A childhood friend's
  • In 'Hang the DJ,' the System claims to pair people with what success rate? 99.8%
  • In 'Fifteen Million Merits,' how do people earn the currency called merits? By pedaling exercise bikes

Black Mirror trivia works because each episode is its own self-contained story, giving fans a wide range of distinct plots, technologies, and endings to be quizzed on rather than one continuous narrative.

A strong Black Mirror quiz can include episodes like San Junipero, White Christmas, Bandersnatch, USS Callister, Nosedive, and The National Anthem, creator Charlie Brooker's recurring themes of technology and society, and the twist endings that define the show's reputation. The theme stays engaging because every episode offers its own distinct set of details to draw questions from.
