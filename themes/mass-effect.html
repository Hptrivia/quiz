  • What is the name of the human ark ship carrying the Ryder family in Andromeda? The Hyperion
  • What is the alias of the mysterious crime lord secretly running Kadara's underworld? The Charlatan
  • What is Shepard's rebuilt ship called in Mass Effect 2? Normandy SR-2
  • In Mass Effect 3, which planet do the Reapers attack at the very start of the game? Earth
  • What is the name of the krogan sterility plague central to Mass Effect 3's story? The Genophage
  • Which corporation runs the colony on Feros and secretly studies the Thorian? ExoGeni
  • What title is given to the main character in Mass Effect: Andromeda? Pathfinder
  • On which human colony is a Prothean beacon discovered early in the first game? Eden Prime
  • What is the name of the ancient Prothean world where Shepard finds the VI named Vigil? Ilos
  • What extinct ancient species left behind the beacons and technology studied in the trilogy? Protheans

Mass Effect trivia works because the trilogy is built on squadmates, loyalty missions and choices that fans still debate. Questions can cover Commander Shepard and the Normandy, the alien species of the Citadel and the fight against the Reapers.

A strong Mass Effect quiz can include Commander Shepard and the SSV Normandy, squadmates from Garrus and Tali to Liara, Wrex, Miranda and Zaeed, Saren and Sovereign in the first game, the Collectors and the suicide mission in Mass Effect 2, the genophage cure and the Krogan on Tuchanka, the Rachni and Prothean history, the Citadel Council and alien homeworlds like Palaven and Thessia, the endings of Mass Effect 3, and the Andromeda spin-off with its Heleus Cluster and strike teams. It works because it rewards fans who remember every dialogue choice.
