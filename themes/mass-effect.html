Mass Effect trivia works because the trilogy is built on squadmates, loyalty missions and choices that fans still debate. Questions can cover Commander Shepard and the Normandy, the alien species of the Citadel and the fight against the Reapers.

A strong Mass Effect quiz can include Commander Shepard and the SSV Normandy, squadmates from Garrus and Tali to Liara, Wrex, Miranda and Zaeed, Saren and Sovereign in the first game, the Collectors and the suicide mission in Mass Effect 2, the genophage cure and the Krogan on Tuchanka, the Rachni and Prothean history, the Citadel Council and alien homeworlds like Palaven and Thessia, the endings of Mass Effect 3, and the Andromeda spin-off with its Heleus Cluster and strike teams. It works because it rewards fans who remember every dialogue choice.
