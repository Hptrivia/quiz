Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. trivia works because the show ties directly into the Marvel Cinematic Universe while building its own cast, gadgets and season-long mysteries, giving both MCU fans and dedicated show-watchers plenty to be tested on.

A strong Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. quiz can include Phil Coulson's red flying car Lola, Agent Melinda May, the Inhuman Daisy Johnson, the Framework storyline, Hydra's infiltration of S.H.I.E.L.D., and the team's shifting base of operations aboard the Zephyr. The theme stays engaging because it spans espionage, superpowers and time travel across seven seasons.
