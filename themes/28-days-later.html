28 Days Later trivia works because the franchise reinvented the zombie genre with a fast-spreading rage virus, giving fans distinct outbreaks, survivor groups and infected across three very different films to be tested on.

A strong 28 Days Later quiz can include Jim waking from his coma in an empty London hospital, Selena's machete, the army blockade the survivors travel north to reach, the American-led repopulation of London in 28 Weeks Later, Don's fatal mistake, and the feral, de-evolved Infected introduced in 28 Years Later. The theme stays engaging because it spans three outbreaks across different decades, each with its own survivors and rules for the virus.
