Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What is India's top film honor for lifetime contribution to cinema, named after a pioneering filmmaker? Dadasaheb Phalke Award
  • Which Maharashtra caves are famed for ancient Buddhist rock-cut paintings and sculptures? Ajanta Caves
  • Who was India's last Viceroy under British rule, overseeing the transition to independence in 1947? Louis Mountbatten
  • Who was India's first Prime Minister? Jawaharlal Nehru
  • Who became the first Indian to travel to space, in 1984? Rakesh Sharma
  • Which 4th-century BCE Sanskrit scholar wrote the Ashtadhyayi, a foundational treatise on Sanskrit grammar? Panini
  • What is the highest civilian award given in India? Bharat Ratna
  • What is the national fruit of India? Mango
  • Which union territory is a group of coral islands off India's southwestern coast? Lakshadweep
  • Which 1975-1977 period, declared by Indira Gandhi, saw civil liberties suspended and press censorship imposed nationwide? The Emergency

India trivia works well because it can move easily between states, capitals, major cities, national symbols, history, cricket, and cultural identity without feeling repetitive. It gives you more range than a narrow capitals-only or flag-only country quiz.

A strong India quiz can draw from New Delhi, Mumbai, the Taj Mahal, the Ganges, the national animal and bird, independence history, cricket culture, Bollywood, religious diversity, and political institutions. That mix helps the theme feel more complete, because players are not only recalling geography but also wider cultural and historical knowledge tied to the country.
