The Blacklist trivia works because each season introduces new blacklisters with their own criminal specialties, while a long-running mystery around Reddington's true identity keeps fans tracking clues across the whole series.

A strong Blacklist quiz can include Raymond Reddington, Elizabeth Keen, Donald Ressler, Dembe Zuma, Aram Mojtabai, Harold Cooper, Tom Keen, the FBI task force run out of the Post Office, and the show's parade of named blacklisters. The theme works because it rewards fans who followed both the case-of-the-week format and the central mystery of who Reddington really is.
