Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which Chinese company operates the messaging app WeChat? Tencent
  • Which eighth-century revolt shattered the prosperity of the Tang dynasty? The An Lushan Rebellion
  • Which ancient treatise on strategy is attributed to Sun Tzu? The Art of War
  • In which decade did the Sino-Soviet split rupture relations between Beijing and Moscow? 1960s
  • Which term describes the striped rainbow rock formations found in Gansu province? Danxia
  • Which Chinese hurdler won 110m gold at the 2004 Athens Olympics? Liu Xiang
  • Which pastry is traditionally eaten during the Mid-Autumn Festival? Mooncakes
  • Which 1919 student protest erupted over the handing of Shandong to Japan at Versailles? The May Fourth Movement
  • Which player became the first Chinese man to win the world chess championship? Ding Liren
  • Which language is the official spoken language of mainland China? Mandarin

China trivia works well because it can move easily between provinces, capitals, major cities, national symbols, history, and cultural identity without feeling repetitive. It gives you more range than a narrow capitals-only or flag-only country quiz.

A strong China quiz can draw from Beijing, Shanghai, the Great Wall, the Yangtze and Yellow rivers, the yuan, dynastic history, major cities, cuisine, and political and economic institutions. That mix helps the theme feel more complete, because players are not only recalling geography but also wider cultural and historical knowledge tied to the country.
