Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What trophies does Dexter keep from his victims early on? Blood slides
  • Which season features a split-personality killer who imagines his mentor alive? Season 6
  • Which character becomes Hannah McKay’s primary pursuer in Season 8? Jacob Elway
  • What signature item does the Ice Truck Killer leave at crime scenes? Dismembered, bloodless body parts
  • Who is Isaak Sirko’s murdered lover that Dexter kills in Episode 1 of Season 7? Viktor Baskov
  • Where does Deb witness Dexter kill Travis Marshall? At the abandoned church

  • Which character is a sanitation worker tied to the Barrel Girls murders? Boyd Fowler
  • Who plays Professor James Gellar (Season 6)? Edward James Olmos

Dexter trivia works because the show is built on a very clear central tension: a forensic analyst living as a serial killer under a personal code. That premise creates memorable cases, villains, relationships, and secrets that viewers tend to remember well.

A strong Dexter quiz can include Dexter, Debra, Harry's code, Rita, Harrison, Miami Metro, blood-spatter work, major antagonists, seasonal killers, cover stories, and the shifting consequences of Dexter's hidden life. The theme works because it can balance police-procedural detail with character psychology, family tension, and the cat-and-mouse structure that gives the series its identity.
