Sword Art Online trivia works because every arc introduces a new game world with its own rules, weapons and villains. Questions can cover Kirito's time trapped in Aincrad, the later VR games and the Alicization war.

A strong Sword Art Online quiz can include Kirito the Black Swordsman and Beater, Asuna the Flash and the Knights of the Blood Oath, Akihiko Kayaba and Heathcliff, the Moonlit Black Cats, Laughing Coffin, Klein, Agil and Silica, Alfheim Online and the World Tree, Sugou as Oberon, Sinon and Death Gun in Gun Gale Online, Yuuki and the Sleeping Knights, Eugeo, Alice and the Blue Rose Sword, the Underworld and Administrator. It works because fans who followed every arc know each game's details.
