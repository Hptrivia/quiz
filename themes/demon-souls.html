  • Which enemy drops the Ronin's Ring when defeated? Scirvir
  • Which world is the starting castle area? Boletarian Palace
  • How many areas does each non-Boletarian world typically contain, each ending in a boss fight, in the original game structure? Three
  • What is the maximum level a stat can reach in Demon's Souls? 99
  • Which NPC is a sly trickster who can push you into pits and appears across multiple worlds? Patches
  • How many HP does Late Moon Grass restore? 400
  • Which key is required to access where Old King Doran is found? Mausoleum Key
  • Which boss is fought on a narrow bridge-like walkway in Tower of Latria (3-2) and is known as a two-enemy battle? Maneater
  • Which NPC can invade you as a Black Phantom in certain tendency conditions and is tied to a katana and honor duel theme? Satsuki
  • What does Mephistopheles ask you to do as part of her questline? Assassinate NPCs in the Nexus

Demon's Souls trivia works because the game is packed with named worlds, punishing bosses, memorable mechanics, and a structure that players tend to remember clearly after finishing it. Even compared with later Souls games, its systems and locations still feel very specific.

A strong Demon's Souls quiz can include Boletarian Palace, Stonefang Tunnel, Tower of Latria, Shrine of Storms, Valley of Defilement, the Nexus, tendency systems, classes, upgrade paths, demons, NPCs, and bosses like the Flamelurker, Old Monk, Tower Knight, and Maiden Astraea. The theme gives you more than simple boss trivia, because it can also test players on world structure, mechanics, items, atmosphere, and the design ideas that shaped an entire genre afterwards.
