Atlanta trivia works because four seasons mixed grounded family drama with genuinely strange detours, giving fans a mix of running characters and standalone episodes to remember. Questions can cover Earn and Paper Boi, Darius and Van, and the show's stranger episodes that fans still argue about.

A strong Atlanta quiz can include Earn Marks and Donald Glover, Alfred 'Paper Boi' Miles and Brian Tyree Henry, Darius and Lakeith Stanfield, Van and Zazie Beetz, their daughter Lottie, Earn's dropout history at Princeton, the 'Robbin' Season' subtitle of Season 2, the Europe tour that runs through Season 3, and standalone episodes fans single out for how strange they get. It works because fans who watched every season know exactly which episode goes where the plot least expects it.
