The Good Wife trivia works because the show mixes case-of-the-week legal drama with long-running political and office storylines. Questions can cover the firm, the Florrick family and the cases that shaped Alicia's career.

A strong Good Wife quiz can include Alicia and Peter Florrick, Will Gardner, Diane Lockhart, Kalinda Sharma, Cary Agos, Eli Gold, Louis Canning, Elsbeth Tascioni, Zach and Grace, Peter's scandal with Amber Madison, the move from Highland Park, Lockhart Gardner and Florrick Agos, Alicia's run for State's Attorney and the shocking courtroom death of Will. It works because fans remember every betrayal and every firm split.
