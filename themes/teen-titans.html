  • In Teen Titans (2003), which honorary Titan wraps himself in flame? Hot Spot
  • Which actor voices Robin in both Teen Titans (2003) and Teen Titans Go!? Scott Menville

  • In Teen Titans (2003), which stretching French villainess disguises herself as other heroes to trap the Titans? Madame Rouge

Teen Titans trivia works because it covers two very different shows built around the same team, from the original series' real stakes and villains to Teen Titans Go!'s wall-to-wall gags. Fans can be tested on both eras, the team's powers, and the enemies they've faced.

A strong Teen Titans quiz can include the core team of Robin, Starfire, Raven, Beast Boy, and Cyborg, Jump City, villains like Slade, the Titans East roster, Raven's demonic heritage, and the shift in tone and humour when Teen Titans Go! takes over. It works because it spans both the earnest action-adventure of the original series and the absurdist comedy of its spin-off.
