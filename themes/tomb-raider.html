Tomb Raider trivia works because Lara Croft's adventures mix tombs, artefacts and survival set pieces that fans replay for years. Questions can cover the Endurance crew, the reboot trilogy's hidden cities and the gear that keeps Lara alive.

A strong Tomb Raider quiz can include Lara Croft and the wreck of the Endurance on Yamatai, crew members like Sam, Roth and Whitman, the Sun Queen Himiko and the Oni, Rise of the Tomb Raider with Trinity, the Remnant and Kitezh, Shadow of the Tomb Raider with Paititi and Dominguez, Croft Manor and Lara's family history, the tools and gear from the climbing axe to the rebreather, optional tombs and relic hunting, and the wider history of the series and its films. It works because it rewards fans who explored every optional tomb.
