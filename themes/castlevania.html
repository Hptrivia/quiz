  • What legendary figure is said to rest in the catacombs beneath the city? The Sleeping Soldier
  • Into what animal does Alucard shapeshift to flank the fire-breathing beast? A wolf
  • What title did Dracula use to identify himself when confronting the Bishop? Vlad Dracula Tepes
  • Where does Dracula suggest he and Lisa travel in the final scene? Whitby
  • What realm is Saint Germain searching for? The Infinite Corridor
  • Who orders Lisa's execution for witchcraft? The Bishop
  • What is the title of the town leader who befriends Trevor and Sypha in season three? The Judge
  • What nomadic group of scholars does Trevor defend from the Church? The Speakers
  • In season two, who kills the vampire warlord Godbrand? Isaac
  • What was Flyseyes in his human life? A philosopher

Castlevania trivia works because the series turns a classic Konami game franchise into a sharply written four-season saga packed with vampires, forgemasters and monster hunters. Questions can cover Dracula's grief over Lisa, the Belmont legacy and the battles that follow his death.

A strong Castlevania quiz can include Lisa of Lupu burned in Targoviste, Dracula's war council, Trevor Belmont and his Morning Star whip, Sypha and the Speakers, Alucard and the Belmont Hold, the forgemasters Hector and Isaac, Carmilla and the Council of Sisters in Styria, Lenore, Striga and Morana, Saint Germain and the Infinite Corridor, the Judge of Lindenfeld, Death and the Rebis. It works because fans who watched every season remember the lore as well as the fights.
