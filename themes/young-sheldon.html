Young Sheldon trivia works because it gives a beloved Big Bang Theory character his own origin story, giving fans plenty of family backstory and childhood detail to be tested on.

A strong Young Sheldon quiz can include the Cooper family's home state of Texas, their small town, Sheldon's early enrollment in high school, his father George's job as a football coach, his twin sister Missy, and his older brother Georgie's schemes. The theme stays engaging because it fills in the family history that The Big Bang Theory only ever referenced.
