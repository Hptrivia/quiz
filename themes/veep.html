  • During her season 6 trip to Qatar, what territory does Selina try to claim credit for freeing? Tibet

Veep trivia works because seven seasons of Washington farce produced a huge cast of aides, rivals and disasters, plus some of the most quotable insults on television. Questions can cover Selina and her staff, the campaigns and the jobs everyone kept losing.

A strong Veep quiz can include Selina Meyer and Julia Louis-Dreyfus, Gary Walsh and the Leviathan bag, Amy Brookheimer, Dan Egan, Mike McLintock, Jonah Ryan and his run for office, Sue Wilson, Ben Cafferty and Kent Davison, Catherine Meyer and Marjorie, Andrew Meyer and Tom James, the Meyer Fund and the presidential library, the tied election and the recount, the Armando Iannucci era and the David Mandel seasons, plus the Emmy-winning insults and running gags fans quote line for line. It works because viewers who watched every season know which humiliation belonged to which campaign.
