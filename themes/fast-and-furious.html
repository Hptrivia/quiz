Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What nickname does reigning champion Takashi go by in "Tokyo Drift"? Drift King
  • Which film served as a tribute to Paul Walker following his death? Furious 7
  • In what year did the Fast & Furious franchise begin? 2001
  • Which film introduces the covert agent Mr. Nobody? Furious 7
  • In "Furious 7," the crew drives a car between skyscrapers in which city? Abu Dhabi
  • What is Brian O'Conner's real job when he infiltrates Dom's crew? Undercover cop
  • What is the name of the satellite Roman and Tej are sent to destroy in F9? Aries
  • What is Dom's father's name, shown racing in F9's flashback? Jack Toretto
  • What do astronauts aboard the ISS jokingly mistake Roman and Tej for in F9? Minions
  • What device does the F9 team use to snatch Jakob's car off a mountain road? A giant electromagnet

Fast & Furious trivia works because the franchise has grown from street-racing drama into a globe-hopping heist saga, giving fans plenty of crew history, stunts and callbacks to be tested on across the whole series.

A strong Fast & Furious quiz can include Dominic Toretto, Brian O'Conner, Letty Ortiz, Roman, Tej, Luke Hobbs and Deckard Shaw, the crew's family-first philosophy, the heist in Fast Five, and the franchise's escalating set pieces. The theme stays engaging because it spans street racing, government missions, and reunions of characters thought dead.
