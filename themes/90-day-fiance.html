90 Day Fiancé trivia works because more than a decade of K-1 visa romances has produced an enormous cast of couples fans can't stop talking about. Questions can cover who came from where, which season they appeared in and how each relationship really ended.

A strong 90 Day Fiancé quiz can include Danielle and Mohamed from Tunisia, Russ and Paola chasing her modelling dreams in Miami, Azan and Nicole, Darcey and Jesse, Angela and Michael, Jasmine and Gino, Emily and Kobe meeting in Xi'an, Asuelu and Kalani at The Last Resort, Big Ed, Before the 90 Days, Happily Ever After and The Other Way, plus the tell-alls hosted by Shaun Robinson. It works because fans remember every visa deadline, every family meeting and every dramatic airport arrival.
