Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What is the name of the 12th hole at Augusta National? Golden Bell
  • What is the nickname for holes 11, 12 and 13 at Augusta National? Amen Corner
  • On which hole did Jordan Spieth make a quadruple bogey in the final round of the 2016 Masters? 12th
  • What was Europe's lead going into the Sunday singles at the 2025 Ryder Cup? 11.5-4.5
  • In match play, what term describes leading by as many holes as remain? Dormie
  • Who won the 2025 US Women's Open at Erin Hills? Maja Stark
  • Which trophy is awarded to the winner of The Open Championship? Claret Jug
  • How many of the 12 singles points did the USA win at the 2025 Ryder Cup, tying a record? 8.5
  • By how many shots did Aaron Rai win the 2026 PGA Championship? 3
  • At which course did Brooks Koepka become the first LIV player to win a major, in 2023? Oak Hill

Golf trivia works because the majors produce new champions every year while the sport's history is full of iconic courses and records. Questions can mix scoring terms and rules with Masters moments and Ryder Cup results.

A strong golf quiz can include Tiger Woods and his 15-stroke US Open win at Pebble Beach, Byron Nelson's 11 straight wins, Rory McIlroy completing the career Grand Slam at the 2025 Masters, Scottie Scheffler, Xander Schauffele, Bryson DeChambeau at Pinehurst, the island green at TPC Sawgrass, the Swilcan Bridge at St Andrews, Hogan Bridge at Augusta, the Crow's Nest, Europe's 2025 Ryder Cup win and the 14-club rule. It works because golf fans remember every major.
