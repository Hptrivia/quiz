Killing Eve trivia works because the show turns a spy-versus-assassin premise into something stranger and funnier than a normal thriller, driven by the pull between its two leads. Fans can be tested on Villanelle's kills, Eve's investigation, and the shifting loyalties around them.

A strong Killing Eve quiz can include Eve Polastri and Villanelle's escalating obsession, Villanelle's handler Konstantin, Carolyn Martens and MI6's shadowy Russia desk, the assassinations Villanelle carries out across Europe, and the show's dark humour and fashion-forward style. It works because it blends espionage plotting with a genuinely strange, magnetic central relationship.
