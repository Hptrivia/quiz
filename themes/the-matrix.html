  • The station name in The Matrix Revolutions is an anagram of which word? Limbo
  • What is the name of the software company Thomas Anderson works for in the first film? Metacortex
  • Which actor plays Trinity across the Matrix films? Carrie-Anne Moss
  • In which Matrix film does Trinity die? The Matrix Revolutions
  • What meal does the traitor Cypher enjoy while explaining he wants to be plugged back in? Steak
  • The exile information-trafficker known as the Merovingian goes by which other nickname? The Frenchman
  • In Resurrections, what fabricated name does Trinity's husband go by? Chad
  • Trinity earned her reputation as a hacker by cracking the database of which agency? The IRS
  • In which Matrix film do Neo and the crew first meet the Merovingian? The Matrix Reloaded
  • In the first film, inside which hollowed-out book does Neo hide his illegal discs? Simulacra and Simulation

The Matrix trivia works because the franchise built a dense, philosophical sci-fi world with its own rules and vocabulary, giving fans both action set pieces and deep lore to be tested on across the trilogy and Resurrections.

A strong Matrix quiz can include Neo, Trinity, Morpheus, Agent Smith and the Oracle, the red pill and blue pill, the hovercraft Nebuchadnezzar, Zion, the Sentinels, and the nature of the simulated reality itself. The theme stays engaging because it can move between the original trilogy's mythology and the newer questions raised by Resurrections.
