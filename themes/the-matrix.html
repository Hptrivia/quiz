Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What is the name of the Merovingian's upscale restaurant in The Matrix Reloaded? Le Vrai
  • What is the name of the training test in which Neo must make an impossible leap between two rooftops? The Jump program
  • Which club does Seraph, Morpheus, and Trinity enter to confront the Merovingian in The Matrix Revolutions? Club Hel
  • In which Matrix film does Trinity die? The Matrix Revolutions
  • What is the name of the coffee shop where Thomas Anderson regularly sees Tiffany in Resurrections? Simulatte
  • As Tiffany in Resurrections, Trinity is married with how many sons? Two
  • In which Matrix film does Neo first meet the Oracle? The Matrix
  • The Latin phrase 'Temet Nosce' above the Oracle's kitchen doorway translates to which saying? Know thyself
  • The Nebuchadnezzar's dedication plaque lists it as which mark and hull number? Mark III No. 11
  • In Resurrections, what is the name of the Analyst's black cat? Deja Vu

The Matrix trivia works because the franchise built a dense, philosophical sci-fi world with its own rules and vocabulary, giving fans both action set pieces and deep lore to be tested on across the trilogy and Resurrections.

A strong Matrix quiz can include Neo, Trinity, Morpheus, Agent Smith and the Oracle, the red pill and blue pill, the hovercraft Nebuchadnezzar, Zion, the Sentinels, and the nature of the simulated reality itself. The theme stays engaging because it can move between the original trilogy's mythology and the newer questions raised by Resurrections.
