  • Remco Evenepoel won the cycling road race and which other event at Paris 2024? Time trial
  • Which city hosted the 2016 Summer Olympics? Rio de Janeiro
  • In which snowboarding event did Shaun White win all three of his Olympic golds? Halfpipe

Olympics trivia works because every Games creates new heroes while the history is packed with legendary performances. Questions can cover medal tables, world records, new sports and the moments that defined each Olympics.

A strong Olympics quiz can include Michael Phelps beating Milorad Cavic by 0.01 seconds, Simone Biles, Usain Bolt, Jesse Owens, Mo Farah's distance doubles, Steve Redgrave's five golds, the Miracle on Ice, the Dream Team, the Jamaican bobsleigh team, Steven Bradbury, Noah Lyles, Julien Alfred, Mondo Duplantis, breaking at Paris 2024, surfing in Tahiti, Johannes Klaebo and Jordan Stolz at Milan Cortina 2026, and the five rings. It works because everyone has a favourite Olympic moment.
