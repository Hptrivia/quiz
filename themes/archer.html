Archer trivia works because the show pairs genuine spy-thriller plotting with rapid-fire running gags and a cast that never stops bickering, giving fans both mission details and years of in-jokes to be tested on.

A strong Archer quiz can include Sterling Archer, Lana Kane, Malory Archer, Cyril Figgis, Cheryl Tunt, Pam Poovey and Dr. Krieger, the agency's various names across seasons, Archer's codename Duchess, and running bits like Danger Zone and Lana's exasperation. The theme stays engaging because it spans the agency's shifting identities, the noir and sci-fi themed seasons, and fourteen seasons of callbacks.
