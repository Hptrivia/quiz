Beauty in Black trivia works because it combines a rags-to-riches survival story with a wealthy family's hidden secrets, giving fans plenty of twists, betrayals and character history to be tested on.

A strong Beauty in Black quiz can include the Chicago setting where the series begins, Kimmie's job at the club, the Bellarie family's cosmetics empire, Mallory's marriage, and the mounting secrets that connect Kimmie's past to the family's present. The theme stays engaging because it moves between Kimmie's personal survival story and the Bellaries' scandal-filled world.
