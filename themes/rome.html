  • Soon after returning to Rome, what game sparks Pullo's tavern brawl? Dice
  • In which century is Rome set? 1st century BC
  • Who were the three co-creators of the TV series Rome? Bruno Heller, John Milius, William J. MacDonald

Rome trivia works because it mixes real history with the personal stories of soldiers and families. Questions can cover Caesar and Pompey, Mark Antony and Octavian, Atia and Servilia, and the adventures of Vorenus and Pullo.

A strong Rome quiz can include Lucius Vorenus and Titus Pullo of the 13th Legion, the stolen eagle in Gaul, Julius Caesar and Pompey, Mark Antony as tribune, young Octavian and his mother Atia, Servilia and the Ides of March, Cato in the Senate, Niobe and the family butcher shop, the gold from the Temple of Saturn and Cleopatra in Egypt. It works because history and drama meet on every page.
