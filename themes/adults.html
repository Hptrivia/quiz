  • Which medical process does Issa begin in the second season? Egg freezing
  • What kind of leak does Samir cause by breaking a pipe? Carbon monoxide
  • What was the total of Billie's hospital bill? $15,000

Adults trivia works because the show packs its friendships into short, chaotic episodes full of specific, quotable details. Fans can be tested on Billie, Anton, Issa, Samir and the rest of the group.

A strong Adults quiz can include Billie, Anton, Issa, Samir, Annabelle and Carly, the friend group's late therapist George, Billie's older boyfriend nicknamed Mr. Teacher, the Feeld app, Anton's fake New York Marathon training, and the eight-episode seasons. It works because the humor comes from small, memorable details of a messy friend group rather than one big plot.
