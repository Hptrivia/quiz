Hazbin Hotel trivia works because the show packs a big cast of demons, angels and overlords into a musical with a fiercely loyal fanbase. Questions can cover Charlie and the hotel staff, the Vees and the angels, and the songs fans know by heart.

A strong Hazbin Hotel quiz can include Charlie Morningstar and Vaggie, Alastor the Radio Demon, Angel Dust and his pig Fat Nuggets, Husk the bartender, Niffty, Sir Pentious and the Egg Boiz, Lucifer and his love of ducks, Adam and Lute, the Extermination and the exorcists, the Vees (Vox, Valentino and Velvette), Carmilla Carmine, Pentagram City and the Pride Ring, and songs like Happy Day in Hell, Hell's Greatest Dad, Poison, Stayed Gone and Loser, Baby. It works because fans who have replayed every number can place every lyric and every rivalry.
