  • How many Grand Slam singles titles did Serena Williams win? 23
  • Which country does Elena Rybakina represent? Kazakhstan
  • What is the second-largest court at Roland Garros called? Court Suzanne Lenglen
  • What is the name of the main arena at the Australian Open? Rod Laver Arena
  • What was the fourth-set tiebreak score in the 1980 Wimbledon final between Borg and McEnroe? 18-16
  • Which nation has won the most Davis Cup titles? United States

Tennis trivia works because fans follow the four majors every year and remember the great champions and finals. The sport's long history gives plenty of room for both easy questions and deep cuts.

A strong tennis quiz can include Rod Laver, Bjorn Borg, John McEnroe, Pete Sampras, Roger Federer, Rafael Nadal and his French Open titles, Novak Djokovic, Carlos Alcaraz, Serena Williams, Margaret Court, Iga Swiatek, Naomi Osaka, Wimbledon, Roland Garros, the US Open, the Australian Open, the Golden Slam, the ATP Finals and the Isner-Mahut marathon. It works because tennis mixes records, rivalries and history.
