Derry Girls trivia works because the show crams so many quotable lines, family arguments and disastrous schemes into three short series. Questions can cover the gang, the Quinn family and the nuns at their Catholic girls' school.

A strong Derry Girls quiz can include Erin Quinn, Orla McCool, Clare Devlin, Michelle Mallon and her English cousin James Maguire, Sister Michael, Granda Joe and his feud with Gerry, Ma Mary, Aunt Sarah, Uncle Colm's endless stories, Our Lady Immaculate College, the trip to Paris that never happened, the Spice Girls, the Take That concert and the Good Friday Agreement vote. It works because fans can quote it endlessly.
