The Handmaid's Tale trivia works because Gilead's society runs on a dense set of invented ranks, rituals and terminology, giving fans a rich, specific vocabulary to be tested on alongside the show's central characters and plot.

A strong Handmaid's Tale quiz can include June Osborne, Serena Joy Waterford, Commander Fred Waterford, Nick Blaine, Aunt Lydia and Moira, along with Gilead terms like the Colonies, Econowives, Particicution, Salvaging and the Mayday resistance network. The theme works because it rewards fans who track both the characters' fates and the show's dense invented world.
