Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which substance is used in the final trap of Saw VI? Hydrofluoric acid
  • Which two words are spoken as the first Saw film ends? Game over
  • What is the name of the health insurance company at the centre of Saw VI? Umbrella Health
  • Which phrase does Jigsaw use when introducing a test? I want to play a game
  • What does the puppet from Jigsaw's videos ride when it appears in person? A tricycle
  • How long after John Kramer's death is the 2017 film Jigsaw set? Ten years
  • What is mounted at the centre of the carousel trap in Saw VI? A shotgun
  • What kind of mask is worn when Jigsaw's victims are abducted? A pig mask
  • In Saw II, where is the combination the captives need written? On their necks
  • Which role did Mateo play in the fake clinic in Saw X? The anaesthesiologist

Saw trivia works because the franchise is built on elaborate, rule-bound traps and constant twist reveals, giving fans plenty of specific games, victims and Jigsaw connections to be tested on across the series.

A strong Saw quiz can include the bathroom where the first film's two captured men wake up, John Kramer's identity as the Jigsaw Killer, his tape-recorded rules for each trap, Amanda Young's role as his apprentice, and the franchise's reliance on flashbacks to connect victims across films. The theme stays engaging because each film adds new traps and reframes earlier reveals.
