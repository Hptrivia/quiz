Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • Which film in the series is set mostly in Mexico? Saw X
  • What had been implanted in Diego's forearms in Saw X? Pipe bombs
  • How long does the self-help author have to finish his game in Saw 3D? Sixty minutes
  • What job does the man tested throughout Saw IV hold? SWAT team leader
  • Which ancient torture device is recreated for the final trap of Saw 3D? A brazen bull
  • In the first Saw film, which failing does Jigsaw's tape accuse Adam of? Apathy
  • Which phrase does Jigsaw use when introducing a test? I want to play a game
  • How many syringes is Carly offered in her test in Jigsaw? Three
  • What kind of building is Rigg's second test set in Saw IV? A motel
  • In the first Saw film, what kind of room do the two captured men wake up in? A bathroom

Saw trivia works because the franchise is built on elaborate, rule-bound traps and constant twist reveals, giving fans plenty of specific games, victims and Jigsaw connections to be tested on across the series.

A strong Saw quiz can include the bathroom where the first film's two captured men wake up, John Kramer's identity as the Jigsaw Killer, his tape-recorded rules for each trap, Amanda Young's role as his apprentice, and the franchise's reliance on flashbacks to connect victims across films. The theme stays engaging because each film adds new traps and reframes earlier reveals.
