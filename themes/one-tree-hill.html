One Tree Hill trivia works because nine seasons in a small North Carolina town gave fans a huge cast of relationships, rivalries and running jokes to keep straight. Questions can cover Lucas and Nathan, the Ravens, the music and the moments that defined each era.

A strong One Tree Hill quiz can include Lucas and Nathan Scott and the feud that starts it all, Haley James Scott and her marriage and music career, Peyton Sawyer and Red Bedroom Records, Brooke Davis and Clothes Over Bros, Julian, Mouth, Skills, Chase and Millicent, the Tree Hill Ravens and Whitey Durham, Dan Scott and the murder of Keith, the time jump into adult life and the Charleston Chiefs and NBA run, plus the cast behind them including Chad Michael Murray, James Lafferty, Bethany Joy Lenz, Hilarie Burton and Sophia Bush. It works because the show rewards viewers who followed every couple, comeback and retired jersey.
