Sitcoms trivia works because decades of half-hour comedies have produced widely recognisable characters, catchphrases and running gags, giving players entry points across generations and networks.

A strong Sitcoms quiz can include characters and moments from Friends, The Office, Parks and Recreation, It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia, 30 Rock, Community, Scrubs, How I Met Your Mother, That '70s Show and The Simpsons, along with the actors, catchphrases and iconic bits each show is known for. The theme works because it lets questions move freely between eras and formats while staying anchored in shows most players have seen.
