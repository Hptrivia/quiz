The Boondocks trivia works because the series pairs biting satire with a tight cast of unforgettable characters, giving fans plenty of specific storylines, catchphrases, and running gags to test their memory against.

A strong Boondocks quiz can include Huey Freeman, Riley Freeman, Robert 'Granddad' Freeman, Uncle Ruckus, Tom and Sarah DuBois, Jazmine DuBois, Cindy McPhearson, A Pimp Named Slickback, Gin Rummy, Colonel H. Stinkmeaner, the suburb of Woodcrest, and the show's recurring targets of satire. The theme stays engaging because it spans the comic strip's origins through the show's most talked-about episodes and season-defining moments.
