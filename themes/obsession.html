Obsession trivia works because Curry Barker's horror debut hides a lot of setup in its friendship scenes before everything turns. Questions can cover Bear and Nikki at the music store, trivia night at the bar, the One Wish Willow and the wish that ruins everything.

A strong Obsession quiz can include Baron 'Bear' Bailey and Nikki Freeman, the Cassells music store and their boss Carter Harper, Wednesday trivia night at Barker's Pub and the rival Rag Tags, the crystal shop The Green Man with Viola and Harry, the One Wish Willow, its tagline and its manufacturer, Bear's wish and the split personality it triggers in Nikki, Ian's secret and his own wish, Sarah and her art school rejection, Bear's cat Sandy, the Hansel and Gretel monologue, plus the cast and production with Michael Johnston, Inde Navarrette, Andy Richter and writer-director Curry Barker. It works because it rewards fans who noticed the small details before the horror kicked in.
