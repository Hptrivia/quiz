  • Who is the main playable protagonist of Uncharted 4? Nathan Drake
  • What is the name of the wealthy district of Libertalia where the pirate founders built their lavish homes? New Devon
  • Uncharted 2 opens its treasure hunt with a museum heist in which city? Istanbul
  • In multiplayer, the AI allies players could summon into a match were called what? Sidekicks
  • The estate hosting the Italian auction belongs to which family? Rossi
  • By what other name is the lost brass city of Iram known in Uncharted 3? Ubar
  • The burning chateau chapter of Uncharted 3 is set in which country? France
  • Which Uncharted game features a famous set-piece aboard a cargo plane over the desert? Drake's Deception
  • The brothers' late mother, Cassandra, worked as a what? Historian
  • In Uncharted 1, what is the name of the Indonesian pirate captain who rivals Drake? Eddy Raja

Uncharted trivia works because the series follows Nathan Drake chasing lost treasures across cinematic, globe-trotting adventures. Fans can be tested on the locations, the allies and the historical mysteries that drive each hunt.

A strong Uncharted quiz can include Nathan Drake and his mentor Sully, allies and rivals like Elena, Chloe and his brother Sam, the treasures hunted from El Dorado to Libertalia, the exotic locations and set-piece chapters, the climbing, gunplay and puzzle-solving, the villains chasing the same prizes, and the story that unfolds across the series. It works because it rewards fans who followed Drake's adventures to the end.
