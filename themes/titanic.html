Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What does old Rose say everyone called the Titanic? The Ship of Dreams
  • Who composed the film's score? James Horner
  • What was Lovett best known for finding before he went after the Titanic? Spanish gold
  • How many minutes before the ship sails does Jack win his ticket? Five
  • What are the expedition's deep-sea submersibles called? Mir
  • Which ride does Jack promise Rose they will go on at the pier? The roller coaster
  • How much money does Cal tell Lovejoy to give Jack for saving Rose? Twenty dollars
  • What job does Ruth fear ending up in if Rose does not marry Cal? A seamstress
  • What is the name of the research ship Lovett's team works from? Keldysh
  • According to the treasure hunters' research, where did Rose move after marrying? Cedar Rapids

Titanic trivia works because the film is full of memorable characters, quotable lines, and precise details about the ship, the passengers, and the night of the sinking. Fans can be tested on both the love story and the disaster.

A strong Titanic quiz can include Jack Dawson, Rose, Cal Hockley, Ruth, Brock Lovett, Fabrizio, Tommy, Molly Brown, the Heart of the Ocean, the Mir submersibles, and the Irish mother's Tir na nOg bedtime story. It works because it mixes romance, real history, and a well-known disaster into a film fans know scene by scene.
