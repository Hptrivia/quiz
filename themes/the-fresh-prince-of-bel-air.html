Fresh Prince trivia works because the show mixes big laugh-out-loud set pieces with family moments fans still remember decades later. Questions can cover Will's schemes, Carlton's dances, Geoffrey's one-liners and the episodes that turned serious.

A strong Fresh Prince of Bel-Air quiz can include Will's move from West Philadelphia, Uncle Phil the judge and his farm childhood, both Aunt Vivs, Carlton and his university peacock mascot, Hilary's weather girl job, Ashley's music career, Geoffrey the butler, Jazz, baby Nicky and Dougie the Whale, Will's father Lou returning, Will getting shot in season five, the invented poet Raphael de la Ghetto, ULA and Carlton's move to Princeton in the finale. It works because fans who grew up on reruns remember the small details.
