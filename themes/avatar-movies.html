Avatar trivia works because Pandora is one of the most detailed worlds in modern blockbuster cinema, with its own clans, creatures, language and ecology. Questions can cover Jake Sully and Neytiri, the RDA and unobtanium, Hell's Gate, the reefs of the Metkayina and the fire ash beyond.

A strong Avatar Movies quiz can include Jake Sully, Neytiri, Grace Augustine, Colonel Quaritch, Trudy and Norm, the Omaticaya and Hometree, Hell's Gate and the RDA, unobtanium, the avatar programme and link units, banshees, direhorses and the Toruk Makto, Eywa and the Tree of Souls, the Sully children, the Metkayina reef clan, tulkun and Payakan, plus the Way of Water and Fire and Ash storylines, the cast and the production milestones behind the series. It works because the world-building is deep enough to carry hard questions while the central story stays instantly recognisable.
