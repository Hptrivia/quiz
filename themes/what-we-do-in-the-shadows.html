What We Do in the Shadows trivia works because six seasons of vampire misadventures produced a huge store of absurd storylines, catchphrases and one-off characters. Questions can cover Nandor, Laszlo, Nadja, Colin Robinson and Guillermo, plus the Vampiric Council and their many enemies.

A strong What We Do in the Shadows quiz can include Nandor the Relentless and Al Quolanudar, Laszlo Cravensworth and Jackie Daytona, Nadja and her doll, Colin Robinson the energy vampire and Baby Colin, Guillermo de la Cruz and his Van Helsing ancestry, the Guide and the Vampiric Council, the Baron, Simon the Devious, Sean and Charmaine next door, the werewolf truce and the kickball game, the Djinn and Nandor's wives, and Guillermo's long road to becoming a vampire. It works because fans can quote every scene and remember every ridiculous plan.
