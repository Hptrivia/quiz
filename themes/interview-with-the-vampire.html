Interview with the Vampire trivia works because the series reshapes Anne Rice's novel across New Orleans, Paris and Dubai, with every memory open to dispute. Questions can cover Louis and Lestat, Claudia's diaries, the Theatre des Vampires and the second interview with Daniel.

A strong Interview with the Vampire quiz can include Louis and his brother Paul, Lestat de Lioncourt, Claudia and her diaries, the Fair Play Saloon renamed Azalea Hall, the Mardi Gras ball, Daniel Molloy and the 1973 San Francisco tapes, Rashid and Dubai, Armand and Santiago, the Theatre des Vampires in Paris, Madeleine, the Talamasca and Raglan James, the trial and the rock star Lestat of season three. It works because fans who watched closely know where each version of the story changed.
