The King of Queens trivia works because nine seasons of Doug's schemes, Carrie's temper and Arthur's outbursts produced plenty of memorable episodes. Questions can cover the Heffernans, their friends from Cooper's and the jobs and plans that never quite worked out.

A strong King of Queens quiz can include Doug at IPS, Carrie at the law firm and the Dugan Group, Arthur Spooner and his dog walking, the Heffernans' home on Aberdeen Street in Rego Park, Deacon, Spence and Danny, Holly the dog walker, Cooper's bar, Lou Ferrigno next door, Doug's ice cream truck dream, Spence's mother and his pet ferret, and the Franken-couple. It works because fans of the show remember the details of every argument and get-rich-quick plan.
