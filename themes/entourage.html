Entourage trivia works because the show is full of fictional films, deals and industry names that fans followed across eight seasons and a movie. Questions can cover Vince and the boys, super-agent Ari Gold and the projects that made or nearly broke Vince's career.

A strong Entourage quiz can include Vince, E, Turtle and Johnny Drama, Ari Gold and his assistant Lloyd, Mrs. Ari, Aquaman for James Cameron, Queens Boulevard at Sundance, Medellin, Billy Walsh, Smoke Jumpers, Johnny's Bananas, Five Towns, Avion tequila, Dana Gordon and Babs Miller, Terrance McQuewick, Sloan and Andrew Dice Clay. It works because fans remember every film, deal and blow-up behind the scenes.
