Scandal trivia works because the show mixes glossy Washington power plays with an ongoing conspiracy that reaches all the way to the presidency. Fans can be tested on Olivia's fixes, her tangled relationship with Fitz, and the secrets B613 is built to bury.

A strong Scandal quiz can include Olivia Pope and her crisis-management firm, her affair with President Fitzgerald Grant, the covert agency B613, Cyrus Beene's political maneuvering, the rigged election storyline, and the show's twisty conspiracies at the heart of the White House. It works because it spans both case-of-the-week crisis fixes and a sprawling ongoing conspiracy.
