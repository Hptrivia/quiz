College basketball trivia works because March Madness creates a fresh set of upsets and heroes every year, while coaching legends and dynasties give the sport a deep history. Questions can mix classic moments with the most recent tournaments.

A strong college basketball quiz can include John Wooden and UCLA's seven straight titles, Mike Krzyzewski's five titles at Duke, Christian Laettner's shot against Kentucky, Dean Smith's Four Corners, Nolan Richardson's 40 Minutes of Hell, Jay Wright's Villanova, Jimmer Fredette, Kentucky's 38-0 start, Northern Iowa upsetting Kansas, the First Four in Dayton, the NET rankings and the 2026 Final Four. It works because fans remember every bracket buster.
