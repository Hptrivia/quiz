BoJack Horseman trivia works because the show mixes absurdist animal-world jokes with genuinely heavy character arcs, giving fans both silly world-building details and real emotional plot beats to be quizzed on.

A strong BoJack Horseman quiz can include BoJack Horseman, Princess Carolyn, Diane Nguyen, Mr. Peanutbutter, Todd Chavez, the renaming of Hollywood to Hollywoo, BoJack's old sitcom Horsin' Around, and the show's mix of celebrity cameos and animal puns. The theme stays engaging because it rewards fans who track both the running jokes and the show's long-form character histories.
