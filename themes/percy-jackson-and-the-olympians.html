Percy Jackson trivia works because the series is full of mythology hidden inside everyday places, from Medusa's garden gnome shop to the Underworld under Los Angeles. Questions can cover the quest for the lightning bolt, the Sea of Monsters and the show's cast.

A strong Percy Jackson quiz can include Percy and his sword Riptide, Annabeth Chase, Grover, Mr. Brunner as Chiron, Mrs. Dodds the Fury, Camp Half-Blood and the Hermes cabin, Luke and Thalia's tree, Clarisse and Ares, Medusa, Procrustes, the Waterland park, the Underworld, Tyson the Cyclops, the Sea of Monsters, Circe, Polyphemus and the Golden Fleece. It works because fans of Rick Riordan's books know each monster and myth.
