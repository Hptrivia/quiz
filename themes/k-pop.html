K-Pop trivia works because the genre has a huge global fanbase, a clear set of big agencies and groups, and plenty of fan traditions to ask about. Fans can be tested on songs, albums, members, and the culture around comebacks and lightsticks.

A strong K-Pop quiz can include BTS, Blackpink, Twice, Girls' Generation, PSY and Gangnam Style, G-Dragon, Sunmi, CNBLUE, the big agencies HYBE, SM Entertainment, JYP, and YG, plus fan culture like comebacks, photocards, and official lightsticks. It works because it mixes chart hits, group lineups, and fandom traditions into questions fans can answer.
