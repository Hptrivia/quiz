The Simpsons trivia works because more than thirty years of episodes have built one of the most detailed towns in television, packed with quotes, places and running gags. Questions can cover the Simpson family, the people of Springfield and the classic episodes fans still quote.

A strong Simpsons quiz can include Homer at the power plant in Sector 7G, Marge, Bart, Lisa and Maggie, 742 Evergreen Terrace, Duff beer and Moe's Tavern, Ned Flanders and the Leftorium, Mr. Burns and Smithers, Krusty and Sideshow Bob, Apu and the Kwik-E-Mart, Principal Skinner and Springfield Elementary, Itchy and Scratchy, Treehouse of Horror and the couch gag, the monorail, Mr. Plow, the Be Sharps, Who Shot Mr. Burns, Frank Grimes and Shelbyville. It works because the show's golden-age episodes are among the most quoted in TV history.
