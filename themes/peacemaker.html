Peacemaker trivia works because it takes a minor Suicide Squad character and gives him his own irreverent, heartfelt series, giving fans plenty of team dynamics, dark comedy and DC lore to be tested on.

A strong Peacemaker quiz can include Peacemaker's pet bald eagle Eagly, the covert team assembled by Amanda Waller, the Butterflies' alien invasion plot, Christopher Smith's backstory, and the team's dysfunctional dynamic across their mission. The theme stays engaging because it mixes gruesome action with genuine character growth for a villain-turned-antihero.
