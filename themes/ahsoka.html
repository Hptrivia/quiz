Ahsoka trivia works because the series picks up threads from The Clone Wars and Star Wars Rebels and carries them into live action, giving fans years of backstory to be tested on. Questions can cover Ahsoka and Sabine, the hunt for Ezra Bridger, and the dark Jedi hired to stop them.

A strong Ahsoka quiz can include Ahsoka Tano and her call sign Fulcrum, Sabine Wren and her loth-cat Murley, Huyang and his lessons in lightsaber building, Hera Syndulla and the Ghost, Baylan Skoll and Shin Hati, Morgan Elsbeth and the Eye of Sion, the star map and the purrgil, Grand Admiral Thrawn and his exile on Peridea, the Great Mothers and the Noti, Ezra Bridger's return, and Ahsoka's journey through the World Between Worlds with Anakin. It works because fans who followed the animated shows can spot every callback.
