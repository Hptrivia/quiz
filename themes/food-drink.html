Food and drink trivia works because it pulls from things people encounter constantly, meals, snacks, recipes, restaurants, national dishes, alcohol, soft drinks, and familiar ingredients. That makes it easy to enter while still leaving room for tougher culinary knowledge.

A strong Food & Drink quiz can include famous dishes, cooking terms, herbs and spices, desserts, international cuisines, kitchen basics, coffee, tea, wine, spirits, soft drinks, and ingredient recognition across different cultures. The theme stays varied because it can move between everyday eating habits, hospitality knowledge, global food identity, and the small facts people often know without realising it.
