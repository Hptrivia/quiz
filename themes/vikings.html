Sample Questions

Tap an answer to reveal it.

  • What is the name of Floki's wife? Helga
  • Floki sails off alone to found a settlement on which island? Iceland
  • Bjorn is mortally wounded during an invasion by which people, led by his own half-brother? The Rus
  • According to the series, the Viking Age begins with the Lindisfarne raid in what year? 793
  • King Aethelwulf, Ecbert's son, dies from what unusual cause? A bee sting
  • Lagertha kills her abusive second husband, Earl Sigvard, by stabbing him where? In the eye
  • After settling among the Franks, Rollo is granted which noble title? Duke
  • In which season does Ragnar die? Season 4
  • How many sons does Ragnar have with his second wife? Four
  • What ritual execution does Bjorn inflict on King Aelle to avenge Ragnar? Blood eagle

Vikings trivia works because it follows one family across generations, giving fans both Ragnar's rise and his sons' separate storylines to track alongside real Norse history and legend.

A strong Vikings quiz can include Ragnar Lothbrok, Lagertha, Bjorn Ironside, Ivar the Boneless, Floki and Aslaug, the settlement of Kattegat, the raid on Lindisfarne, and the show's blend of historical fact and Norse legend. The theme stays engaging because it spans multiple generations of the same family across the full run of the series.
