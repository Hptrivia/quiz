  • King Aethelwulf, Ecbert's son, dies from what unusual cause? A bee sting
  • An assassin of which kind is hired to kill Bjorn during his wilderness ordeal? A Berserker

Vikings trivia works because it follows one family across generations, giving fans both Ragnar's rise and his sons' separate storylines to track alongside real Norse history and legend.

A strong Vikings quiz can include Ragnar Lothbrok, Lagertha, Bjorn Ironside, Ivar the Boneless, Floki and Aslaug, the settlement of Kattegat, the raid on Lindisfarne, and the show's blend of historical fact and Norse legend. The theme stays engaging because it spans multiple generations of the same family across the full run of the series.
