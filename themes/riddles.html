Riddles trivia works because it tests how players think rather than what they already know, mixing wordplay, logic, and classic brain teasers that reward a lightbulb moment. That makes it easy to pick up for anyone, with the fun coming from the twist in each answer.

A strong riddles quiz can include classic wordplay riddles, logic puzzles, lateral-thinking questions, and the kind of brain teasers passed around for generations, from 'what has keys but cannot open a lock' to counting and probability puzzles. The category works because each riddle rewards a different kind of thinking, so no two questions feel the same even though none require specialist knowledge.
