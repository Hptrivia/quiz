Sample Questions

  • In The Godfather, for how much does the bandleader finally release Johnny Fontane from his contract? 1,000 dollars
  • In The Godfather Part III, what is the name of Michael's charitable foundation? Vito Andolini Corleone Foundation
  • In what year was The Godfather Part III released? 1990
  • In The Godfather, which song does Johnny Fontane sing at Connie's wedding? I Have but One Heart
  • In The Godfather, what is the name of Jack Woltz's prized racehorse? Khartoum
  • In The Godfather Part III, which opera does Anthony Corleone perform in for his debut? Cavalleria rusticana
  • In The Godfather Part III, which Sicilian song does Anthony sing for his father? Brucia la terra
  • In The Godfather Part II, what stolen goods do young Vito, Clemenza and Tessio sell door to door? Dresses
  • In which year does the story of The Godfather Part III begin? 1979
  • In The Godfather Part II, what phrase does the Black Hand extortionist use for taking his cut? Wet his beak

Godfather trivia works because the films are packed with scenes people quote and reference constantly, from the horse's head to 'leave the gun, take the cannoli.' Questions can cover all three films, the Corleone family and the cast and crew behind them.

A strong Godfather quiz can include Vito Corleone and the oranges, Michael and the restaurant shooting of Sollozzo and McCluskey, Sonny at the toll booth, Luca Brasi and the dead fish, the baptism massacre, Tom Hagen the consigliere, young Vito at Ellis Island and the killing of Don Fanucci, Genco olive oil, Hyman Roth and Cuba, Fredo and the New Year's Eve kiss, Frank Pentangeli, Vincent Mancini, Anthony's opera debut in Palermo and the Immobiliare deal. It works because the trilogy rewards fans who know every family betrayal.
