Love Is Blind trivia works because each season moves to a new city with a new set of couples, and the path from pod proposal to wedding day produces unforgettable moments. Questions can cover the couples, the retreats and the answers given at the altar.

A strong Love Is Blind quiz can include hosts Nick and Vanessa Lachey, the glowing pods and ten days of blind dating, the post-pod retreats in Mexico, Malibu and beyond, the seasons set in Atlanta, Chicago, Dallas, Seattle, Houston, Charlotte, Washington D.C., Minneapolis, Denver and Ohio, Lauren and Cameron, Jessica and Mark and the wine-drinking dog, Giannina running into the woods, Shake and Deepti, Zack and Bliss, the orange argument, and the reunions where every secret comes out. It works because fans remember who said yes, who said no and who never made it down the aisle.
