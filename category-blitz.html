Category Blitz

Spin a letter, then race the clock

Spin the wheel for a letter, then type a Name, Animal, Place, Thing, and Food that starts with it — all before the clock runs out. Daily Blitz gives you 45 seconds; Solo and Versus let you pick Easy (60s), Medium (45s), or Hard (30s).

  • Name — any person's first name
  • Animal — any animal, including pets and breeds
  • Place — any city, country, region, or landmark
  • Thing — any everyday object
  • Food — any fruit, vegetable, dish, snack, or drink

Also check out Daily Blitz — one free letter a day.

Solo

Versus

Pass-and-play with a friend

Category Blitz is a fast-paced name place animal thing style word game — the same idea behind classic category games like Scattergories, reimagined with a spinning letter wheel and a countdown timer. Play solo to sharpen your recall speed, or pass the phone to a friend for local, no-signup versus rounds — no accounts, no waiting on anyone else's turn.

Every round works the same way: spin for a letter, then race to name a person's name, an animal, a place, a thing, and a food that all start with it. It's a quick warm-up for trivia night, a classroom icebreaker, or just something to play in a car ride — no board, no pen and paper, just your phone.
