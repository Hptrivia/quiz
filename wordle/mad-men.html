Mad Men Wordle

Mad Men captivated audiences with its unflinching portrait of 1960s Manhattan advertising and the morally complicated men and women navigating its corridors. The show's brilliance lies in its restraint—pivotal moments emerge through glances and silences rather than dramatic outbursts, revealing the ambitions, insecurities, and desires lurking beneath carefully maintained facades. From Don Draper's mysterious past to the office's shifting power dynamics, Mad Men rewards viewers who notice both the grand cultural upheavals and intimate character details that define the era.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Mad Men from familiar names to more specific terms.
