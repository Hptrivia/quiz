Once Upon a Time Wordle

Once Upon a Time captivated audiences by blending classic fairy tales with modern drama, transporting beloved storybook characters into a contemporary Maine town where magic and curses collide with ordinary life. The show's enduring appeal lies in its clever reimagining of iconic figures—from the Evil Queen ruling as a small-town mayor to a reformed villain seeking redemption—creating intricate layers of mystery and emotional depth. Fans have spent years uncovering the complex connections between characters' magical pasts and their present-day identities, making it perfect thematic material for testing your knowledge.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Once Upon a Time from familiar names to more specific terms.
