Borderlands Wordle

Borderlands stands out in gaming for blending explosive action with irreverent humor and absurdist storytelling across its sprawling universe. From the desert wastelands of Pandora to the techno-nightmares of later entries, the series captivates players through its rogues' gallery of charismatic characters, procedurally-generated arsenals, and vault-hunting adventures that balance mayhem with memorable moments. Whether you're chasing legendary loot or uncovering the twisted plots of Handsome Jack and the Calypso Twins, Borderlands has built a devoted fanbase hungry to test their knowledge of its ever-expanding mythology.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Borderlands from familiar names to more specific terms.
