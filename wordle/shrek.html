Shrek Wordle

Shrek revolutionized animated comedy by subverting fairy tale tropes with irreverent humor and heart, creating a cultural phenomenon that spawned multiple sequels and a devoted fanbase. The film's genius lies in its layered jokes—from shrewd pop culture references to absurdist character moments—that appeal equally to children and adults, making it endlessly quotable and rewatchable decades after its release. Whether exploring the swamp's unlikely inhabitants, the autocratic Lord Farquaad's twisted kingdom, or the touching bond between a grumpy ogre and his talkative donkey companion, Shrek offers rich material for fans to test their knowledge.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Shrek from familiar names to more specific terms.
