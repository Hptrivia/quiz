House of Cards Wordle

House of Cards captivated audiences as a dark political drama that peeled back the curtain on Washington corruption, following Frank Underwood's calculated ascent from congressional operative to the nation's highest office. The show's cultural impact stemmed from its unflinching portrayal of ambition and betrayal across six seasons, complete with Frank's iconic fourth-wall breaks that turned viewers into confidants of his machinations. Its expansive cast of politicians, staffers, and media figures created an intricate web of alliances and vendettas that defined modern prestige television.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through House of Cards from familiar names to more specific terms.
