Sherlock Wordle

BBC's Sherlock brilliantly transplants Arthur Conan Doyle's detective into the 21st century, where Sherlock Holmes and John Watson navigate modern London's criminal underworld with rapid-fire deductions and psychological cat-and-mouse games. The series captivated audiences with its intricate plotting, memorable characters ranging from Scotland Yard's Lestrade to the enigmatic Moriarty, and mind-bending twists that unfold across its four seasons. Whether you've memorized every case or caught the subtle clues scattered throughout, this collection celebrates the show's clever mysteries and unforgettable moments.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Sherlock from familiar names to more specific terms.
