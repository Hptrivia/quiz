Physics Wordle

Physics is the fundamental science that explains how the universe operates, from the motion of falling objects to the behavior of distant stars and subatomic particles. Its principles shape everyday phenomena we experience—from why objects fall to how electricity powers our homes—making it both intellectually captivating and deeply practical. This theme draws on centuries of discovery, offering everything from Newton's groundbreaking laws to cutting-edge modern theories that continue to reshape our understanding of reality.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Physics from familiar names to more specific terms.
