The Americans Wordle

The Americans captivated audiences with its tense portrayal of two Soviet KGB officers maintaining an elaborate cover as an unremarkable American family in 1980s Washington, D.C. The FX series became a critical darling by masterfully weaving espionage tradecraft with deeply personal family drama, forcing its protagonists to navigate constant moral compromises between their secret allegiances and genuine emotional bonds. Six seasons of intricate spy operations, elaborate disguises, and escalating stakes created a rich tapestry that kept viewers obsessed with every detail.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through The Americans from familiar names to more specific terms.
