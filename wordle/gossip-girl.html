Gossip Girl Wordle

Gossip Girl captivated audiences with its addictive blend of Manhattan high society intrigue, shocking revelations, and ruthless social warfare among privileged teens. The show's appeal lies in its relentless cycle of betrayals, carefully constructed lies, and the mysterious anonymous blogger who orchestrated public takedowns with a single post. Its most devoted fans can recall every major scandal, romantic entanglement, and character scheme that defined the Upper East Side's cutthroat hierarchy.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Gossip Girl from familiar names to more specific terms.
