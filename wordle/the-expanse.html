The Expanse Wordle

The Expanse stands as one of science fiction's most ambitious series, blending hard sci-fi worldbuilding with deeply personal character drama across six seasons. The show's sprawling narrative spans humanity's expansion into the solar system, where political intrigue between Earth, Mars, and the Belt's scattered stations creates constant tension and conflict. What makes The Expanse resonate with devoted fans is its commitment to detail—from the linguistic authenticity of Belter Creole to the intricate mechanics of interplanetary politics and the mysterious threat of the protomolecule.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through The Expanse from familiar names to more specific terms.
