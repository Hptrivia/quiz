Obsession Wordle

Obsession is Curry Barker's feature directorial debut, a horror film that builds its dread through intimate character moments before unleashing supernatural chaos. The story centers on Bear and Nikki, friends whose lives spiral into darkness after a fateful encounter with the One Wish Willow—a cursed artifact that grants desires with devastating consequences. What makes this film compelling is how Barker weaves setup throughout everyday scenes at the music store and local pub, rewarding viewers who catch the ominous details lurking beneath the surface friendships.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Obsession from familiar names to more specific terms.
