Final Fantasy Wordle

Final Fantasy stands as one of gaming's most enduring franchises, captivating players for over three decades with its blend of epic storytelling, memorable characters, and innovative gameplay. From Cloud's iconic struggle against Sephiroth to the emotional journey of Tidus and Yuna, each installment has left an indelible mark on the industry while weaving together timeless elements like magical summons, crystal-based worlds, and diverse job systems that unite the series across generations.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Final Fantasy from familiar names to more specific terms.
