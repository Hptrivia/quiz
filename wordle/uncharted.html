Uncharted Wordle

Uncharted stands as one of gaming's most cinematic franchises, following Nathan Drake through thrilling expeditions in search of historical treasures and mythical civilizations. Known for blending explosive action sequences with intricate environmental puzzles, the series captivates players with its richly detailed exotic settings and morally complex cast of allies and adversaries. Whether scaling ancient ruins or unraveling the secrets behind legendary artifacts, the Uncharted saga delivers the adventure and intrigue that keeps fans invested across every chapter.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Uncharted from familiar names to more specific terms.
