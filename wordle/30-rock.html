30 Rock Wordle

30 Rock captured audiences with its relentless comedic pace and razor-sharp satire of television production, following the frazzled head writer of a live sketch show as she navigated eccentric cast members, ruthless network executives, and her own romantic disasters. The series became a cultural phenomenon by cramming multiple jokes into every scene—from obscure references to elaborate running gags—creating a show that rewarded devoted viewers with layers of humor both obvious and hidden. Its stellar ensemble cast and willingness to embrace the absurd made it an enduring favorite among comedy fans.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through 30 Rock from familiar names to more specific terms.
