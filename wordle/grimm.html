Grimm Wordle

The Grimm Wordle pulls words from across the series and challenges you to guess each one a letter at a time.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Grimm from familiar names to more specific terms.
