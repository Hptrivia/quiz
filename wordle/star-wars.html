Star Wars Wordle

Star Wars stands as one of cinema's most influential franchises, captivating audiences for nearly five decades with its epic tale of galactic conflict between light and dark forces. The saga's enduring appeal lies in its unforgettable heroes and villains, iconic imagery, and the timeless struggle between the Jedi and Sith that resonates across multiple generations of devoted fans worldwide.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Star Wars from familiar names to more specific terms.
