Squid Game Wordle

Squid Game captivated global audiences with its high-stakes narrative where indebted players compete in twisted versions of childhood games for a massive cash prize. The Netflix phenomenon combines visceral survival horror with sharp social commentary, exploring themes of desperation, class inequality, and human morality through a meticulously designed competition overseen by mysterious masked enforcers. Its haunting imagery, complex character dynamics, and shocking twists have made it a cultural touchstone that resonates far beyond typical thriller fare.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Squid Game from familiar names to more specific terms.
