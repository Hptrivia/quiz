Control Wordle

Control stands out as Remedy Entertainment's supernatural action shooter that blends visceral combat with mind-bending environmental storytelling. Players navigate the Federal Bureau of Control's labyrinthine headquarters—a brutalist structure that constantly shifts and transforms—while uncovering mysteries about Jesse Faden's past and battling an otherworldly force known as the Hiss. The game's cult following stems from its seamless fusion of compelling narrative, innovative gameplay mechanics, and unsettling atmosphere that rewards exploration and attention to detail.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Control from familiar names to more specific terms.
