Seinfeld Wordle

Seinfeld redefined television comedy by finding humor in the mundane—a show famously described as being about nothing, yet somehow about everything. For nine seasons, it captured the neuroses and social anxieties of four New Yorkers navigating relationships, jobs, and the unwritten rules of everyday life with brilliant precision. The show's legacy rests on its ability to turn minor annoyances into comedic gold, making it endlessly quotable and endlessly memorable.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Seinfeld from familiar names to more specific terms.
