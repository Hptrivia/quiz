Star Wars Jedi Wordle

Star Wars Jedi immerses players in an epic saga of survival and redemption, following Cal Kestis as he evades Imperial forces while rebuilding the Jedi Order across two thrilling installments. Fallen Order and Survivor showcase intense lightsaber combat, puzzle-solving exploration, and a rich cast of allies including the beloved droid BD-1, all set against gorgeously realized alien worlds. The games have captivated millions with their blend of action, storytelling, and deep Star Wars lore.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Star Wars Jedi from familiar names to more specific terms.
