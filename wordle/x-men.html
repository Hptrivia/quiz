X-Men Wordle

The X-Men film series brought mutant superheroes into the mainstream, captivating audiences with complex characters wrestling with prejudice, identity, and extraordinary abilities. From the gritty original trilogy to the time-bending prequels and spinoffs, the franchise has introduced iconic figures like Wolverine, Professor X, and Magneto across multiple timelines and decades. Its enduring appeal stems from richly developed storylines, memorable villains, and a sprawling cast that continues to evolve, making it perfect for exploring deep cuts beyond the headlines.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through X-Men from familiar names to more specific terms.
