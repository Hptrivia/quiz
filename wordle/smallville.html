Smallville Wordle

Smallville reimagined Superman's origin story by following Clark Kent through his formative years in rural Kansas, exploring how a teenage alien grappled with extraordinary abilities while navigating typical coming-of-age challenges. The show's ten-season run became a cultural phenomenon for its blend of superhero mythology with intimate character relationships and serialized drama, transforming a well-known legend into something deeply personal and surprisingly grounded.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Smallville from familiar names to more specific terms.
