Doctor Who Wordle

Doctor Who stands as one of science fiction's most enduring and beloved franchises, captivating audiences since 1963 with its tale of a time-travelling alien and his ever-changing companions. The show's genius lies in its ability to reinvent itself through the Doctor's regenerations, allowing new actors to breathe fresh life into the character while maintaining decades of accumulated mythology. From the Daleks to the Weeping Angels, from the British call box TARDIS to the sonic screwdriver, Doctor Who has created an expansive universe that rewards both casual viewers and devoted fans who've followed the Time Lord across generations.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Doctor Who from familiar names to more specific terms.
