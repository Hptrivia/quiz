White Collar Wordle

White Collar captivated audiences with its charismatic protagonist Neal Caffrey, a sophisticated forger and con artist who strikes an unconventional deal with FBI Agent Peter Burke. The series brilliantly balances stylish heists and intricate schemes with genuine character development, exploring the complicated dynamic between law enforcement and the criminal underworld. Its blend of clever plotting, witty banter, and moral ambiguity made it a standout in the crime-drama genre.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through White Collar from familiar names to more specific terms.
