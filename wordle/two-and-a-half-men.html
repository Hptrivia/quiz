Two and a Half Men Wordle

Two and a Half Men dominated late-night television for over a decade with its irreverent humor centered on three men sharing a luxurious Malibu home. The show became a cultural phenomenon thanks to its eccentric cast of characters, quotable one-liners, and shocking behind-the-scenes upheaval that kept audiences talking season after season.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Two and a Half Men from familiar names to more specific terms.
