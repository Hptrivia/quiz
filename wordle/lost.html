Lost Wordle

Lost captivated millions with its blend of survival drama, supernatural mystery, and intricate character development across six seasons. The show's genius lay in weaving together a sprawling ensemble cast, their interconnected pasts, the island's impossible phenomena, and constantly shifting power dynamics that kept audiences theorizing and debating. With its combination of intimate character moments and mind-bending mythology, Lost created an endlessly rich universe of trivia possibilities.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Lost from familiar names to more specific terms.
