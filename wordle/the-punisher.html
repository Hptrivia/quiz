The Punisher Wordle

The Punisher stands out as a gritty, intense entry in the Marvel television universe, following Frank Castle's brutal quest for vengeance against those responsible for his family's death. The series earned critical acclaim for its unflinching portrayal of vigilante justice, complex moral ambiguity, and a cast of compelling characters caught in Frank's destructive path. With its focus on espionage, corruption, and personal trauma rather than superhero spectacle, the show developed a dedicated fanbase drawn to its darker, more grounded storytelling.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through The Punisher from familiar names to more specific terms.
