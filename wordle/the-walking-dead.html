The Walking Dead Wordle

The Walking Dead transformed zombie fiction by grounding survival horror in the collapse of human civilization and the impossible choices it demands. Its decade-plus run captivated audiences through unflinching character development, shocking deaths that permanently alter the narrative, and escalating conflicts between rival groups struggling for power and resources. The show's power lies in how it weaves intimate personal moments with large-scale threats, forcing survivors to confront what they're willing to do—and who they're willing to sacrifice—in order to endure.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through The Walking Dead from familiar names to more specific terms.
