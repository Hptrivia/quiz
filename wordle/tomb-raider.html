Tomb Raider Wordle

Tomb Raider stands as one of gaming's most iconic action-adventure franchises, defined by archaeologist Lara Croft's daring expeditions into ancient ruins and perilous landscapes. The modern reboot trilogy reimagined the series with survival-focused gameplay and interconnected narratives spanning mysterious civilizations, from the storm-ravaged island of Yamatai to the lost city of Kitezh. Its enduring appeal lies in the blend of exploration, combat, and environmental storytelling that keeps players returning to uncover every secret and artifact.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Tomb Raider from familiar names to more specific terms.
