The Mandalorian Wordle

The Mandalorian brought a gritty, frontier spirit to the Star Wars universe, following an armored bounty hunter navigating dangerous missions across remote worlds. The series captivated audiences through its compelling relationship between the stoic Din Djarin and the enigmatic child Grogu, anchoring an epic tale of redemption and belonging. With its blend of episodic adventures, layered mythology, and exploration of Mandalorian culture and honor codes, the show has become a cornerstone of modern Star Wars storytelling.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through The Mandalorian from familiar names to more specific terms.
