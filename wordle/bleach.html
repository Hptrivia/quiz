Bleach Wordle

Bleach stands as one of anime's most iconic supernatural action series, following Ichigo Kurosaki as he discovers his ability to see spirits and joins the Soul Reapers in their battle against Hollows. The show captivated audiences worldwide with its distinctive art style, memorable characters, and an intricate combat system centered around transformative Zanpakuto weapons and powerful Bankai releases. From the shadowy streets of Karakura Town to the grand halls of Soul Society and the desolate wastelands of Hueco Mundo, Bleach's expanding universe introduced fans to unforgettable factions, formidable adversaries, and some of shonen anime's most visually striking battles.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Bleach from familiar names to more specific terms.
