Pretty Little Liars Wordle

Pretty Little Liars captivated audiences with its intricate web of deception and suspense, following four friends navigating the dark secrets of their small Pennsylvania town while being stalked by an anonymous tormentor. The show became a cultural phenomenon for its shocking revelations, complex character development, and the iconic mystery of who was really pulling the strings behind the scenes. With unexpected plot twists and hidden identities lurking around every corner, this teen thriller keeps fans endlessly fascinated by its layered storytelling.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Pretty Little Liars from familiar names to more specific terms.
