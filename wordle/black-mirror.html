Black Mirror Wordle

Black Mirror stands out as one of television's most provocative explorations of how technology reshapes human behavior and society. Each self-contained episode presents a chilling "what-if" scenario—from disturbing social media dystopias to nightmarish virtual realities—that leaves viewers questioning the price of innovation. The show's reputation rests on its sharp social commentary and jaw-dropping plot twists that redefine what you thought you knew about each story.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Black Mirror from familiar names to more specific terms.
