The Blacklist Wordle

The Blacklist captivated audiences with its blend of procedural crime-solving and deep mythology, centering on the unlikely alliance between FBI profiler Elizabeth Keen and the enigmatic criminal informant Raymond Reddington. The show's enduring appeal stems from its dual narrative structure: standalone cases featuring colorful international fugitives paired with an overarching conspiracy about Reddington's shadowy past and true motivations. Eight seasons of intricate plotting and character development created a devoted fanbase invested in both the weekly criminals and the show's central secrets.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through The Blacklist from familiar names to more specific terms.
