Vietnam Wordle

Vietnam is a Southeast Asian nation with a rich tapestry spanning ancient dynasties, colonial influences, and a pivotal 20th-century conflict that shaped global politics. Known for its stunning landscapes—from the limestone karsts of Halong Bay to the lush Mekong Delta—Vietnam has become equally celebrated for its aromatic cuisine, thriving coffee industry, and vibrant contemporary culture. Its story weaves together war history, artistic traditions, philosophical movements, and the resilience of a people who've built a dynamic modern society.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Vietnam from familiar names to more specific terms.
