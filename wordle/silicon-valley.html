Silicon Valley Wordle

Silicon Valley brilliantly skewers the world of tech startups through the struggles of Richard Hendricks and his compression algorithm company Pied Piper. The show's razor-sharp humor exposes the absurdities of venture capital, ego-driven billionaires, and the relentless pressure to innovate in a cutthroat industry. With its witty ensemble cast and biting commentary on startup culture, Silicon Valley has become essential viewing for anyone fascinated by tech world drama.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Silicon Valley from familiar names to more specific terms.
