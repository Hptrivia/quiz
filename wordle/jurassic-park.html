Jurassic Park Wordle

Jurassic Park redefined blockbuster filmmaking when it arrived in 1993, blending cutting-edge visual effects with a gripping narrative about ambition gone awry on a remote island. The film's legacy rests on its unforgettable dinosaur encounters, morally conflicted protagonists, and the cautionary tale of recreating extinct life through genetic engineering. From the thundering T-Rex to the cunning Velociraptors, its moments of terror and wonder continue to captivate audiences across generations.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Jurassic Park from familiar names to more specific terms.
