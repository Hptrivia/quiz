Bones Wordle

Bones captivated audiences for twelve seasons by merging gripping forensic mysteries with compelling character dynamics, following brilliant anthropologist Dr. Temperance Brennan and charming FBI agent Seeley Booth as they uncover killers through skeletal evidence and investigative partnership. The show's enduring appeal stems from its unique combination of scientific detail, procedural intrigue, and the evolving romantic tension between its two leads, making it a rich source for fans who cherish both the cases and the relationships that define the series.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Bones from familiar names to more specific terms.
