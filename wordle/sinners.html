Sinners Wordle

Ryan Coogler's Sinners is a vampire horror film set during a single blood-soaked night in 1930s Mississippi, where twin brothers Smoke and Stack defend their juke joint from supernatural threats. The film weaves historical detail, character depth, and atmospheric dread throughout its runtime, creating rich material for those who've absorbed its intricate story and visual storytelling.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Sinners from familiar names to more specific terms.
