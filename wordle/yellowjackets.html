Yellowjackets Wordle

Yellowjackets is a psychological thriller that captivates audiences by weaving together two parallel narratives: a harrowing account of a high school soccer team's fight for survival after their plane crashes in an isolated wilderness, and the fractured lives of those same women decades later as they grapple with the trauma and secrets they've buried. The show has become a cultural phenomenon for its masterful storytelling, complex characters, and the mounting sense of dread surrounding what truly transpired during that fateful winter in the woods. Fans are drawn to unraveling its interconnected mysteries across both timelines while piecing together the truth behind the survivors' guilt and paranoia.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Yellowjackets from familiar names to more specific terms.
