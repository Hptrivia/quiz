Sex and the City Wordle

Sex and the City revolutionized television by following four unforgettable women navigating love, ambition, and friendship in Manhattan. The HBO series became a cultural phenomenon for its witty exploration of modern romance, candid discussions about desire, and glamorous yet relatable portrayal of single life in the city. From Carrie's column to Samantha's conquests to Charlotte's quest for the perfect match, the show captured the messy reality of relationships with humor and heart.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Sex and the City from familiar names to more specific terms.
