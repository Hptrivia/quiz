Mr. Robot Wordle

Mr. Robot captivated audiences with its gritty exploration of a brilliant but fractured hacker navigating the underground world of corporate sabotage. The show earned critical acclaim for weaving together cutting-edge cybersecurity concepts with psychological complexity, as protagonist Elliot Alderson's unstable perspective constantly challenges viewers to question what's real. Its intricate plotting, morally ambiguous characters, and shocking revelations made it a phenomenon for fans who thrive on rewatching and uncovering hidden details.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Mr. Robot from familiar names to more specific terms.
