Mass Effect Wordle

Mass Effect stands as one of gaming's most beloved sci-fi epics, renowned for its sweeping narrative across three interconnected chapters and the player choices that ripple throughout the entire experience. The series captivated millions with its richly developed cast of alien companions, morally complex decisions, and the escalating threat of the Reapers—ancient synthetic destroyers that force Commander Shepard to unite a fractured galaxy against annihilation. Fans remain passionately invested in every detail, from the politics of the Citadel Council to the fates of beloved crew members, making it a perfect subject for deep lore exploration.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Mass Effect from familiar names to more specific terms.
