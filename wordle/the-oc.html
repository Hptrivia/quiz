The O.C. Wordle

The O.C. defined early 2000s teen drama with its blend of wealthy Newport Beach intrigue, emotional character arcs, and an indie soundtrack that became as iconic as the show itself. Across four seasons, the series captured the messy lives of the Cohen family and their circle—from Ryan's outsider journey to the romantic entanglements and conflicts that kept fans obsessed. With memorable moments, quotable lines, and a devoted fanbase that never stopped caring, The O.C. remains a cultural touchstone for a generation.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through The O.C. from familiar names to more specific terms.
