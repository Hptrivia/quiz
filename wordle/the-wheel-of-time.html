The Wheel of Time Wordle

The Wheel of Time stands as one of fantasy's most ambitious television adaptations, bringing Robert Jordan's sprawling saga to screen with stunning world-building and complex character arcs. The series captivates audiences through its central mystery of the Dragon Reborn, intricate magic systems, and richly developed factions like the powerful Aes Sedai. From the humble origins of the Two Rivers crew to the cosmic battles that shape their destiny, the show weaves together prophecy, politics, and personal transformation across its expanding universe.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through The Wheel of Time from familiar names to more specific terms.
