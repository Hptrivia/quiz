House of the Dragon Wordle

House of the Dragon, HBO's prequel to Game of Thrones, chronicles the golden age and ultimate collapse of House Targaryen through intimate family conflict and dragon-fueled ambition. The series captivates audiences with its exploration of succession crises, rival factions known as the Greens and Blacks, and the catastrophic civil war sparked by competing claims to the Iron Throne. Its richly detailed world of royal intrigue, dragon lore, and Targaryen legacy offers endless material for fans testing their knowledge of the dynasty's most pivotal figures and moments.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through House of the Dragon from familiar names to more specific terms.
