Frasier Wordle

Frasier stands as one of television's most celebrated comedies, following the refined psychiatrist Dr. Frasier Crane as he navigates life hosting a Seattle radio advice show while managing his eccentric family and colorful coworkers. The series became legendary for its sharp, rapid-fire humor and surprisingly heartfelt character development, creating an ensemble cast whose interactions—from Martin's no-nonsense wisdom to the romantic tension between Niles and Daphne—gave audiences eleven seasons of both laugh-out-loud moments and genuine emotional depth.

Guess each word one letter at a time: green tiles mean the letter is in the right position, yellow means it appears somewhere else in the word, and grey means it is not in the word at all. Each page covers a new word, working through Frasier from familiar names to more specific terms.
